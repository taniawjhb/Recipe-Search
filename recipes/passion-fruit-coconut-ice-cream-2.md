## Ingredients

- 1 cup passionfruit puree
- 1 can (400 millilitres) coconut milk
- 1 can (400 grams) condensed milk
- 1 cup heavy cream
- 1/4 cup milk powder

## Method

1. Blend all the ingredients together in a blender till well combined.
2. Transfer to an ice cream maker and freeze according to the manufacturer's instructions.
3. To make this ice cream without an ice cream maker, after you have blended all the ingredients, transfer it into the container that you plan to freeze the ice cream in (or leave the blended ice cream in the blender if you have enough freezer space). After 45 minutes to 1 hour, check on it and if you see the mixture freezing at the edges, remove from the freezer and give it a whizz. Repeat the process 3-4 times and then transfer to the container you want the ice cream to freeze in and let it freeze completely.
