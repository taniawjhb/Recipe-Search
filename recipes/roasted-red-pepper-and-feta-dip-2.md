## Ingredients

- 4 red bell peppers
- 1/4 cup extra-virgin olive oil
- 2 tablespoons red wine vinegar
- 2 garlic cloves, peeled
- 2 cups crumbled feta cheese

## Method

1. Char peppers directly over gas flame or in broiler until blackened on all sides. Enclose in a paper bag 10 minutes. Peel, seed, and quarter peppers.
2. Puree peppers, oil, garlic, red wine vinegar and cheese in a processor. Season with salt and pepper. Chill at least 3 hours or overnight.
