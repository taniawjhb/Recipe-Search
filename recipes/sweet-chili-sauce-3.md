## Ingredients

- 1 cup water
- 1 cup rice vinegar
- 1 cup sugar
- 2 teaspoons fresh ginger root, finely grated
- 1 teaspoon garlic, finely grated
- 2 teaspoons hot chile, minced
- 2 teaspoons tomato sauce
- 2 teaspoons cornstarch

## Method

1. Pour water and vinegar into a saucepan, and bring to a boil over high heat.
2. Stir in sugar, ginger, garlic, chile pepper, and ketchup; simmer for 5 minutes.
3. Stir in cornstarch. Remove saucepan from stove to cool.
4. Then transfer to a bowl, cover, and refrigerate until needed.
