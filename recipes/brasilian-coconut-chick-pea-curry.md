## Ingredients

- 1.5 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 red capsicum/bell pepper, cut into 3 x 0.75 centimetres strips
- 2 cans chickpeas, drained
- 400 millilitres coconut milk, full fat for best flavour
- 400 millilitres can crushed tomato
- 1 cup (250 millilitres) chicken or vegetable stock/broth, low sodium
- 1 tablespoon paprika
- 1 tablespoon cumin
- 1/2 to 1 teaspoon cayenne pepper (adjust spiciness to taste)
- 1.5 teaspoons sugar
- 0.5 teaspoon salt
- 50 grams baby spinach leaves (or kale)
- 3 tablespoons coriander/cilantro, roughly chopped, plus more for serving
- 1 tablespoon lime juice, plus extra wedges for serving

## Method

1. Heat the oil in a pot or large deep skillet over high heat. Add the garlic and onion, cook for 1 minute.
2. Add the capsicum, cook for 2 minutes until the onion is translucent and slightly golden on the edges.
3. Add the remaining curry ingredients except the baby spinach. Stir, bring to a simmer then lower to medium-low so it's simmering gently.
4. Simmer 12-15 minutes until it changes from pale pink to an orangey red colour.
5. Stir in the spinach until just wilted, then stir in the coriander and lime juice. Add more salt if needed.
6. Serve over rice with a dollop of yogurt, an extra sprinkle of coriander and a squeeze of lime.
