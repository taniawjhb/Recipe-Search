## Ingredients

- 400 grams sliced pork steak
- 2 tablespoons Panaeng Curry Paste
- 8 lime leaves, shredded
- 1 red chilli
- 3 baby marrow, sliced 2 centimetres
- 3 patty pan, quartered
- A handful of broccoli florets
- 400 millilitres coconut milk
- 200 millilitres coconut cream
- 1/2 cup shredded basil
- 1 tablespoon fish sauce
- 1 teaspoon peanut butter
- 1 teaspoon sugar
- Coconut/sunflower oil for frying
- 1 tablespoon roasted, chopped peanuts for serving

## Method

1. In a medium saucepan, on medium-high heat, start by adding your oil and half the curry paste and cook until aromatic.
2. Brown your pork in the curry paste, adding salt to taste. Remove the pork once browned and set aside.
3. In the same saucepan, add coconut milk, coconut cream and bring to a simmer, making sure to stir and deglaze the bottom.
4. Once simmering, add the rest of the curry paste, vegetables (keep the basil and chilli till the end), fish sauce, peanut butter, and sugar.
5. When the vegetables are cooked halfway (around 6 minutes), add your pork back into the curry and simmer for another 5 minutes or until pork is cooked through.
6. Stir through the chopped basil and red chilli. Serve with rice.
