## Ingredients

- 2 loaves pita bread
- extra virgin olive oil
- 1/2 teaspoon sumac, more for later
- salt and pepper
- 1 heart of romaine lettuce, chopped
- 1 English cucumber, chopped
- 5 Roma tomatoes, chopped
- 5 green onions (both white and green parts), chopped
- 5 radishes, stems removed, thinly sliced
- 2 cups chopped fresh parsley leaves, stems removed
- 1 cup chopped fresh mint leaves (optional)

### For the lime vinaigrette

- 1 1/2 limes, juice of
- 1/3 cup extra virgin olive oil
- salt and pepper
- 1 teaspoon ground sumac
- 1/4 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- scant 1/4 teaspoon ground allspice

## Method

1. Toast the pita bread in your toaster oven until it is crisp but not browned. Heat 3 tablespoons of olive oil in a large pan. Break the pita bread into pieces, and place in the heated oil. Fry briefly until browned, tossing frequently. Add salt, pepper and 1/2 teaspoon of sumac. Remove the pita chips from the heat and place on paper towels to drain.
2. In a large mixing bowl, combine the chopped lettuce, cucumber, tomatoes and green onions with the sliced radish and parsley.
3. To make the lime vinaigrette, whisk together the lime juice, olive oil and spices in a small bowl.
4. Dress the salad with the vinaigrette and toss lightly.
5. Finally, add the pita chips, and more sumac if you like, and toss one more time. Transfer to small serving bowls or plates.
