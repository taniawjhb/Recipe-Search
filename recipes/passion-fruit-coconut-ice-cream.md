## Ingredients

- 1 cup passionfruit puree
- 1 can (400ml) coconut milk
- 1 can (400g) condensed milk
- 1 cup heavy cream
- ¼ cup milk powder

## Method

1. Blend all the ingredients together in a blender till well combined.
2. Transfer to an ice cream maker and freeze according to the manufacturer's instructions.

## Notes

To make this ice cream without an ice cream maker, after you have blended all the ingredients, either transfer it into the container that you plan to freeze the ice cream in, or if you have enough freezer space leave the blended ice cream in the blender. After 45 min - 1 hour check on it and if you see the mixture freezing at the edges, remove from the freezer and give it a whizz. Repeat the process 3-4 times and then transfer to the container you want the ice cream to freeze in and let freeze completely.
