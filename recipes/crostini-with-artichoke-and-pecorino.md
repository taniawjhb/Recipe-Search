## Ingredients

- 140 grams artichoke in olive oil, drained
- pinch of chilli flakes
- small handful mint leaves
- zest 1/2 lemon
- 1 tablespoon olive oil, plus extra for drizzling
- 2 large or 4 small slices of crusty bread
- 1 garlic clove sliced
- 50 grams pecorino thinly sliced (or vegetarian alternative)

## Method

1. Heat oven to 200°C/180°C fan. Halve or quarter the artichokes, if whole, and put them in a bowl with the chilli flakes, mint, zest and oil. Season lightly with salt and set aside.
2. Put the bread on a baking sheet and scatter over the garlic. Drizzle with olive oil and season with sea salt. Bake for 10 minutes, or until golden and crisp. Set aside to cool.
3. Fold the pecorino through the marinated artichokes and spoon over the garlic bruschetta just before eating.
