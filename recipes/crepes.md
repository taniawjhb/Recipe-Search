## Ingredients

- 1 cup / 150 grams plain flour (all purpose flour)
- 1 1/2 cups / 375 millilitres milk (I use low fat)
- 2 eggs
- 3 tablespoons / 40 grams unsalted butter
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 2 tablespoons white sugar
- Pinch of salt

## Method

1. Place batter ingredients in a blender. Pulse 10 times, or until smooth and lump free.
2. Place batter in fridge for 1 hour.
3. Heat a non stick skillet over medium high heat. Add 1/4 teaspoon butter and melt.
4. Add 1/4 cup batter and quickly swirl to spread. Cook the first side for 1 minute or until there are light golden brown patches. Flip and cook the other side for 30 seconds.
5. Slide out of the skillet onto a tray / board. Repeat with remaining crepes, overlapping them on the board.
