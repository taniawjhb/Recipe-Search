## Ingredients

- 300 grams plus 2 tablespoons all-purpose plain flour
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 14 grams dried yeast
- 1 pinch of sugar
- 1 cup warm water
- 3 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 medium onion, finely chopped
- 2 cloves garlic
- 400 grams ground beef or ground lamb, or a mixture
- 1/2 red or yellow bell pepper, or 1 green pointy pepper, finely diced
- 2 medium tomatoes, deseeded and diced
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon
- 1 teaspoon paprika
- 1 teaspoon ground allspice
- 1/2 teaspoon chili powder
- 1 tablespoon tomato paste
- Small bunch flat leaf parsley
- Salt (1 teaspoon recommended) and freshly ground black pepper to taste
- Pinch chili powder
- 1 egg plus 15 millilitres (1 tablespoon) olive oil to brush the pide

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 180°C (350°F). Stir the dried yeast and sugar into a small bowl and pour in 1/2 cup warm water. Dissolve the yeast in the water, mixing with your fingers. Set aside for the yeast mixture to get frothy for 5 minutes.
2. Combine the flour and salt in a large bowl. Make a well in the middle and pour in 2 tablespoons olive oil and the yeast mixture. Pour in the remaining 1/2 cup warm water. Using your hands, draw in the flour from the sides and work the mixture into a dough.
3. Knead for 3-5 minutes, until you reach a soft, smooth dough. The dough gets sticky as you knead, so pour in the remaining 1 tablespoon olive oil and stir in an additional 2 tablespoons flour to help shape into a soft dough.
4. Place the dough in a large bowl and cover with cling film. Leave it in a warm place for 1 hour; it will double in size.
5. Meanwhile, prepare the filling. Heat 15 millilitres (1 tablespoon) olive oil in a wide heavy pan and stir in the onions, garlic and peppers. Sauté for 2-3 minutes over medium heat, until they start to soften. Stir in the tomatoes and sauté for another 2 minutes. Pour in the lemon juice and season with salt and ground black pepper. Turn the heat off.
6. Place the ground meat, spices, tomato paste and fresh herbs in a bowl and combine with the cooked vegetables. Mix well. The topping is ready.
7. Once the dough is risen, place it on a lightly floured surface. Knead for a minute then divide the dough into two pieces and roll into two balls.
8. On a lightly floured surface, roll the dough balls into 2 oval shapes of 20 centimetres by 40 centimetres, with 1/2 centimetre thickness. Line a large baking tray with baking paper and place the 2 oval flatbreads on the tray.
9. Spread the filling evenly over the 2 flatbreads, leaving 2 centimetres at the edges as a border. Fold in the sides to act as a border to keep the filling intact. Squeeze the oval dough at each end to make it pointy.
10. Beat an egg in a small bowl and mix it with 1 tablespoon olive oil. Brush the edges of the dough with this mixture.
11. Bake for 25-30 minutes, until the pides are golden brown and crispy at the edges. Once cool, cut into slices and serve.
