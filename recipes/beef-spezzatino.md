## Ingredients

- 3 tbsp olive oil, or as needed
- 900g chuck steak, cubed
- 1/2 cup cake flour, more if needed
- 100g pancetta, roughly chopped
- 1 large onion, finely diced
- 2 carrots, finely diced
- 2 celery stalks, finely diced
- 2 tsp sea salt
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 1 tsp brown sugar (optional)
- 200g portabellini mushrooms, halved
- 4 garlic cloves, sliced
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 2 tbsp balsamic vinegar
- 1/3 cup (80ml) red wine
- 2 1/2 cups beef stock (adjust if using a pressure cooker)
- 1 bay leaf
- 1 rosemary sprig

## Method

1. If using the oven preheat to 160°C. Season your flour and then add your beef chunks and toss to coat lightly, heat the olive oil in a large casserole and sear the beef over high heat, in batches, until browned - do not burn the flour. Transfer to a bowl and set aside.
2. Add the diced pancetta and cook, stirring, until it starts to crisp up. Transfer to the bowl with beef.
3. Add a splash of red wine to the pan and let it cook down. Reduce the heat, add a little olive oil and the onion, celery and carrots. Season with the salt and pepper and sprinkle with the sugar.
4. Sauté over low heat for 10 minutes, stirring frequently, until the vegetables are very soft. You can add a splash of stock or wine to the pan, as needed, while the soffrito is cooking.
5. Stir in the mushrooms and garlic and cook for a minute or so. Add the wine and balsamic vinegar and stir in the tomato paste.
6. Pour in the beef stock and bring to a simmer (if using a pressure cooker add half the stock). Return the beef and pancetta plus any juices to the pan and stir everything together. Add the rosemary and bay leaf, cover the pot with a tight fitting lid and transfer to the oven for 90 minutes. (For a pressure cooker, cook at high pressure for 40 minutes - allow a natural pressure release.)
7. Check the stew at this point – is the beef very tender? If yes then take out of the oven. If not, continue to cook for a further 30 minutes.
8. If the beef is tender, adjust seasoning and top with some freshly chopped parsley, serve with creamy polenta, mashed potatoes or even pasta.
