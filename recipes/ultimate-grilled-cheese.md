## Ingredients

- 100 grams cream cheese, softened
- 1/4 cup mayonnaise
- 2 cups cheddar cheese, grated
- 2 cups parmesan/gruyere/other, grated
- 1/2 clove garlic finely crushed (optional)
- 1/2 teaspoon seasoning salt
- 1/4 teaspoon cracked black pepper
- 12 slices ciabatta thick sliced
- 1/2 cup butter, softened, more if needed

## Method

1. Blend all ingredients except bread and butter in a medium bowl.
2. Butter one side of each slice of bread.
3. Put sandwiches together using about 1/2 cup of filling each.
4. Place buttered side of bread on the outside of sandwich.
5. Toast on griddle or in skillet until a deep golden brown. Make sure you cook this slowly over low to med-low heat, so that the inside has a chance to melt before the outside gets too done. Kick up the heat when it gets going.
