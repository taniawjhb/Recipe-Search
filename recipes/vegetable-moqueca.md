## Ingredients

- 4 tablespoons olive oil
- 2 white onions, chopped
- 2 yellow peppers, deseeded and thinly sliced
- 2 red peppers, deseeded and thinly sliced
- 8 spring onions, trimmed and finely chopped
- 6 cloves of garlic, finely chopped
- 4 teaspoons sweet paprika
- 1 to 1 1/2 teaspoons chilli powder
- 800 millilitres coconut milk
- 200 millilitres vegetable stock
- 4 large tomatoes, deseeded and chopped
- 4 tablespoons tomato puree
- 1 large aubergine, cut into 2.5 centimetres chunks
- 6 small courgettes, cut into 1.5 centimetres chunks
- Juice of 2 limes
- Handful of fresh coriander
- Sea salt and black pepper

## Method

1. Heat the oil in a frying pan over medium-high heat, add the onions and peppers and fry till softened (4-6 minutes).
2. Add the spring onion, garlic, paprika and chilli powder and cook for another minute.
3. Add the coconut milk, vegetable stock, chopped tomato and tomato puree, bring to a simmer and cook uncovered for 5 minutes. Season with salt and pepper.
4. Add the aubergines and cook for 2 minutes before adding the courgettes — simmer for another 4-5 minutes until just tender.
5. Add the lime juice then taste and adjust the seasoning. Remove from heat and add coriander. Serve with coconut rice.
