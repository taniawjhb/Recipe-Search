## Ingredients

- 5 cups whole milk (for the rice)
- 2 cups whole milk (for the slurry)
- 1/4 cup rice
- 1 cup sugar
- 3 tablespoons rice flour
- 1 egg yolk

## Method

1. Pour 5 cups of whole milk and the rice into a saucepan.
2. Set the heat to low and stir frequently until the rice becomes tender and absorbs some of the milk.
3. Once the rice is soft, mix in the sugar and stir until fully dissolved.
4. In a separate bowl, whisk 1 cup of milk with the rice flour until smooth and lump-free.
5. Gradually add the mixture to the pot, whisking continuously to ensure a velvety consistency.
6. Continue stirring over low heat until the pudding thickens to a creamy texture.
7. Remove from the heat and gently incorporate the egg yolk, whisking swiftly to blend.
8. Divide the pudding among oven-safe ramekins, ensuring even portions.
9. Arrange the ramekins in a deep baking dish and fill with water to create a gentle water bath.
10. Place the baking dish on the upper rack of a preheated oven at 210°C.
11. Bake until the tops turn beautifully golden-brown, forming a delicate caramelized layer.
