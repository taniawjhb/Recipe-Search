## Ingredients

- 500g fresh spinach
- 8 tbsp olive oil
- 1-2 tbsp white wine vinegar
- 2 cloves garlic, finely chopped
- Half cup milk
- 1 tbsp flour
- Salt to taste
- Pinch nutmeg

## Method

1. In a large pot, add a splash of water and bring to the boil. Add spinach and wilt. Drain and squeeze out excess water, then chop fine.
2. Heat olive oil on medium heat, then add chopped garlic, stirring for 1-2 minutes.
3. Add spinach to garlic and stir well, then add vinegar, salt and nutmeg and cook on low heat for 10 minutes.
4. Add a few drops of milk to the flour until it becomes a paste. Add balance of milk to the paste, then stir through the spinach. Add a little more milk if needed. Increase heat and cook for a further 5 minutes until mixture becomes creamy.
