## Ingredients

- 1 head cabbage
- 2 tablespoons olive oil, or as needed
- 1 small onion, thinly sliced
- 1/2 chopped green bell pepper
- 1 green onion, sliced
- 2 sprigs fresh thyme
- 1 whole Scotch bonnet chile pepper
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 cup shredded carrots
- 1/4 cup white vinegar
- 2 tablespoons white sugar

## Method

1. Peel tough outer leaves from the cabbage, core the head, and shred the cabbage.
2. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium high heat; raise heat to high. Stir the onion, green bell pepper, and green onion in the hot oil and cook until the vegetables have softened, about 5 minutes, stirring often. Stir the thyme, Scotch bonnet pepper, and salt into the onion mixture.
3. Mix the shredded cabbage and carrots into the onion mixture until thoroughly combined, cover the skillet, and reduce heat to medium-low. Cook until the cabbage has started to soften, about 10 minutes, stirring occasionally.
4. Uncover the skillet and stir the vinegar and sugar into the cabbage mixture; cook and stir until the cabbage is tender, about 3 more minutes. Discard the Scotch bonnet pepper and thyme sprigs before serving.
