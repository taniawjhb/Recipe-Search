## Ingredients

- Butter/iceberg lettuce
- 2 carrots, julienned
- 2 tomatoes, thinly sliced
- Olives
- White onion rings
- 2 tbsp spirit vinegar
- 5 tbsp olive oil

## Method

1. Tear the lettuce roughly and add to a bowl.
2. Cut the carrots into fine julienne strips and add to the lettuce.
3. Slice the tomatoes, remove the pits from the olives, and add to the bowl.
4. In a separate bowl, whisk together the olive oil, vinegar and a little salt and pepper.
5. Add the dressing just before serving and toss together. Taste and adjust seasoning.
