## Ingredients

- 1 cup white rice, rinsed
- 1 litre water
- 1 cinnamon quill
- 1 piece lemon rind
- 500 millilitres milk
- 4 egg yolks
- 1/2 cup white/brown sugar
- 1 tablespoon butter, cubed
- Cinnamon powder to decorate

## Method

1. Place water, cinnamon quill and lemon rind in a saucepan and bring to the boil.
2. Once boiling, add the washed rice and boil till rice begins to split open (25-30 minutes). Add a bit of water if necessary towards the end.
3. Once prepared, add 450 millilitres milk, leaving 50 millilitres to whisk with the 4 egg yolks. Once the milk is boiling, reduce to a simmer, and simmer for a further 10 minutes, then VERY SLOWLY stir in the egg mixture, making sure to keep stirring so that eggs don't scramble.
4. Add your sugar on top of the rice mixture, keeping heat low, and let the sugar melt into the rice pudding.
5. Once sugar is nearly dissolved, give it a stir, adding the butter cubes to give it that beautiful glossy finish.
6. Serve into bowls, and finish with powdered cinnamon. Serve hot or cold.
