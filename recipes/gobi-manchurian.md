## Ingredients

- 250 grams cauliflower florets (gobi, cleaned)
- 1/2 cup all-purpose flour (maida, preferably organic or at least unbleached)
- 1/4 cup cornstarch (white corn flour or yellow corn flour)
- 1/2 to 1 teaspoon red chilli powder or paste (less spicy variety) (optional)
- 1/4 teaspoon black pepper, crushed or ground
- 1/4 teaspoon salt (adjust to taste)
- 1/2 cup water (more if needed)
- Oil for deep frying as needed
- 1 1/2 tablespoons oil
- 1 tablespoon garlic, finely chopped
- 1/2 tablespoon ginger, finely chopped
- 1 green chilli, chopped (optional)
- 1/3 cup onions or spring onions, finely chopped
- 1/4 cup bell pepper (capsicum), chopped finely (optional)
- 1 tablespoon soya sauce
- 2 to 3 tablespoons red chilli sauce
- 1 to 2 tablespoons tomato ketchup (optional, use if using hot sauce)
- 1/2 teaspoon red chilli powder
- 1/2 tablespoon rice wine vinegar
- 1 teaspoon sugar (adjust to taste)
- Salt as needed
- 3 to 4 tablespoons water
- 1/2 to 3/4 teaspoon black pepper, crushed or ground
- 1/8 teaspoon salt (adjust to taste)
- 2 tablespoons spring onion greens, chopped, for garnish

## Method

1. Clean 250 grams of gobi and cut the florets to medium size.
2. Add them to 3 cups of hot water and rest for 5 minutes.
3. Later drain the gobi completely in a colander or spread on a clean cloth until no moisture remains. Any moisture in the gobi will cook it soft and not crisp.
4. Heat up the oil for deep frying on a medium heat.
5. Meanwhile make the batter by adding cornstarch, all-purpose flour, red chilli powder, black pepper and salt to a mixing bowl.
6. Mix and add water little by little as needed to make a free-flowing, lump-free batter. The consistency has to be medium and not too thick or very runny. Adjust the salt as needed by tasting the batter.
7. Add the gobi florets to the batter in batches and coat them well.
8. When the oil turns hot enough, check by dropping a small amount of batter into the hot oil — the batter has to rise without browning. This is the correct temperature.
9. Pick up each cauliflower floret and gently drop them one by one into the hot oil. Fry until the florets turn golden and crisp, stirring in between for even frying. Drain on a kitchen tissue or a steel colander. Repeat with the rest.
10. For extra crisp gobi manchurian, you can also refry the once-fried gobi again in oil.
11. For the sauce, mix together red chilli powder with a little water to a paste and keep aside (optional, mainly for the red colour).
12. Heat 1 1/2 tablespoons oil in a wide pan. When hot, add the garlic, ginger and green chillies. Sauté for a minute or two.
13. Then add the spring onions and capsicum. Sauté on a high flame for 2 minutes.
14. Add the soya sauce, red chilli sauce, tomato ketchup, red chilli paste, sugar and vinegar. Mix and add water. Mix and cook, stirring, and add pepper.
15. Cook on a medium heat until the sauce thickens. The sauce has to be slightly sour, sweet and hot. If needed adjust the salt and add more sauces.
16. Allow the sauce to cool for 1 to 2 minutes. Add the fried cauliflower and stir gently to coat well with the manchurian sauce.
17. Garnish with spring onion greens and serve immediately as an appetiser or as a side with noodles or fried rice.
