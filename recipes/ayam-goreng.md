## Ingredients

- 1.25 kilograms / 2.5 pounds chicken thighs and drumsticks, bone in skin on
- 2 teaspoons coriander seeds (toasted spices)
- 2 teaspoons cumin seeds (toasted spices)
- 1 teaspoon fennel seeds (toasted spices)
- 3 garlic cloves, roughly chopped (curry paste)
- 1 tablespoon ginger, roughly chopped (curry paste)
- 1 tablespoon galangal, roughly chopped (curry paste)
- 1 1/2 teaspoons curry powder (any type fine, mild or spicy – your choice) (curry paste)
- 1 lemongrass, white part only, roughly chopped (curry paste)
- 1 teaspoon turmeric powder (curry paste)
- 2 small shallots, peeled and roughly chopped (curry paste)
- 1 teaspoon fine salt (curry paste)
- 1/2 teaspoon chilli powder, adjust to taste (curry paste)
- 2 teaspoons brown sugar (curry paste)
- 7 tablespoons coconut milk (curry paste)
- 1/2 cup cornflour / cornstarch (cooking)
- 1.75 litres oil (cooking)
- 1 tablespoon garlic, minced, not too small else it burns (garnish, optional)
- 1 tablespoon large red chilli (cayenne pepper), deseeded and minced (garnish, optional)
- 1 tablespoon green onion, minced (garnish, optional)
- Pinch of salt (garnish, optional)
- Coriander/cilantro leaves (garnish, optional)

## Method

1. Toast spices: Toast the spices in a small skillet over medium heat (no oil) for 2 minutes or until the spices smell fragrant. Transfer the seeds into a blender, small food processor OR into a tall jug that fits a stick blender.
2. Add the remaining Curry Paste ingredients and blend until smooth. Pour the Curry Paste over the chicken in a ziplock bag or bowl. Toss to coat, then marinate for 24 hours in the fridge, up to 48 hours.
3. Dechill chicken: Remove the chicken from the fridge 30 minutes prior to cooking and transfer into a bowl (most marinade should be stuck to the chicken).
4. Cornflour coating: Add the cornflour to the chicken and toss to coat – it will thicken the paste; this is what makes the craggy coating.
5. Pour oil into a wide, heavy-based pot to a depth of 6 centimetres. Heat over medium-high heat to 180°C – maintain temp as best you can.
6. Carefully place 3 pieces of chicken in; do not touch for 2 minutes (to let the crust adhere). The oil temperature should drop to 150°C – increase heat if needed.
7. Fry for 8 minutes (wings for 5 minutes), or until deep golden brown and the internal temperature at the thickest part is cooked through.
8. Keep warm: Place cooked chicken onto a rack and keep warm in the oven. Cook the remaining chicken.
9. Serve immediately, sprinkled with coriander and garlic-chilli garnish, if using.
10. Garnish: Heat 3 tablespoons oil in a small skillet over medium heat. Add garlic and chilli, cook until the garlic is starting to go light golden. Then add green onion and cook until the garlic is golden. Drain on paper towels, sprinkle with salt. Cool then sprinkle on the chicken.
