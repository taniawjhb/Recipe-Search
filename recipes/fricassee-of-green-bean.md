## Ingredients

- 300 grams green beans
- 2 shallots, finely sliced
- 1/2 clove garlic
- Pinch dry red chilli
- Small knob of butter
- Dash olive oil

## Method

1. Bring a pan of water to the boil, add the green beans and simmer for four minutes. Prepare a bowl of ice cold water.
2. Remove the beans from the heat, drain and briefly place into the cold water. Drain again.
3. In a pan, lightly fry the shallot in a little oil and add the green beans. Season to taste with salt and freshly ground black pepper.
4. Serve with the sauce and champ, or as a side.
