## Ingredients

- 500 grams minced lamb
- 2 tablespoons tomato paste
- 1 small red onion, finely diced
- 2 garlic cloves, crushed
- 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon
- 1/4 teaspoon allspice
- 1/2 teaspoon dried oregano
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon pepper
- 2 tablespoons sunflower oil
- 4 slices mature Cheddar
- 4 burger buns
- Rocket leaves
- Red onion rings
- Tomato slices

## Method

1. Place the minced lamb in a bowl along with the tomato paste, onion and garlic. Sprinkle on the cinnamon, allspice, oregano, salt and pepper.
2. Use your hands to mix the ingredients together thoroughly. Divide the mixture into 4 equal portions.
3. Shape each portion into a ball and flatten to the size of your burger bun.
4. Place the burgers on a grill and grill for about 14 minutes, turning occasionally until cooked through.
5. To finish, top each burger with a slice of cheese and grill until the cheese melts. You can also add your burger buns to the grill pan if you wish to have them toasted (keep an eye on them).
6. Finally assemble your burger in the bun using the rocket leaves, onion rings, tomato slices, and then top with some baba ganoush to add the delicious flavour of aubergine.
