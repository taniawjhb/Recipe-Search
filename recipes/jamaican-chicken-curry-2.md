## Ingredients

- 2 tablespoons oil
- 1 tablespoon minced ginger
- 1 tablespoon minced garlic
- 1 cup (160 grams) onions, chopped
- 1 1/2 tablespoons Jamaican curry powder
- 1 scotch bonnet pepper, sliced
- 3 sprigs fresh thyme, or 1/2 teaspoon dried thyme
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1/2 teaspoon ground allspice
- 500 grams boneless skinless chicken thighs, cut into 3 pieces each
- 1 large potato, cut into 1-inch chunks
- 1 cup (250 millilitres) water

## Method

1. Heat a large pot or saute pan over medium heat, once hot add the oil.
2. When the oil is hot, add ginger and garlic, and stir for 20 seconds. Add chopped onion and mix well, cook until the onions have softened up (about 1 to 2 minutes).
3. Add the Jamaican curry powder, the sliced scotch bonnet pepper, thyme, salt and allspice and mix well.
4. At this point, if you have any browning or sticking that has occurred, use 1/4 to 1/3 cup of water to deglaze, scraping well, and allowing the water to evaporate.
5. Add chicken, potato and 1 to 2 cups of water, stir well and then simmer on medium heat for about 20 to 30 minutes covered, checking and adding liquid as required.
6. Once the potatoes are cooked and the chicken is tender remove from the heat. Check and adjust seasoning.
