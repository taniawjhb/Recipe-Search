## Ingredients

- 12 red peppers
- 10 chicken thighs
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, peeled and chopped
- 2 garlic cloves, peeled and crushed
- 1 ½ litres chicken stock
- Handful fresh basil leaves, torn
- Handful fresh mint leaves, torn
- 2 tsp fresh oregano
- Salt and black pepper
- Extra virgin olive oil, to serve

## Method

1. Preheat oven to 200°C and roast the peppers for 25 minutes, then leave to cool. Scrape out the seeds, peel and slice thinly.
2. At the same time cook the chicken thighs. Season them with salt and pepper and roast alongside the peppers for 20 minutes, or until cooked through. Cool. Discard skin and bones and shred the meat.
3. Heat oil in a large saucepan, add the onion and saute until golden. Add strips of peppers and the garlic and cook a further 2 minutes.
4. Add the stock to the peppers along with the chicken and herbs. Adjust seasoning and simmer for 5-10 minutes.
