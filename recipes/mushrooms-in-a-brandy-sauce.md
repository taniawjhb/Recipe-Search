## Ingredients

- 250 grams portabellini or wild mushrooms
- 1 clove garlic, finely minced
- 1 tablespoon butter
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 60 millilitres marsala or sherry or brandy
- 1/4 teaspoon finely chopped red chili
- 1 teaspoon finely chopped thyme
- 1/2 cup cream
- 1 teaspoon black truffle paste (optional)

## Method

1. In a pan heat the olive oil and butter with the garlic and chili.
2. Cook for 1-2 minutes or until the garlic gets a touch of gold, then add the sliced mushrooms (brush and remove tough parts first).
3. Cook the mushrooms for a few minutes, adding a little extra olive oil if you need.
4. Once the mushrooms are cooked add the marsala and burn off the alcohol.
5. Add the cream and reduce.
6. Now add the parsley and stir through before serving - add the truffle if using it.
