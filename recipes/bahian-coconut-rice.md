## Ingredients

- 250gms basmati rice
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 medium onion, finely chopped
- 1 1/2 tsp sea salt
- 50gms grated fresh coconut
- 200ml coconut milk

## Method

1. Put the rice in a sieve and wash under cold water until the water runs clear. Leave to drain for a few minutes.
2. Heat the oil in a saucepan over a medium heat. Add the onion and cook gently for about 8 minutes, until softened.
3. Add the salt and grated coconut now.
4. Pour in the coconut milk and 200ml water and stir again.
5. Bring to the boil, reduce the heat to low and cover the pan. Simmer for 10 minutes until most of the liquid has been absorbed.
6. Remove from the heat and leave to steam covered for about 10 minutes.
7. Garnish with some fresh coriander and serve the rice warm.
