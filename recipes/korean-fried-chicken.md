## Ingredients

For the chicken:

- 8 chicken breasts
- 1 teaspoon salt
- pinch black pepper (or to taste)
- 1 teaspoon grated ginger
- 4-5 cups oil for frying, about 2 inches deep (use a deep, bottom-heavy, medium size pot or pan)

For the wet batter:

- 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
- 1/2 teaspoon baking powder
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1/2 teaspoon onion powder
- 1/4 cup potato starch (or cornstarch)
- 3/4 cup water

For the red spicy sauce:

- 2 tablespoons grated onion
- 1 tablespoon minced garlic
- 1 teaspoon finely grated ginger
- 1 tablespoon soy sauce
- 4 tablespoons rice wine (or mirin)
- 2 to 3 tablespoons hot sauce like Sriracha
- 4 tablespoons chilli sauce or ketchup
- 2 tablespoons gochujang
- 2 tablespoons corn syrup or honey
- 1 tablespoon sugar
- 1 teaspoon sesame oil

## Method

1. Wash the chicken, and drain thoroughly. Mix with the salt, pepper, and ginger. Let it sit in the fridge for 2 hours or longer (overnight to 24 hours).
2. Mix the wet batter ingredients in a bowl, and stir well until smooth with no visible lumps.
3. Add the oil to a deep fryer, wok, or large pot. Heat the oil to 320°F (up to 330°F). Dip each piece in the wet batter with tongs and shake off the excess batter before dropping the chicken in the oil, one piece at a time. Fry them in two batches. Cook until lightly golden, about 6 minutes, depending on the size. Remove them with a wire skimmer or a slotted spoon. Drain on a wire rack or in a large mesh strainer set on a bowl.
4. Reheat the oil to 350°F (up to 360°F). Add the chicken (you can do this in one batch for the second frying), and deep fry again for about 5 minutes, until golden brown. Drain on a wire rack.
5. For the red sauce: add 1 tablespoon of cooking oil to a pan and then the onion, garlic, and ginger. Stir for a minute until fragrant. Add all the other sauce ingredients, and stir well. Simmer until it thickens slightly, about 4 to 5 minutes. Turn the heat off. The sauce will get slightly thicker as it cools down.
6. You can either toss the fried chicken pieces in the sauce or hand-brush them. Sprinkle with optional sesame seeds or chopped scallion to serve.
