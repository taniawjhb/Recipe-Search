## Ingredients

- 2 cups milk
- 2 cups heavy cream
- 2 cups coconut milk
- 1 cup coconut flakes, toasted
- 8 egg yolks
- 1 1/2 cups sugar
- Pinch salt

## Method

1. Bring the milk, heavy cream, coconut milk and coconut flakes to a simmer in a heavy saucepan for a few minutes. Allow to steep for 10 minutes.
2. Whisk the egg yolks, sugar and salt together, long enough to dissolve the sugar.
3. Temper the hot liquid into the egg mixture. Return everything to the saucepan over low heat, stirring all the while, until the mixture starts to thicken. Strain through a chinois. Chill immediately over an ice bath.
4. Churn in a standard ice cream machine.
