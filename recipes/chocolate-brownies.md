## Ingredients

- 240 grams butter, melted and cooled
- 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
- 150 grams brown sugar
- 200 grams white sugar
- 4 large eggs, room temperature
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- 150 grams all purpose flour
- 120 grams good quality, unsweetened cocoa powder
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 100 grams roughly chopped chocolate or large chocolate chips
- 100 grams roasted, blanched hazelnuts
- Zest of 1 orange

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 175°C then line a 7x11 inch baking tray with parchment paper and set aside.
2. In a large bowl combine melted butter, oil and both sugars. Add the eggs, vanilla, orange peel and salt then whisk for about one minute until evenly combined and light in color.
3. Over the same bowl sift in the flour and cocoa powder. Gently fold the dry ingredients into the wet ingredients until JUST combined (do NOT over mix). Fold in the chocolate chunks and the hazelnuts.
4. Pour the batter into the prepared pan, then smooth the top.
5. Bake for 35-40 minutes, or until the centre of the brownies no longer jiggles and is JUST set to the touch.
6. Remove from the oven and allow to cool to room temperature before removing from the baking tray and slicing into 16 brownies. Enjoy!
