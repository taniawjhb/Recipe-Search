## Ingredients

- 1 kilogram prawns, shell on, deveined and cleaned
- 3 tablespoons butter
- 1/4 cup olive oil
- 2 cloves garlic, finely chopped
- 1 glass white wine
- Salt and pepper
- 8 cups prawn stock
- 1/4 cup olive oil
- 1 medium onion, diced
- 1 stick celery, finely chopped
- 1 carrot, finely chopped
- Large pinch of saffron
- 2 cups arborio rice
- 1 cup dry white wine
- 1 tablespoon flat leaf parsley, chopped
- 4 tablespoons cold butter
- Salt and pepper

## Method

1. In a large heavy-based saucepan, add the butter and olive oil. Place over a medium heat. Once it is hot add the garlic and stir quickly. Almost immediately add the prawns. Add them in a single layer allowing them to colour, but NOT cook all the way through.
2. As the prawns are cooked remove and set aside. Once all the prawns have been cooked, remove their heads and shells. Reserve all the juices and shells.
3. Return the pan to the heat, add half a roughly chopped onion, a small bunch of fresh celery leaf and stir to coat.
4. Once the onion starts to soften add the white wine and deglaze the pan. Now add back all the prawn shells and reserved juices.
5. Add 2 litres of a good quality light vegetable stock and simmer for 20 minutes, skimming off any foam that surfaces. Strain and use immediately, or allow to cool and freeze.
6. Heat the oil in a large saucepan over medium heat; add onion, carrot and celery and season with salt and pepper.
7. Add the rice and stir to coat with the oil; season with salt and pepper and cook until slightly translucent. The rice should be hot and sizzling; stir carefully to ensure it does not stick.
8. Add wine to the hot rice, the liquid should hiss and be quickly absorbed. Stir gently until almost all the liquid has cooked off. Add hot stock a ladleful at a time, completely stirring in the liquid each time to coax the starches out of the rice.
9. Keep adding broth as needed so there is always a 1/2 centimetre liquid layer over the rice.
10. After about 8 minutes add the saffron which has been soaked in 2 tablespoons warm water.
11. After 15 to 20 minutes, the rice should be close to al dente. Add the reserved prawn meat, cut into bite size hunks, and stir to combine well — the prawns will cook through in the heat of the rice.
12. Taste and adjust seasoning. Now cover the pan and allow it to stand for 2-3 minutes.
