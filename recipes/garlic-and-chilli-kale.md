## Ingredients

- 1 bag mixed kale (2 bunches, about 300 grams)
- 2 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
- 2 cloves garlic
- Pinch of chili flakes (peperoncino)
- Salt & pepper to taste

## Method

1. Pull the kale leaves from their stems, then tear leaves into pieces. Wash thoroughly.
2. Bring a large pot of water to boil. Place the leaves in, toss, and boil for about 5-7 minutes in boiling water until bright green and slightly softened. Drain with a colander.
3. Heat up a large sauté pan with EVO oil, garlic, and a pinch of chili flakes. Sauté for 1-2 minutes, then add the kale leaves, adjust with salt and pepper, and toss to coat.
4. Sauté for 5-10 minutes on medium heat, stirring often. Once the kale is tender you are ready to serve.
5. Turn off the heat and plate.
