## Ingredients

- 5 cups vegetable stock
- ¾ teaspoon salt
- 1¾ cups polenta
- 1 cup cream
- 120g crumbled Gorgonzola cheese
- 3 tablespoons unsalted butter

## Method

1. Boil stock. In a medium-sized saucepan, add the salt to the stock and bring to a boil.
2. Add polenta. Gradually whisk in the polenta. Reduce the heat to low and cook until the mixture thickens and the polenta is tender, stirring often, about 10 minutes.
3. Add remaining ingredients. Turn off the heat and add the cream, cheese, and butter. Stir to blend and serve immediately!
