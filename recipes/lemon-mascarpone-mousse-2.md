## Ingredients

- 210 grams mascarpone cheese
- 1 cup whole, whipping or heavy cream, cold
- 2-3 tablespoons icing sugar
- 1 tablespoon lemon zest
- 1-2 tablespoons lemon juice

## Method

1. In a large bowl whip the cream until stiff. Set aside 1/2 cup of the whipped cream for the topping.
2. In a medium bowl, beat the mascarpone, sugar, juice and zest until creamy. Fold the mascarpone mixture into the whipped cream until combined.
3. Spoon the mousse into 4 cups or glasses and chill for 1 hour. Top with a dollop of whipped cream, sprinkle with chopped white chocolate or lemon zest before serving if desired.
