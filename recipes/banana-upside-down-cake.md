## Ingredients

- 300 grams caster sugar
- 5 ripe bananas
- 1/4 teaspoon cinnamon
- 140 grams soft unsalted butter
- 180 grams plain cake flour
- 1 1/2 teaspoons baking powder
- 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 4 eggs, separated
- 140 grams caster sugar
- 2 large ripe bananas, peeled and mashed

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 170°C.
2. Generously butter the base and side of a heavy based 23 centimetres springform tin, then line it with baking paper.
3. To make the banana caramel, put the sugar and 150 millilitres water in a heavy based saucepan and cook over a high heat until the sugar has dissolved. Let it boil until thickened to a golden brown caramel, pour it into the cake tin, tipping slightly from side to side until evenly coated.
4. Peel the bananas and cut them in half lengthways, arrange them over the caramel in a nice pattern, trimming any extra if need be. Now dust with the ground cinnamon.
5. To make the cake batter, sift together the flour, baking powder and cinnamon into a large bowl. Put the egg whites in a separate clean bowl and whisk until stiff peaks form. Put the butter and sugar in the bowl of a mixer and whisk until light and fluffy. Slowly whisk in the egg yolks one at a time. Then fold in the mashed bananas followed by the dry ingredients. Once combined, fold in the stiff egg whites.
6. Pour the batter into the tin and spread evenly with a spatula. Bake for 50-60 minutes, or until a skewer inserted into the centre comes out cleanly.
7. Remove and leave to cool for a few minutes before unmolding. Be careful when unmolding — the caramel will be warm.
