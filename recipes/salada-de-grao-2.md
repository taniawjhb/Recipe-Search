## Ingredients

- 2 cans chickpeas, drained and washed
- 1/2 red onion, sliced thin
- 2 boiled eggs
- Handful of chopped parsley
- 1/3 cup olive oil
- 1/4 cup white wine vinegar
- Salt & pepper

## Method

1. Place chickpeas, sliced onion, olive oil, vinegar, salt, pepper and half the chopped parsley in a salad bowl and toss.
2. Slice the hard-boiled eggs into wheels and place on top of the salad.
3. Finish with fresh chopped parsley.

## Notes

This salad is traditionally made with flakes of cooked bacalhau spread on top.
