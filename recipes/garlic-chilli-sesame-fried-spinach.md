## Ingredients

- 400 grams spinach, washed, cut into 4 centimetre strips
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 3 tablespoons neutral oil
- 1 Thai red chilli
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 2 tablespoons toasted sesame seeds
- 1 tablespoon sesame oil

## Method

1. In a large frying pan or wok on high heat, add the oil, chilli and garlic. Stir-fry until the garlic is highly golden and fragrant, about 30 seconds.
2. Add the spinach and stir-fry on high heat until the spinach is wilted and softened, about 2 minutes.
3. Season with salt and pepper to taste and add the sesame oil, toss to coat and then sprinkle with sesame seeds just before serving.
