## Ingredients

- 700g ground beef
- Salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste
- 1 cup parsley/thyme/sage leaves, finely minced
- 2 minced garlic cloves
- 1 egg
- 1/2 cup vegetable oil
- 1 cup finely minced onion (about 1/2 large onion)
- 1/2 cup dry white wine
- 3 tablespoons Dijon mustard
- 1 1/2 cups heavy cream

## Method

1. In a mixing bowl, combine the beef, salt and pepper, parsley, garlic and egg. Mix well together and shape the meat into 12 meatballs.
2. In a heavy pan, heat the vegetable oil over medium-high heat until it shimmers. Cook the meatballs, about 3 minutes on each side, until light brown. The meatballs won't stick to the pan because you're using so much oil. Pour off most of the rendered fat.
3. Once the meatballs are browned, remove and keep warm.
4. Remove most of the oil from the pan, then add the finely chopped onion. Let the onions cook over medium heat until tender but not brown, about 3 minutes. Add the wine and let it simmer and cook down a bit.
5. Whisk in the Dijon mustard and, once amalgamated, whisk in the cream. Add back the meatballs. Cook in the cream over medium heat, uncovered, about 5 minutes, or until the cream has reduced by about 1/3.
6. Cover the pan and simmer on a low flame until the meatballs are cooked through.
