## Ingredients

- 800 grams eggplant, cut into 2 centimetre cubes
- 2 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil (for the eggplant)
- 1/2 teaspoon each salt and pepper
- 2 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil (for the sauce)
- 4 garlic cloves, finely minced
- 1/2 small onion, very finely chopped
- 65 millilitres dry white wine, anything not too sweet or woody
- 700 grams tomato passata
- 65 millilitres water
- 1 teaspoon dried oregano
- 1/2 teaspoon red chilli flakes, optional
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Fresh basil and parmesan, to serve

## Method

1. Start by cutting the aubergine into 2 centimetre cubes. Heat a large pan over medium-high heat and add a generous splash of oil. Once hot, add the aubergine cubes in a single layer and fry, turning occasionally, until evenly golden and tender. Remove and drain on paper towel, then allow to cool.
2. Heat the oil in a very large skillet over medium heat. Add the garlic, cook for 20 seconds until golden, then add the onion and chilli flakes and cook for 2 to 3 minutes until translucent and cooked through.
3. Increase the heat to medium-high and add the wine, if using. Let it simmer, scraping the base of the pan to deglaze, until the wine mostly evaporates.
4. Add the tomato passata, dried oregano, red pepper flakes, salt and pepper. Stir and simmer 20-25 minutes.
5. Stir in the eggplant, then add cooked and drained pasta. Gently toss until the pasta is coated in the sauce (so the eggplant does not turn to mush).
6. Serve immediately, garnished with a good pinch of fresh basil and parmesan.
