## Ingredients

- Fettuccine pasta
- 2/3 cup extra-virgin olive oil
- 1 large bunch asparagus, trimmed, thinly sliced on a deep diagonal
- 4 garlic cloves, smashed
- Zest of 1 lemon
- 1/2 teaspoon crushed red pepper flakes
- Cream (added to reduce)
- 8 large basil leaves
- 1 lemon, juiced
- 60 grams Parmesan, finely grated (about 1 cup), plus more for serving

## Method

1. Cook the pasta in a large pot of boiling salted water, stirring occasionally, until al dente. Drain, reserving 1 1/2 cups pasta cooking liquid.
2. Meanwhile, heat the oil in a large Dutch oven or other heavy pot over medium-high until shimmering. Add the asparagus, season with salt, and cook, stirring often, until just beginning to take on color, about 1 minute.
3. Add the garlic, lemon zest and red pepper flakes and cook, stirring, until fragrant, about 30 seconds. Add the cream and reduce by half.
4. Add the lemon juice to half a cup of reserved pasta water, then add this along with the pasta and basil to the pot with the asparagus mixture and return to medium-high heat. Now add 60 grams Parmesan and more reserved pasta cooking liquid as needed.
5. Cook, tossing vigorously but gently and adding more pasta cooking liquid if needed, until the sauce is creamy and emulsified and the pasta is coated, about 1 minute. Taste and season with more salt if needed.
6. Remove and discard the garlic, or paste it and add it back for a bold punchy flavour.
7. Divide the pasta among bowls, placing a lemon strip in each, and top with more Parmesan.
