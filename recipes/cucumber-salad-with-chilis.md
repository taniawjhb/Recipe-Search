## Ingredients

- 2 cucumbers
- 1 small red onion, finely sliced
- 1 tbsp white vinegar
- 3 green chillis, finely chopped
- 1 tbsp light olive oil
- Salt to taste

## Method

1. Peel skin off cucumber.
2. Cut cucumber in half and remove the seeds.
3. Finely slice the halves into a bowl.
4. Finely slice the chillis and onion and add to a separate bowl.
5. To the bowl with the onion and chili add the oil and vinegar, as well as salt and pepper.
6. Now add the finely sliced cucumber and mix well - set aside for at least 10 minutes.
