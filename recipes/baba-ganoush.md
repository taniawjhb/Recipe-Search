## Ingredients

- 1 large eggplant
- 1⁄4 cup tahini
- 3 garlic cloves
- 1⁄4 cup fresh lemon juice
- 1 pinch ground cumin
- Salt, to taste
- 1 tablespoon extra virgin olive oil
- 1 tablespoon fresh flat-leaf parsley
- 1⁄4 cup brine-cured black olives, such as kalamata

## Method

1. Prick the eggplant with a fork in several places and bake in a hot oven for 25-40 minutes until the aubergine is blistered and soft.
2. Remove from the oven, let cool slightly, then peel off and discard the skin.
3. Place the eggplant flesh in a bowl. Using a fork, mash the eggplant to a paste. Add the 1/4 cup tahini, the garlic, the 1/4 cup lemon juice and the cumin and mix well.
4. Season with salt, then taste and add more tahini and/or lemon juice, if desired.
5. Transfer the mixture to a serving bowl and spread with the back of a spoon to form a shallow well.
6. Drizzle the olive oil over the top and sprinkle with paprika. Serve with the black olives and parsley.
