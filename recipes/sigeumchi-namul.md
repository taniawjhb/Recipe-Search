## Ingredients

- 250 grams English spinach or baby spinach
- 1 teaspoon fine sea salt, to add to the water
- 6 cups water to boil
- 1 teaspoon green onion, finely chopped
- 1/2 teaspoon minced garlic
- 1/4 teaspoon fine sea salt or to taste
- 1 teaspoon toasted sesame seeds
- 1 tablespoon sesame oil

## Method

1. Trim the spinach roots and wash the spinach in cold water thoroughly.
2. Boil the water in a pot/sauce pan for 5 to 7 minutes. Add the salt (1 teaspoon). Once the water starts to boil, plunge the spinach into the pot and leave it for 30 seconds.
3. Drain the boiled water away and run cold water on the spinach for 1-2 minutes.
4. Squeeze the spinach to remove excess water.
5. Cut the spinach into 2-3 pieces with a knife. (If you are using baby spinach, you can skip this step.)
6. Add the seasoning sauce and mix well with your hands.
7. Serve it on a plate and enjoy.
