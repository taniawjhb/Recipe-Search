## Ingredients

- 1kg fresh mussels
- 1 can tomato passata or peeled tomatoes
- 1.5 teaspoon peperoncino flakes
- 3 garlic cloves, peeled and chopped
- 3 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
- 1 handful fresh parsley, chopped
- 1/4 cup white wine, optional

## Method

1. Rinse the mussels thoroughly. Use a wire brush to scrub away any debris from the shells, and tug off the beards. Peel and finely chop the garlic. Wash and roughly chop the parsley.
2. Place the cleaned mussels into a deep pan and cook them solo over medium heat until they open up. Transfer the opened mussels to a large bowl and let them cool slightly. Strain the cooking liquid through a cheesecloth or fine tea towel into another bowl—set this fragrant broth aside.
3. Once the mussels are cool enough to handle, leave some mussels in their shells for presentation, and remove the rest from their shells.
4. In a large skillet, heat 3 tablespoons of olive oil and sauté the chopped garlic and peperoncino flakes for a minute or two, until just golden. Now, pour in the tomato passata, reserved mussel broth, a pinch of salt, and a touch of black pepper, add a splash of white wine if you're using it. Stir everything together and cook for another couple of minutes.
5. Stir well and let the sauce simmer gently over low to medium heat for 5-10 minutes, until it thickens slightly. Sprinkle in some of the chopped parsley.
6. Now add the reserved mussel meat and mussels in shells as well as any other juices that have collected, toss to coat. Serve immediately, garnished with a final flourish of chopped parsley.
