## Ingredients

- 800 grams frozen prawn meat (defrost overnight in fridge)
- 4 teaspoons sunflower oil
- 2 onions, finely chopped
- 2 tablespoons fish masala
- 1/2 teaspoon turmeric
- 2 tomatoes, finely chopped
- 1 tin coconut cream
- salt and black pepper to taste
- 50 grams grated coconut
- chopped fresh coriander to garnish

## Method

1. Heat the oil in a saucepan and add the onions and finely chopped garlic. Saute the onions until translucent.
2. Add the fish masala to the onions. Stir through and cook until the aroma is released. Now add the dried coconut and cook for two minutes.
3. Add the tomato and simmer for 20 minutes, add a little water if needed.
4. Add the prawns and gently simmer for two or three minutes.
5. Stir through the coconut cream and simmer for 5 minutes.
6. Garnish with the chopped coriander and serve with coconut rice.
