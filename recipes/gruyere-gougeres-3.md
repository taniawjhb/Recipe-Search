## Ingredients

- 5 tablespoons butter
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon fresh ground black pepper
- 1/4 teaspoon freshly ground nutmeg
- 1 cup water, plus 1/2 teaspoon water, divided
- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 1 cup grated gruyere cheese
- 5 large eggs, at room temperature (very important)

## Method

1. Add the butter, salt, pepper and nutmeg to 1 cup water in a medium saucepan and bring to a boil over medium-high heat.
2. When the butter melts, reduce the heat to low.
3. Add the flour to the saucepan all at once and beat with a wooden spoon over low heat for about a minute until the mixture pulls away from the sides of the pan.
4. Remove the pan from the heat.
5. Add the cheese to the pan and beat with a wooden spoon until thoroughly mixed.
6. Add 4 eggs, one at a time, beating each into the batter until well mixed before adding the next.
7. Continue beating the mixture until it is smooth, shiny and firm.
8. Now in a frying pan heat sufficient oil to deep fry small balls, until 140°C.
9. Carefully fry small teaspoon-size balls of batter until puffed up and golden and cooked through, remove with a slotted spoon and drain on paper towel, sprinkle with salt and serve warm.
