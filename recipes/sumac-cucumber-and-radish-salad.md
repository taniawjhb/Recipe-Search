## Ingredients

- 1/4 cup lemon juice
- 3 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 tablespoon honey
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 5 Persian cucumbers, thinly sliced
- 1 bunch radishes, thinly sliced
- 1/2 red onion, thinly sliced
- 1/4 cup chopped dill, loosely packed
- 1/2 cup loosely packed mint leaves (about 30 leaves)
- 1 tablespoon toasted sesame seeds
- 1-2 teaspoons sumac

## Method

1. Start by prepping the vinaigrette. Whisk together the lemon juice, olive oil, honey and salt in a large serving bowl.
2. Prep the veggies. It is highly recommended to use a mandoline to slice the radishes and cucumbers, as you will get even, uniform pieces (plus it is much faster). Once prepped, add the veggies, along with the sliced red onion, dill, mint, toasted sesame seeds and sumac, into the bowl with the vinaigrette.
3. Mix well with tongs and adjust the salt as needed. This salad does not hold up well and the veggies start to lose their crunch if they sit too long.
