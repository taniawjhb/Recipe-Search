## Ingredients

- 300 grams cake flour
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 2 egg yolks
- 3 tablespoons sunflower oil
- 125 millilitres water
- 125 grams fresh white breadcrumbs
- 100 grams butter
- 750 grams Granny Smith apples (peeled, cored and thinly sliced)
- 150 grams cherries, deseeded
- 125 grams light brown sugar
- 50 grams flakes toasted almonds
- 1 teaspoon cinnamon
- 100 grams melted butter
- Runny honey to glaze

## Method

1. Sift the flour and the salt into the bowl, in a separate large bowl combine the eggs, water and sunflower oil.
2. Make a well in the centre of the flour and add the liquid, bring together into a ball and transfer to the counter.
3. Knead until a soft smooth dough has been formed, about 15 minutes. Wrap in cling wrap and set aside for 30 minutes.
4. Preheat the oven to 200°C. In a frying pan fry the breadcrumbs with the butter until golden brown. Remove from heat and transfer to a bowl.
5. Peel the apples and slice them into very thin slices in a large bowl.
6. Add the breadcrumbs, sugar, almonds, cinnamon to the apples. Mix very well using your hands to ensure the breadcrumbs coat all the pieces of apple.
7. Lightly flour a very clean tablecloth. Gently roll out the dough and then stretch it out with your hands. Stretch the dough until it reaches a 60 centimetre square piece. Then using a knife trim the edges.
8. Brush the dough with the melted butter, reserving some to glaze the outside.
9. Scatter the filling evenly over the dough, and then using the tablecloth as a guide, roll up the strudel into a long log.
10. Transfer the strudel to a greased baking sheet and glaze with the remaining butter. Bake for 40 minutes until the strudel is golden brown and cooked through.
11. Remove the strudel from the oven and glaze with a little honey, serve warm with a simple custard.
