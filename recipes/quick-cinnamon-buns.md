## Ingredients

### Dough

- 480 grams cake flour, sifted
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 177 millilitres lukewarm milk
- 90 millilitres lukewarm cream
- 50 grams sugar
- 23 grams fresh yeast (1 1/2 envelope active dry yeast)
- 90 grams melted butter
- 2 egg yolks
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

### Topping

- 160 grams softened butter
- 180 grams soft dark sugar
- 6 teaspoons finely ground cinnamon
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

## Method

1. In a medium bowl stir fresh yeast with 2 tablespoons of the sugar until it liquefies. Stir in the lukewarm milk and cream and then add the egg yolks and melted butter - whisk to combine.
2. In a large bowl whisk together the flour and salt.
3. Pour the milk mixture over the dry ingredients and start kneading it until it pulls away from the edges of the bowl. Give the dough the shape of a ball. Sprinkle oil onto a clean bowl, place the dough and cover with plastic wrap. Let it rest for about 1 hour at room temperature (warm space) until doubled in size.
4. While the dough rises, add your vanilla extract to the soft butter and work it in, until the butter is light and creamy and the vanilla has been incorporated. Set aside. Combine cinnamon and brown sugar. Set aside.
5. Preheat the oven to 190°C. Butter two 12 cup muffin tins, generously with the reserved butter.
6. On a floured surface, using a rolling pin roll the dough to a square of 50 x 50 centimetres. Roll the dough gently, allow the rolling pin to do the work. Then trim the pastry into a neat square.
7. Dab the butter around the pastry and then spread it until you have a thin, even layer about 2 millimetres thick. Now take your reserved cinnamon sugar and scatter it evenly over the entire piece of pastry. Very gently with your hands smooth out the sugar so that the pastry is evenly coated. Now gently press the sugar down into the butter.
8. Measure your dough and then measure a third of the way along the pastry and carefully fold the pastry. Press down gently on the folded half, now lift the pastry over again to close it, check to ensure it lines up evenly in thirds and press down again gently.
9. Trim the open edges with a sharp knife coated in flour. Now cut the pastry into 2.5 centimetres strips. Taking one strip at a time, gently pat the strip flat, until it is nicely tightened. With your sharp floured knife, cut the pastry twice all the way through the bottom resulting in three even strips.
10. Now plait these strips all the way through and then take the closed end and roll it inwards. Place in a muffin cup. Once all the strips are plaited, beat your egg and glaze the tops of the buns lightly.
11. Bake in the preheated oven for 25-30 minutes, until cooked through and nicely golden. Allow to cool for a few minutes in the tin and then carefully remove to a cooling rack.
12. Once warm dust with icing sugar or a runny icing glaze; for a decadent treat drizzle them whilst warm with a warm caramel sauce.
