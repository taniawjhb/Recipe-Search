## Ingredients

- 500g tenderstem broccoli or regular broccoli, cut into florets
- 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- 1/4 tsp each salt and pepper
- 1 garlic clove, finely minced

### Sour Cream Sauce

- 1/2 cup sour cream
- 1/4 cup Greek yoghurt
- 1.5 tsp honey
- 1-2 tbsp lime juice
- Zest of 1 lime
- 1/2 garlic clove, crushed smooth
- 1/4 tsp each salt and pepper
- 2 tbsp finely chopped chives

## Method

1. Preheat oven to 220°C/425°F (200°C fan).
2. Sauce: Mix the sour cream sauce ingredients until combined. It should be loose enough to drizzle, but not too runny. Set aside for 15 minutes.
3. Toss broccoli: Pile the broccoli on a tray, drizzle with oil, and sprinkle with salt, pepper and garlic.
4. Roast for 12-15 minutes until the broccoli floret ends are dark brown and crunchy (the best part!) and the stem is just tender. (If you want it really soft, lower the temperature to 180°C/350°F and cook for 20 minutes.)
5. Plate up: Transfer to a serving plate and allow to cool until warm. Drizzle over the sauce, sprinkle with almonds or pine nuts and serve. Excellent served warm or at room temperature.
