## Ingredients

- 500 grams sliced pork fillet
- 4 tablespoons Panaeng Curry Paste
- 8 lime leaves, shredded
- 1 red chilli
- 3 baby marrow, sliced 2 centimetres
- 3 patty pan, quartered
- Handful of broccoli florets
- 2 cans coconut milk
- 1/2 cup shredded basil
- 3 tablespoons fish sauce
- 1 teaspoon peanut butter
- 1 teaspoon sugar
- Coconut/sunflower oil for frying
- 1 tablespoon roasted, chopped peanuts for serving

## Method

1. In a medium saucepan over medium-high heat, start by adding your oil and the curry paste and cook until aromatic.
2. In the same saucepan, add the coconut milk and bring to a simmer, making sure to stir and deglaze the bottom.
3. Simmer for 10 minutes, then add the vegetables (keep the basil and chilli till the end), fish sauce, peanut butter, and sugar.
4. When the vegetables are cooked halfway (around 6 minutes), add your pork into the curry and simmer for another 5 minutes or until the pork is cooked through.
5. Stir through the chopped basil and red chilli. Serve with rice.
