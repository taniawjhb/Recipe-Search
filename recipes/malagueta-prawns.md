## Ingredients

- 6 red chillies, finely chopped
- 3 cloves garlic
- 4 tablespoons tomato puree
- 1 teaspoon dried oregano
- 2 teaspoons red chilli flakes
- 4 tablespoons red wine vinegar
- 2 teaspoons sweet paprika
- 3 teaspoons salt
- 50 millilitres ground nut oil
- 1 kilogram prawns, deveined, shell on

## Method

1. Split the chillies and place in a pan with the oil and garlic. Roast in an 180°C oven for ten minutes.
2. Remove and blend with all the other ingredients.
3. Drain the prawns and mix well with the marinade.
4. Place on a metal skewer, sprinkle with sea salt and grill until the prawns turn completely opaque.
5. Serve immediately with a wedge of lime to garnish.
