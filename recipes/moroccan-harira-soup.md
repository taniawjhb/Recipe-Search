## Ingredients

- Extra virgin olive oil
- 1 large yellow onion, finely chopped
- 2 celery stalks, chopped
- 1 carrot, peeled and chopped
- Salt
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1½ teaspoons black pepper
- 1½ teaspoons turmeric
- 1 teaspoon cumin
- ½ teaspoon ground ginger
- ½ teaspoon ground cinnamon
- ½ teaspoon cayenne
- 2 cans crushed tomatoes
- 3 tablespoons tomato paste
- 1 cup packed chopped fresh cilantro
- 1 can green lentils, rinsed
- 1 cup red lentils, rinsed
- 1 can chickpeas
- 1.7l vegetable or chicken stock
- ¼ cup long grain rice, rinsed, or ¼ cup broken vermicelli
- Lemon wedges, for serving

## Method

1. In a large Dutch oven, heat 4 tbsp extra virgin olive oil over medium heat until shimmering. Add the onions, celery, and carrots. Season with salt. Cook for 5 minutes, stirring regularly, until softened.
2. Add the garlic and spices and cook for 1 to 2 minutes, stirring regularly.
3. Add the crushed tomatoes, tomato paste, cilantro, lentils (both green and red), and chickpeas. Add a dash more salt and cook for 5 minutes, stirring.
4. Add the stock and raise the heat. Bring to a boil for 5 minutes, then turn the heat to low. Cover and let simmer for 45 minutes or until the legumes are fully cooked and very tender (check occasionally and plan to add more stock or water. The soup will be thick, but it should not be so thick that you cannot pour it. Make sure to adjust the salt as you add more liquid).
5. Stir in the rice and cook for another 15 minutes or until the rice is fully cooked.
6. Serve with lemon wedges.
