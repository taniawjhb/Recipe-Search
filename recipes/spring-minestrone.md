## Ingredients

- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 6 green onions
- 2 large garlic cloves
- 450 grams potatoes, cut into 2 centimetre chunks
- 1 can tomatoes, chopped before using
- 4 cups vegetable stock or chicken stock
- salt
- 1 cup zucchini, roughly chopped
- 1 can butter beans, rinsed and drained
- 1 cup peas (fresh or frozen)
- 250 grams asparagus, cut into 1 inch chunks
- 2 cups greens (chard, spinach, kale, etc), sliced into thin ribbons
- up to 1/4 cup pesto
- grated parmesan or pecorino cheese for garnish (omit for vegan version)
- 1 teaspoon black pepper

## Method

1. To begin prepare your vegetables, chop the onions, garlic, peel and cut the potatoes, and slice the zucchini into discs.
2. Now in a dutch oven add some olive oil then sauté the onions, once softened but not browned add the garlic and cook for 1 minute. Now add the potatoes and cook 1 minute more.
3. Now add the tomatoes, stock and salt. Bring to a simmer, add salt to taste, then cover and cook over medium-low heat for 15 minutes.
4. Now add the cannellini beans, finely shredded kale and zucchini discs and cook for another 5 minutes.
5. Finally add the peas and cut asparagus spears, and cook on a low heat until the potatoes and all vegetables are cooked.
6. Turn off the heat and stir in the pesto. Add black pepper and more salt to taste (depending on how salty your stock is, you may need to add 1 to 2 teaspoons of salt).
7. Serve topped with grated cheese.
