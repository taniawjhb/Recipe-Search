## Ingredients

### For the dough

- 300 grams + 2 tablespoons. all-purpose plain flour
- 1 teaspoon. salt
- 14 grams dried yeast
- 1 pinch of sugar
- 1 cup warm water
- 3 tablespoons. olive oil

### For the topping

- 1 medium onion, finely chopped
- 2 cloves garlic
- 400 grams/14 ounces. ground beef or ground lamb or mixture
- 1/2 red/yellow bell pepper or 1 green pointy pepper, finely diced
- 2 medium tomatoes, deseeded and diced
- 1 tablespoon. lemon juice
- 1 tablespoon. olive oil
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon
- 1 teaspoon paprika
- 1 teaspoon ground allspice
- 1/2 teaspoon chili powder
- 1 tablespoon tomato paste
- Small bunch flat leaf parsley
- Salt (1 teaspoon. salt is recommended) and freshly ground black pepper to taste
- Pinch chili powder
- 1 egg + 15 millilitres/1 tablespoon. olive oil to brush the pide

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 180°C/350°F.
2. Stir the dried yeast and sugar in a small bowl and pour in 1/2 cup warm water. Dissolve the yeast in water, mixing with your fingers. Set aside for the yeast mixture to get frothy for 5 minutes.
3. Combine the flour and salt in a large bowl. Make a well in the middle and pour in 2 tablespoons. olive oil and the yeast mixture.
4. Pour in the remaining 1/2 cup warm water to the flour mixture. Using your hands, draw in the flour from the sides and work the mixture into a dough.
5. Knead for 3-5 minutes, until you reach a soft, smooth dough. The dough gets sticky as you knead, so pour in the remaining 1 tablespoon. olive oil and stir in an additional 2 tablespoons. flour to help shape into a soft dough.
6. Place the dough in a large bowl and cover with cling film. Leave it in a warm place for 1 hour; it will be doubled in size.
7. In the meantime, prepare your filling. Heat 15 millilitres/1 tablespoon. olive oil in a wide heavy pan and stir in the onions, garlic and peppers. Sauté for 2-3 minutes over medium heat, until they start to soften.
8. Stir in the tomatoes and sauté for another 2 minutes. Pour in the lemon juice and season with salt and ground black pepper. Turn the heat off.
9. Place the ground meat, spices, tomato paste and fresh herbs in a bowl and combine with the cooked vegetables. Mix well. The topping is ready.
10. Once the dough is risen, place it on a lightly floured surface. Knead the dough for a minute, then divide into two pieces and roll into two balls.
11. On a lightly floured surface, roll the dough balls into 2 oval shapes of 20 centimetres x 40 centimetres, with 1/2 centimetre (0.2") thickness. Line a large baking tray with baking paper and place the 2 oval flat bread doughs on the tray.
12. Spread the filling evenly over the 2 flat breads, leaving 2 centimetres at the edges as a border with no filling. Fold in the sides to act as a border to keep the filling intact. Squeeze the oval dough at each end to make it pointy.
13. Beat an egg in a small bowl and mix it with 1 tablespoon. olive oil. Brush the edges of the dough with this mixture.
14. Bake for 25-30 minutes, until the pides are golden brown and crispy at the edges.
15. Once cool, cut into slices and serve.
