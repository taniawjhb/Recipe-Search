## Ingredients

- 60 g butter
- 2 cups basmati rice
- 2 tsp turmeric
- 2 bay leaves
- 6 green cardamom pods
- 8 cloves
- 1 tsp fennel seeds
- 2 tsp salt
- 3 cups boiling water

## Method

1. Melt the butter in a saucepan.
2. Add the spices and toast briefly.
3. Stir in the rice and salt until coated in the butter.
4. Add the boiling water.
5. Cover with a tight-fitting lid and simmer on the lowest heat for 15 minutes. Do not stir in the meantime.
