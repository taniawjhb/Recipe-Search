## Ingredients

- 4 green cayenne pepper chillies, deseeded and chopped
- 6 Thai green chillies, chopped
- 2 red shallots, chopped
- 2 lemongrass stems, trimmed and finely chopped (about 2.5-3 tablespoons)
- 2 tablespoons galangal, grated
- 5 cloves garlic, chopped
- 2 1/2 teaspoons dried shrimp paste
- 2 teaspoons lime zest, grated
- 3 makrut lime leaves
- 1 teaspoon ground coriander
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1/4 teaspoon white pepper
- 1/4 teaspoon turmeric
- 2 tablespoons chopped cilantro root and stem
- 1/4 cup coriander leaves, loosely packed
- 2-3 tablespoons water, as needed

## Method

1. Combine all the ingredients except the water in a powerful blender or food processor.
2. Blend to a fine paste, scraping down the sides as you go. Add the water as required to help it blend (you may not need any as the herbs and chillies have moisture).
3. Use to make Thai green curry.
