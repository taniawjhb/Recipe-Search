## Ingredients

- 1 cup cherry tomatoes (halved)
- 1 tablespoon chives (fresh, chopped)
- 1 1/3 cup avocado (chopped)
- 3 cups romaine hearts (chopped)
- 2 teaspoons parsley (chopped)
- 1 teaspoon dill (chopped)
- drizzle balsamic vinegar (to taste)
- drizzle extra virgin olive oil (to taste)
- 2 tablespoons red wine vinegar
- 6 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil

## Method

1. Start by cutting the tomatoes. Roughly chop the chives, parsley, and dill, and add everything to a bowl. Roughly chop the romaine lettuce and add to the bowl, toss gently and season with a little salt and pepper, then place on a serving platter.
2. Gently peel the avocados, cut into rough chunks and add these to the tomato and lettuce salad.
3. Whisk together the olive oil and vinegar, season with a little salt and pepper, and drizzle over the prepared salad.
