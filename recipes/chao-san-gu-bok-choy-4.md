## Ingredients

- 2 tablespoons (30 grams) dried shitake mushrooms, rinsed and soaked (soaking water retained)
- 1 tablespoon oil
- 3 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
- 230 grams enoki or shimeji mushrooms
- 80 grams portabellini mushrooms, washed, trimmed and sliced
- 2 tablespoons rice wine vinegar
- 3 tablespoons oyster sauce
- 1 tablespoon soy sauce
- 2 teaspoons sugar
- 4 tablespoons mushroom soaking water
- 2 tablespoons minced green onions (scallions)
- A few sprigs cilantro
- 300 grams baby bok choy (280 grams, bottoms trimmed and split in half lengthwise)
- 1 tablespoon vegetable oil
- 1 teaspoon salt

## Method

1. Soak the shitake in warm water, once tender remove, reserve soaking water. Cut larger mushrooms into 3 or 4 and leave small ones complete.
2. Heat the oil in a pan and fry the garlic slivers until lightly golden, now add the soaked shitake and stir fry for 1 minute.
3. Now add the remaining mushrooms and continue to fry.
4. Add the rice wine, soy sauce and oyster sauce, sugar and 4 tablespoons soaking water.
5. Reduce the heat and cook for about 8 minutes, stirring constantly, until the mushrooms are cooked and most of the liquid is gone. While this is happening prepare your bok choy.
6. Wash your cut bok choy in a large bowl of water so the vegetables are fully submerged. Agitating or stirring the vegetables in the water will release any dirt or sand. Repeat this process another 2 times or as necessary, until the water is clean and there is no sand or dirt at the bottom of the bowl.
7. Bring 6 cups of water, 1 tablespoon vegetable oil, and 1 teaspoon salt to a boil. Blanch the bok choy for 40 seconds, stirring occasionally. They should be just wilted and bright green.
8. Scoop out the bok choy into a bowl and pour 2 cups of cool water over them. The water will stop the cooking process, but you don't want your veggies to be ice cold, so pour the bok choy into a colander right after they've been submerged in the cool water. Drain.
9. Arrange the bok choy into a circle around a large serving plate, with a space in the middle for the mushrooms.
10. Serve the mushrooms onto the same platter, drizzle lightly with sesame oil and garnish with chopped scallions and coriander.
