## Ingredients

### Cooked pears

- 2 large pears cored and sliced lengthwise
- 3 tablespoons olive oil divided
- 1/4 cup honey
- 3 tablespoons freshly squeezed lemon juice

### Salad ingredients

- 5 ounces baby spinach
- 1 cup pecan halves toasted, chopped
- 150 grams goat cheese

## Method

1. Core the pears and slice each one length-wise. You don't need to peel them.
2. Heat 2 tablespoons of olive oil in a large skillet on medium-high heat.
3. Add sliced pears and cook them for about 3 minutes per side or until soft and pliable. Remove from heat.
4. Add honey, lemon juice, and another tablespoon of olive oil and stir everything together.
5. Toast your pecan halves in a pan and set aside to cool.
6. Combine all the salad ingredients (except pecans) in a large serving bowl, including cooked pears and all the juices from the skillet, and toss to combine. Then, top the salad with chopped toasted pecan halves.
