## Ingredients

### For the sauce

- 2 tablespoons ghee (clarified butter)
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tablespoon ground cumin
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 teaspoon ground ginger
- 1 teaspoon Kashmiri chilli powder
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1/4 teaspoon ground turmeric
- 1 (14 ounces) can tomato sauce
- 1 cup heavy whipping cream
- 2 teaspoons paprika
- 1 tablespoon white sugar

### For the chicken

- 1 tablespoon vegetable oil
- 1 clove garlic, crushed
- 1 teaspoon crushed ginger
- 500 grams deboned, skinless chicken thighs, cut into bite-size pieces
- 1 teaspoon curry masala powder
- 1/2 teaspoon salt, or to taste
- 1 teaspoon white sugar, or to taste

## Method

1. Heat the ghee in a large skillet over medium heat, add the onion and cook about 5 minutes.
2. Stir in the garlic and cook until fragrant, about 1 minute.
3. Stir in the cumin, 1 teaspoon salt, ginger, chilli, cinnamon and turmeric; fry until fragrant, about 2 minutes.
4. Stir in the tomato sauce, bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low and simmer 10 minutes.
5. Mix in the cream, paprika and 1 tablespoon sugar. Return to a simmer and cook, stirring often, until thickened, 10–15 minutes.
6. Meanwhile, make a paste from the crushed garlic, ginger, masala powder, salt, sugar and a few tbsp of oil. Coat the chicken and thread onto skewers.
7. Heat a grill/braai to high and cook the chicken until slightly charred (it need not be cooked through).
8. Add the charred chicken to the curry and cook a further 20–30 minutes until done.
