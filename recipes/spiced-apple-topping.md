## Ingredients

- 2 medium apples - peeled and sliced
- 3/4 cup brown sugar
- 4 tablespoons butter
- 1/2 teaspoon vanilla - divided
- 1 teaspoon cinnamon - divided

## Method

1. Peel and slice the apples then add to a medium sized pan.
2. Now add the brown sugar, and butter.
3. Now cook over medium high heat until brown sugar has dissolved and butter has melted. Add 1/4 teaspoon vanilla and 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon and reduce heat to simmer.
4. Cook 6-8 minutes longer. Remove from heat and allow to cool slightly and thicken while you prepare the french toast.
