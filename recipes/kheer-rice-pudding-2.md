## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup basmati rice
- 2 cups milk
- 3 tablespoons sugar
- 1/4 cup raisins
- 2 tablespoons roughly chopped almonds
- 1/2 teaspoon cardamom powder
- 1/2 teaspoon fennel powder
- water

## Method

1. Soak the rice in water for 1 hour.
2. Bring the milk, sugar, cardamom and fennel powder to a boil.
3. Add the rice and cook over low heat with occasional stirring.
4. Simmer for about 15-20 minutes until the rice is tender.
5. Add the raisins and chopped almonds (you can add some more milk if needed).
6. Allow to simmer for another 5 minutes.
