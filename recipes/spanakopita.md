## Ingredients

- 300 grams spinach leaves, trimmed from thick stems, thoroughly washed, dried, then chopped into 2.5 centimetres pieces
- 1/2 teaspoon salt

Filling

- 175 grams Greek feta, crumbled
- 1/2 cup Greek yoghurt
- 2 green onions / scallions, finely sliced
- 1 egg
- 2 tablespoons mint, finely chopped
- 1 tablespoon dill, finely chopped
- 1/2 teaspoon lemon zest
- 1/2 tablespoon lemon juice
- 2 garlic cloves, finely chopped
- 1/2 teaspoon nutmeg, freshly grated
- 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon cayenne pepper

Filo pastry

- 16 sheets filo pastry
- 120 grams butter, melted
- 60 grams finely grated parmesan
- 1/2 teaspoon white sesame seeds (or more black)
- 1/2 teaspoon black sesame seeds (or more white)

## Method

1. Preheat oven to 220°C. Line a tray with baking paper / parchment paper.
2. Place spinach in a large colander or bowl. Sprinkle with salt, toss through. Leave for 10 minutes to sweat.
3. Place a handful of spinach in a tea towel, then wring out tightly to remove excess water.
4. Place spinach in a bowl with remaining filling ingredients. Mix well to combine.
5. Assembly: Cut 16 sheets of filo pastry into 32 x 25 centimetres.
6. Layer 8 sheets of filo pastry on the tray, brushing each layer with melted butter.
7. Spread filling on filo pastry. Smooth surface and leave a 2.5 centimetres border.
8. Cover spinach with a sheet of filo pastry. Brush with butter, sprinkle with Parmesan. Repeat for sheets #2 to #5. Then cover with filo sheets #6 to #8, brushing with butter in between, but do NOT sprinkle with cheese.
9. Press edges down to seal. Crimp and trim if desired or just leave the edges flat on the tray.
10. Brush the top with butter, sprinkle with sesame seeds.
11. Bake 25 minutes, or until golden brown on the surface.
12. Serve immediately! It is at its absolute prime fresh out of the oven, though still good as long as it's hot. The base does lose crispiness as it cools.
