## Ingredients

- 30g flat leaf parsley
- 5 large cloves garlic
- 1 tsp dry oregano
- 150ml olive oil
- 1/2 tsp dry chili flakes
- 35ml red wine vinegar
- 1 tsp sea salt
- 1 tsp freshly ground black pepper

## Method

1. Bring a small pan of water to the boil and have an ice bath ready. Blanch the parsley for 1 minute, then drain immediately and plunge into icy water. Drain and pat dry.
2. Place all the ingredients into a food processor and pulse.
3. Serve immediately or refrigerate in a sealed container for 3-4 days.
