## Ingredients

- 1 tablespoon fine sea salt
- 160 grams fine green beans
- 1/4 cup extra virgin olive oil
- 1 tablespoon red wine vinegar
- 2 tablespoons finely sliced mint leaves
- 1 clove garlic, finely sliced
- Freshly ground salt and black pepper

## Method

1. Bring 2 litres of water to the boil in a large saucepan, then stir in the fine salt.
2. Add the beans, return to the boil, cook for 3-4 minutes until just tender. Drain beans and plunge into ice water.
3. When the beans are cool, drain well and pat dry.
4. Toss with mint, oil, vinegar, garlic, salt and pepper and serve at room temperature.
