## Ingredients

For the kofta:

- 3 potatoes, boiled & mashed
- 3/4 cup paneer / cottage cheese, grated
- 1 chilli, finely chopped
- 2 tablespoons coriander, finely chopped
- 1/4 teaspoon cumin powder
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 2 tablespoons raisins
- 2 tablespoons cashew / kaju, chopped
- 2 tablespoons maida / plain flour
- oil, for frying

For the onion tomato puree:

- 2 tablespoons oil
- 1 onion, sliced
- 1 teaspoon ginger garlic paste
- 2 tomatoes, sliced
- 2 tablespoons cashew / kaju

For the curry:

- 1 tablespoon butter
- 2 tablespoons oil
- 1 teaspoon cumin / jeera
- 2 pods cardamom
- 1 bay leaf
- 1 inch cinnamon
- 2 cloves
- 1 teaspoon kashmiri red chilli powder
- 1/2 teaspoon turmeric
- 3/4 teaspoon coriander powder
- 1/4 teaspoon cumin powder
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 cup cream / malai
- 1/2 cup water
- 1 teaspoon kasuri methi, crushed
- 1/4 teaspoon garam masala

## Method

1. In a large mixing bowl take 3 potatoes and 3/4 cup paneer.
2. Add 1 chilli, 2 tablespoons fresh coriander, 1/4 teaspoon cumin powder and 1/2 teaspoon salt.
3. Add 2 tablespoons raisins and 2 tablespoons cashew to have a crunchy bite in the kofta.
4. Mix well making sure all the spices are well combined.
5. Now add 2 tablespoons flour and mix well forming a soft dough. Maida helps to absorb moisture and bind the mixture well.
6. Prepare small ball sized koftas by greasing your hands with oil.
7. Deep fry on medium hot oil.
8. Stir occasionally, making sure the koftas are cooked uniformly.
9. Fry until the koftas turn golden brown and crisp. Drain off the koftas and keep aside.
10. For the puree, in a pan heat 2 tablespoons oil and saute 1 onion and 1 teaspoon ginger garlic paste.
11. Saute until the onions change colour slightly.
12. Now add 2 tomatoes and saute slightly.
13. Now add 2 tablespoons cashew and continue to saute until the tomatoes soften completely.
14. Cool completely and transfer to a blender.
15. Blend to a smooth paste adding water if required.
16. Now sieve the mixture to get rid of skin and seeds. Filter until a silky smooth onion-tomato puree is attained. Keep aside.
17. For the curry, in a large pan heat 1 tablespoon butter and 2 tablespoons oil.
18. Saute 1 teaspoon cumin, 2 pods cardamom, 1 bay leaf, 1 inch cinnamon and 2 cloves until it turns aromatic.
19. Now with the flame on low, add 1 teaspoon chilli powder, 1/2 teaspoon turmeric, 3/4 teaspoon coriander powder and 1/4 teaspoon cumin powder.
20. Saute until the spices turn aromatic.
21. Further add in the prepared onion tomato puree, 1 teaspoon salt and mix well.
22. Cover and cook until the mixture starts to thicken and oil separates from the sides.
23. Now add 1/4 cup cream and mix on low flame until it is well combined.
24. Now add 1/2 cup water and mix well adjusting the consistency as required.
25. Get the curry to a boil, add 1 teaspoon kasuri methi and 1/4 teaspoon garam masala. Mix well.
26. Finally, pour the curry over the kofta and malai kofta is ready to enjoy.
