## Ingredients

- 1 globe eggplant
- 1 ½ tablespoons olive oil
- 1 tablespoon zaatar spice mix
- 1 tablespoon Ras el Hanout
- ¼ teaspoon salt, more to taste
- 1 fat garlic clove
- ½ cup tahini paste (liquids and solids both, not just the oil)
- ¼ cup warm tap water, more to desired consistency
- 1–2 tablespoons lemon juice (or to taste)
- 2 garlic cloves (finely minced)
- ½ teaspoon kosher salt (see notes)
- ¼ teaspoon pepper

## Method

1. Preheat oven to 200C.
2. Slice eggplant in half, then slice deeply at a diagonal at one inch intervals, "crosshatching", careful not to cut through the skin.
3. Season each side with ⅛ teaspoon kosher salt, sprinkling it into the slices if possible.
4. Mix oil, spices and garlic together in a bowl to make a paste. Brush or spoon the entire contents over the eggplant and place on a sheet pan in the oven.
5. Bake for 1 hour, rotating halfway through. After an hour, pierce with a fork; if they are very tender and juicy they are done. Bigger eggplants may take longer.
6. Make the tahini sauce in a medium bowl, starting by adding the tahini. If your tahini paste has separated, try to mix it in the jar with a fork before measuring.
7. Whisk the tahini with the warm water, starting with ¼ cup and adding more to desired consistency (½ cup for a looser dressing, or keep it thick). It will thicken as you whisk.
8. Add the remaining ingredients, lemon juice, minced garlic, salt and pepper, and whisk until creamy and smooth.
9. Taste and adjust salt and lemon to your liking, then spoon over the roasted aubergines.

## Notes

Different brands of tahini paste are thicker than others; the sauce will thicken further as it cools in the fridge.
