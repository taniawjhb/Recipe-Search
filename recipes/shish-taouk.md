## Ingredients

- 1 cup whole-milk Greek yogurt
- 2 tablespoons freshly squeezed lemon juice, or more to taste
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 6 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tablespoon red pepper flakes
- 1/2 tablespoon salt
- 1 1/2 teaspoons ground cumin
- 1 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
- 1 teaspoon paprika
- 1/8 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1200 gms boneless, skinless chicken thighs, halved
- 4 long metal skewers

## Method

1. Whisk the yogurt, lemon juice, olive oil, tomato paste, garlic, red pepper flakes, salt, cumin, black pepper, paprika and cinnamon together in a bowl.
2. Place the chicken thigh halves into the yogurt marinade and coat thoroughly on all sides. Cover the bowl with plastic wrap and refrigerate 2 to 8 hours.
3. Preheat an outdoor grill for medium-high heat and lightly oil the grate. Using 2 skewers for each kebab, thread half of the chicken thighs onto each pair of skewers, making a fairly thick "log" shape.
4. Place the kebabs on the grill. Do not try to turn them until they begin to unstick from the grill, 3 or 4 minutes. Turn the kebabs and grill the other side 3 or 4 minutes; turn. Continue cooking and turning until the chicken is no longer pink in the center and the juices run clear, about 6 minutes.
