## Ingredients

### Salad

- 350 grams cooked chicken, cut into thin batons
- 6 heaped cups wombok cabbage (Napa cabbage), finely shredded
- 1/2 red onion, very finely sliced
- 1 red pepper, finely sliced into thin batons
- 2 cucumbers, deseeded then finely sliced into half moons (or 1 long continental/English cucumber)
- 1 large carrot, peeled then julienned
- 1 large chilli, deseeded then julienned
- 1 cup (tightly packed) mint leaves, large leaves roughly torn by hand
- 1 cup (tightly packed) coriander leaves

### Dressing

- 2 tablespoons lime juice
- 2 tablespoons rice vinegar
- 1/4 cup fish sauce (substitute light or all-purpose soy sauce)
- 1/4 cup vegetable oil
- 1 tablespoon white sugar
- 1 large garlic clove, very finely minced
- 2 teaspoons red chilli, deseeded then very finely minced

### Finishing

- 1/2 cup peanuts, roasted unsalted, finely chopped

## Method

1. Dressing: Shake the dressing ingredients in a jar. Set aside 10 minutes to let the flavours meld.
2. Toss: Place all the salad ingredients in a very large bowl. Pour over half the dressing and toss well. Set aside 5 minutes.
3. Toss again: Just before serving, toss again then add most of the remaining dressing. Taste then add more dressing if you want.
4. Serve sprinkled with lots of peanuts.
