## Ingredients

- 400 grams black beans, drained
- 2 large handfuls baby spinach leaves, roughly chopped
- 500 grams ripe tomatoes, chopped into large chunks
- 1/2 cucumber, halved lengthways, seeds scooped out and sliced on an angle
- 1 large red onion, halved and finely sliced
- 6-8 radishes, sliced
- 2 avocados, peeled and sliced
- 100 grams feta, crumbled
- Handful of herbs (reserved from the dressing)
- Large bunch mint
- Small bunch coriander
- Small bunch basil
- 1 fat green chilli, deseeded and chopped
- 1 small garlic clove
- 100 millilitres extra virgin olive oil
- 2 limes, zested and juiced
- 2 tablespoons white wine vinegar
- 2 teaspoons honey

## Method

1. Make the dressing by blending all of the dressing ingredients (mint, coriander, basil, chilli, garlic, olive oil, lime zest and juice, white wine vinegar and honey) in a food processor (or very finely chop them), saving a few herb leaves for the salad. You can make the dressing up to 24 hours before serving.
2. Scatter the beans and spinach over a large platter. Arrange the tomatoes, cucumber, onion and radishes on top and gently toss together with your hands.
3. Top the salad with the avocados, feta and herbs, and serve the dressing on the side.
