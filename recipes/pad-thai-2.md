## Ingredients

- 1 cup dried noodles, soaked in warm water until softened
- 1/2 cup sliced tofu
- 1/2 cup bean sprouts (half for cooking, half for garnish)
- 1/4 cup shredded carrot
- 6 sticks of chive, cut into 1 centimetre pieces
- 1/2 cup cubed tofu
- 1 egg, beaten
- 2 cloves garlic, finely chopped
- 1 tablespoon fish sauce
- 3 tablespoons oyster sauce
- 2 tablespoons tamarind juice (use tamarind paste and dilute with water as needed)
- 1 teaspoon sugar
- 1/4 cup chicken stock
- Coconut/sunflower oil for frying
- 1 tablespoon roasted peanuts for serving

## Method

1. First, mix fish sauce, oyster sauce, tamarind juice, sugar and chicken stock in a bowl. Set aside.
2. Heat garlic and oil in a frying pan on low heat until aromatic and garlic starts softening.
3. Then turn the heat up and add tofu, frying for about 2 minutes and making sure not to crumble the tofu.
4. Add bean sprouts, carrot and chives and egg. Stir well on medium heat.
5. Next, add the noodles and the seasoning mixture and cook till the noodles are cooked.
6. Serve with some crushed peanuts, and a wedge of lime to squeeze over the finished Pad Thai.
