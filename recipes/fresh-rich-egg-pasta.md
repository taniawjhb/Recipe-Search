## Ingredients

- 400g 00 flour
- 60g egg yolk
- 3 whole eggs
- 2 tbsp olive oil

## Method

1. Start by calculating and measuring your eggs. We require a 60% hydration rate, so 60g of egg for every 100g of flour. This hydration ratio is made up of 3 whole eggs or 180g of eggs; to these add additional egg yolks to take the total weight to 240g. Once the eggs are measured, whisk lightly and set aside.
2. Tip the flour onto the counter. Make a well in the middle of the flour and add the eggs and the olive oil. Using a fork, beat together the eggs and oil and begin to incorporate the flour, starting with the inner edge.
3. Once about half the flour is incorporated, start kneading with both hands, using palms. When the dough comes together in a cohesive mass, transfer it to a lightly floured work surface and continue kneading for about 5 minutes. The dough should be elastic and a little sticky. Allow to rest for at least 30 minutes at room temperature before using.
