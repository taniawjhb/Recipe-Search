## Ingredients

### For the crust

- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- ½ cup granulated sugar
- ½ teaspoon salt
- 1 cup (2 sticks) butter, chilled and cut into small cubes

### For the filling

- 240g cream cheese, softened
- 2 cups granulated sugar
- ¼ cup flour
- ¾ cup fresh-squeezed lemon juice
- ⅓ cup powdered sugar, plus more for topping
- 1 cup blueberries
- 6 large eggs
- 1 teaspoon vanilla

## Method

1. Preheat oven to 350°F and line a 9x13 inch pan with foil or parchment, leaving plenty of overhang around the edges to lift the bars out before cutting.
2. Prepare the crust by combining flour, sugar, salt and butter cubes in a food processor or blender. Cover and pulse until fine crumbs form. (You can also do this with a pastry cutter, fork, or your fingers in a large bowl.)
3. Press the crust into the prepared pan and bake for 10 minutes while you prepare the filling.
4. Prepare the filling by combining cream cheese and sugar in a large bowl. Cream together until smooth. Mix in flour, powdered sugar, lemon juice, eggs and vanilla until completely incorporated. Stir in the blueberries.
5. Pour the filling over the crust and return to the oven for 30 minutes.
6. Allow to cool for about 20 minutes, then cover and chill in the fridge for 4 hours or overnight. Lift the foil edges to remove the bars from the pan, slice into squares, sprinkle with additional powdered sugar and serve.
