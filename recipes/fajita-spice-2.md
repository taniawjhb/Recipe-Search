## Ingredients

- 3 tablespoons cornstarch
- 6 teaspoons chili powder
- 3 teaspoons salt
- 3 teaspoons paprika
- 3 teaspoons white sugar
- 1 1/2 teaspoons onion powder
- 1 teaspoon cayenne pepper
- 1 1/2 teaspoons ground cumin

## Method

1. Combine the cornstarch, chili powder, salt, paprika, sugar, onion powder, cayenne pepper, and cumin together in a small bowl.
