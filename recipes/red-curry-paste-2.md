## Ingredients

- 3 dried red chillies
- 3 small red chillies
- 1 slice galangal/ginger (or both)
- 2/3 of a stalk of lemongrass
- peel of 2 limes (remove and use the peel only, avoid the bitter white pith)
- 1.5 shallots/small red onions
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1 slice of turmeric / 1 teaspoon dry turmeric
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon shrimp paste

## Method

1. Start by finely chopping all the ingredients, as much as possible.
2. Then add them to a heavy stone mortar and grind until a smooth paste is formed and all the aromas have been released.

## Notes

Fiery and flavourful — the basis of many great dishes. Curry pastes can be stored in the fridge in a clean container for 7-10 days, but they are best fresh.
