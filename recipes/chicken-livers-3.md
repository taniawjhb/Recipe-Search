## Ingredients

- 250 grams chicken livers
- Seasoned flour for dusting (salt, pepper, paprika)
- 1/2 onion, diced
- 2 cloves garlic
- 3 sprigs of thyme
- Dry red chili
- 75 millilitres white wine
- 1 tablespoon tomato paste
- 100 millilitres cream
- 2 tablespoons fresh chopped parsley
- 3 tablespoons olive oil
- Salt & pepper

## Method

1. Rinse and clean the livers, then pat dry.
2. Lightly dust the livers in the seasoned flour (1 tablespoon flour; 1 teaspoon paprika, salt & pepper to taste).
3. Add olive oil to the pan, and brown livers on med-high heat, till edges are crisped but liver still rare.
4. Remove livers and set aside, then add onion and minced garlic to the pan. Cook till softened over medium heat. Now add the tomato paste and cook for a further minute or two until the tomato paste begins to darken.
5. Now add the white wine to the pan, cook over medium-high heat until wine begins to reduce.
6. Add your cream and sprinkling of thyme, now add the livers back to the pan and cook until the sauce thickens and the livers are just cooked.
7. Check and adjust seasoning and sprinkle with parsley.
8. Serve immediately with some sliced baguette.
