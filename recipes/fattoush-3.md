## Ingredients

### Salad

- 2 loaves pita bread
- Extra virgin olive oil
- 1/2 teaspoon sumac, more for later
- Salt and pepper
- 1 heart of romaine lettuce, chopped
- 1 English cucumber, chopped
- 5 Roma tomatoes, chopped
- 5 green onions (both white and green parts), chopped
- 5 radishes, stems removed, thinly sliced
- 2 cups chopped fresh parsley leaves, stems removed
- 1 cup chopped fresh mint leaves (optional)

### Dressing

- 1 1/2 limes, juice of
- 1/3 cup extra virgin olive oil
- Salt and pepper
- 1 teaspoon ground sumac
- 1/4 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- Scant 1/4 teaspoon ground allspice

## Method

1. Prepare the pita chips: Toast the pita bread until crisp but not browned. Break into pieces and fry in olive oil, tossing until golden. Season with salt, pepper and sumac, then drain on paper towels.
2. Mix the salad: In a large bowl, combine the chopped lettuce, cucumber, tomatoes, green onions, radish and parsley.
3. Make the vinaigrette: Whisk together the lime juice, olive oil and spices in a small bowl.
4. Assemble and serve: Dress the salad lightly, toss, then mix in the pita chips and extra sumac, if desired. Divide into serving bowls and enjoy.
