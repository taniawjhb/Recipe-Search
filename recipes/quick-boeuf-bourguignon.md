## Ingredients

- 900g chuck steak, cut into 2-3cm pieces
- 1 cup dry red wine
- 1/2 cup beef stock
- 2 carrots, sliced 1/2cm thick
- 3 slices of bacon
- 225g fresh mushrooms, quartered
- 2 tbsp flour
- 12 small pearl onions or 1 large onion, chopped
- 1/4 tsp basil
- 2 cloves garlic, minced

## Method

1. Slice the bacon into 1cm pieces and fry in the cooker. Add the onion and cook for 2 minutes.
2. Add the steak and brown the meat for 5 minutes, being careful not to overcrowd the pressure cooker.
3. Stir in the flour and blend thoroughly. Stir in the wine, beef stock and seasonings. Close the lid.
4. Cook for 25 minutes, then remove the lid.
5. Add the carrots and mushrooms and simmer with the lid on until done, about 15-20 minutes. If needed, add a little stock if the gravy is too thick.
