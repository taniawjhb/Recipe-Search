## Ingredients

- 3/4 cup butter, cubed
- 1 1/2 cups packed light brown sugar
- 2 large eggs, room temperature
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 2 1/4 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 1/2 teaspoons baking powder
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- Zest of 2 lemons
- Juice of 1 lemon
- 180 grams white chocolate, coarsely chopped
- 1 tablespoon grated lemon zest, optional
- 150 grams icing sugar
- 3 tablespoons lemon juice

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 175°C. In a small pot, melt the butter and lemon zest, remove from the heat; stir in the brown sugar. Cool slightly.
2. Beat in the eggs one at a time, then the lemon juice and vanilla; stir to combine well.
3. In another bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder and salt; stir into the butter mixture. Stir in the chopped chocolate (the batter will be thick). Spread into a lined and greased 20x32 centimetre pan.
4. Bake until golden brown and a toothpick inserted in the centre comes out clean (do not overbake), 18-21 minutes. Cool for 10-15 minutes in the tin then add the glaze.
5. In a small bowl combine the lemon zest, lemon juice and icing sugar, whisk for 2 minutes until thoroughly combined, then cover the top of the still warm blondies.
6. Allow to cool completely and then remove and cut into cubes.
