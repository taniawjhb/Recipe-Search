## Ingredients

- 500 grams granulated sugar
- 300 grams water
- 1 stick cinnamon
- 50 grams glucose
- 6 cloves
- peel of 1 lemon
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice
- 400 grams butter
- 150 grams walnuts
- 150 grams almonds
- 1 teaspoon cinnamon
- 1/2 teaspoon cloves, ground
- 900 grams phyllo dough sheet
- pistachios, ground, to serve

## Method

1. In a pot, add the sugar, water, cinnamon stick, glucose syrup, cloves and lemon rind. Place pot over medium heat.
2. In about 2 to 3 minutes, as soon as the sugar melts, remove pot from heat. Add the lemon juice, stir and set aside to cool.
3. Melt the butter in a saucepan over low heat.
4. In a food processor or using a mortar and pestle, grind the walnuts, almonds, cinnamon and cloves together. You don't want to finely grind them but you don't want to have large pieces because they will rip through the phyllo dough. Transfer the filling to a bowl and the melted butter to a separate bowl.
5. Preheat the oven to 160°C (fan).
6. Spread out the phyllo dough on a clean working surface. Lay one sheet separately on your working surface. Drizzle with melted butter, making sure not to let the brush directly touch the phyllo. Cover with another sheet of phyllo and drizzle with butter. Spread the filling over the entire surface.
7. Place two wooden sticks in the middle of the phyllo and roll the smaller side towards the middle, leaving an empty space at the edge. Do not roll too tight and gently press the ends together towards the middle with your hands, creating folds in the phyllo and remove the sticks.
8. Brush a 30x40 centimetre baking pan with butter and add the saragli. Drizzle with butter immediately and repeat the same process until the baking pan is full of saragli rolls. When the pan is full, drizzle any leftover butter over the rolls.
9. Bake for 70 to 90 minutes.
10. When ready, remove from oven and immediately pour the cool syrup over the scorching hot saragli rolls. Allow them to soak up the syrup for 30 minutes.
11. Sprinkle with ground pistachio nuts and serve.
