## Ingredients

- 250 millilitres water
- 1 tablespoon granulated sugar
- 2.5 millilitres salt
- 175 grams cake flour
- 1 large egg, beaten
- oil for deep frying

## Method

1. Bring the water, salt and sugar to the boil. Remove from the heat and beat in the flour until you have a smooth paste. Now add the well beaten egg and mix until the mixture is smooth and satiny.
2. Spoon the mixture into a piping bag with a large star nozzle fitted.
3. Heat the oil in a frying pan or saucepan; the oil should be deep enough that the churros can float. Pipe 7cm strips of the batter into the hot oil a few at a time, cook for 3-4 minutes or until golden brown.
4. Drain the churros on kitchen towel before dusting with a mixture of caster sugar and fresh ground cinnamon.
