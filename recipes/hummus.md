## Ingredients

- 1 can chick peas
- 2 tbsp tahini
- 4 cloves garlic
- 2 tsp ground cumin
- 1/4 cup lemon juice
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- Large pinch cayenne pepper
- Extra lemon juice, olive oil, paprika and flat leaf to garnish

## Method

1. Place all the ingredients into a food processor and process until smooth.
2. With the motor running, slowly add vegetable stock until the desired consistency is achieved.
3. Check and adjust seasoning.
4. Serve in a bowl, topped with a sprinkle of paprika, a drizzle of olive oil and a splash of lemon juice.
