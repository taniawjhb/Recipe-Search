## Ingredients

- 200 millilitres double cream
- 700 millilitres whole milk
- 6 large egg yolks
- 4 tablespoons cornflour
- 50 grams caster sugar
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract / vanilla paste

## Method

1. Put the cream and milk into a large pan and gently bring to just below boiling point. Meanwhile, in a large bowl, whisk the yolks, cornflour, sugar and vanilla. Gradually pour the hot milk mixture onto the sugar mixture, whisking constantly.
2. Wipe out the saucepan and pour the mixture back into it. Heat gently, stirring with a wooden spoon until the custard is thickened, work quickly to ensure that no lumps form. Eat hot or cold.
