## Ingredients

- 3 large ripe bananas
- 3/4 cup sugar
- 1 egg, slightly beaten
- 1/3 cup melted butter
- 1 cup blueberries
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1 1/2 cups flour

## Method

1. Mash bananas in a large mixing bowl until smooth. Add the sugar and egg. Now add the butter and blueberries.
2. In a separate bowl combine dry ingredients and then gently stir into banana mixture.
3. Pour into 12 well-greased muffin cups. Bake at 180°C degrees for 20 minutes.
