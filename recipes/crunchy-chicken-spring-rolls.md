## Ingredients

- 1 tablespoon soy sauce
- 1 tablespoon rice wine or white wine
- 1 pinch freshly ground black pepper
- 1 teaspoon cornstarch
- 500 grams ground chicken
- 2 tablespoons vegetable oil, divided
- 2 cloves garlic, finely minced
- 1 teaspoon fresh ginger, grated
- 1 stalk green onion, chopped
- 1/2 small cabbage, shredded
- 2 carrots, shredded
- 2 tablespoons oyster sauce
- 1 tablespoon cornstarch
- 60 millilitres water
- 50 spring roll wrappers, defrosted
- Vegetable oil for frying

## Method

1. In a large bowl, mix the soy sauce, wine, pepper and 1 teaspoon cornstarch. Add the chicken mince, combine well and marinate for 10 minutes.
2. Heat a wok over high heat. Add 1 tablespoon oil and stir-fry the chicken until browned. Remove and set aside.
3. Wipe out the wok and reduce the heat to medium. Add the remaining oil, then the spring onion, garlic and ginger. Stir for 30 seconds without browning.
4. Add the carrots and cabbage. Increase the heat to medium-high and stir-fry for about 2 minutes, until the carrots soften. Return the chicken to the wok, add the oyster sauce and mix well.
5. Spread the mixture on a baking sheet to cool. Tilt the tray so any liquid collects, then discard the liquid once cooled.
6. Whisk the cornstarch and water to make a slurry. Keep the spring roll wrappers covered with a damp towel. Place one wrapper in a diamond shape and add 1 tablespoon filling near the bottom corner.
7. Fold the bottom corner over the filling and roll tightly. Fold in the sides, then continue rolling. Brush the edge with slurry to seal. Place seam-side down and keep covered to prevent drying.
8. Heat oil to 175°C. Deep-fry the rolls for 2-3 minutes, turning until evenly golden. Drain on kitchen paper and serve warm.
