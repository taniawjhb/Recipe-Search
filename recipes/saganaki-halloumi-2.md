## Ingredients

- 500 grams halloumi cheese
- flour (for dredging)
- 1/2 cup olive oil (or less)
- 2 lemons, cut into wedges

## Method

1. Cut the cheese into strips 2 centimetres wide and 1 centimetre thick.
2. Place the strips one by one under the cold water tap and coat lightly with flour, set aside until you have dredged all the strips.
3. Heat the oil in a heavy skillet and pan fry the cheese, turn once until golden brown on both sides.
4. Remove, dab with paper towels and serve immediately with lemon wedges.
