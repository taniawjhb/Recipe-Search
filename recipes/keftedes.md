## Ingredients

- 500 grams ground beef
- 200 grams ground pork
- 1 large red onion, grated
- 1 clove of garlic, minced
- 1 cup breadcrumbs
- 1 egg
- 1/4 cup chopped fresh parsley
- 6-7 leaves fresh mint (chopped)
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1/2 teaspoon oregano
- salt and pepper
- oil for frying
- flour for dredging

## Method

1. Add all the ingredients into a large bowl and mix well (for about 5-10 minutes), squeezing with your hands, to allow the flavours to blend. (If the mixture isn't firm enough, add some more breadcrumbs). Cover the bowl with some plastic wrap and let it rest in the fridge for 1 hour.
2. Turn the mixture out of the fridge and roll into meatballs the size of a walnut (or bigger if you prefer). Dredge the rolled meatballs lightly in flour, making sure to shake off any excess. Fry until nicely browned on all sides.
3. Fry the keftedes in batches of 10-15 at a time, until nicely browned on all sides.

## Notes

Instead of frying you can bake/grill the keftedes in the oven. Preheat the oven to 200°C, place the keftedes on a greased baking sheet about an inch apart. Bake for about 20 minutes, turning the meatballs over midway through cooking time. The grilled version is much lighter than the fried.
