## Ingredients

### Dough

- 450 grams store-bought bread dough or ready-made wraps

### Topping

- 1/2 sweet red pepper, cored, cut into chunks
- 1 shallot, halved
- 2 garlic cloves, peeled
- 15 grams fresh parsley leaves with some stem
- 200 grams ground lamb or ground beef
- 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
- 1 teaspoon ground allspice
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1/2 teaspoon Aleppo-style pepper
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1/2 teaspoon cayenne pepper
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 4 tablespoons tomato paste
- 2 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
- Lemon wedges, for serving

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 230°C and position the rack in the middle.
2. Prepare the meat mixture: In a food processor, pulse the red peppers, shallot, garlic and parsley until finely chopped. Add the ground lamb (or beef), spices, salt, tomato paste and olive oil, then pulse again until well combined.
3. Prep the dough: Divide the bread dough into 4 equal balls. On a floured surface, roll each into a thin 23 centimetre disk.
4. Assemble and bake: Place a flatbread on a lined baking sheet, reshape if needed, then spread 3-4 tablespoons of topping evenly, leaving a slight border. Bake for 5-7 minutes, until the dough is crisp and the meat is cooked.
5. Finish and serve: Repeat with the remaining dough. Drizzle lemon juice over the hot lahmacun and serve warm or at room temperature.
