## Ingredients

- 600 grams beef mince
- 1 red onion, finely chopped
- 1 medium sized carrot, finely chopped
- 1-2 stalks celery, finely chopped
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 1 small chilli
- 1 sprig rosemary
- 1 bay leaf
- 1 can whole tomato
- 2 tablespoons tomato paste
- 250 millilitres beef stock
- Handful of fresh chopped parsley
- Olive oil
- Knob of butter
- Salt and pepper

## Method

1. Start by adding butter and olive oil to a heavy based pot on medium/high heat.
2. Add your finely chopped sofrito (and garlic/chilli) and cook till softened and fragrant.
3. Next, add your meat, bay leaf and sprig of rosemary, giving it a nice stir so that flavours are evenly distributed. At this stage, we want to brown the meat. In order to get nice caramelised meat, do not over stir. Allow a few minutes between stirring.
4. Once your meat is nicely browned, add your can of tomato, tomato paste and a can of water (use your tomato can as it still has tomato in it!).
5. Reduce your heat to medium, pop the lid on and allow to simmer for 30 minutes - stirring occasionally.
6. Check your seasoning.
7. Stir through fresh chopped parsley before serving with your favourite pasta. If your mixture starts looking too dry, add a little bit of liquid (water, stock, milk).
