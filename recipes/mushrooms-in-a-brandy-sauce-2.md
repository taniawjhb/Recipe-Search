## Ingredients

- 250 g Portabellini or wild mushrooms
- 1 clove garlic, finely minced
- 1 tbsp butter
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 60 ml marsala or sherry or brandy
- 1/4 tsp finely chopped red chili
- 1 tsp finely chopped thyme
- 1/2 cup cream
- 1 tsp black truffle paste (optional)

## Method

1. In a pan heat the olive oil and butter with the garlic and chili.
2. Cook for 1-2 minutes until garlic gets a touch of gold, now add the sliced mushrooms (brush and remove tough parts first).
3. Cook the mushrooms for a few minutes, adding a little extra olive oil if you need.
4. Once the mushrooms are cooked, add the marsala and burn off the alcohol.
5. Add the cream and reduce.
6. Now add the parsley and stir through before serving - add the truffle if using it.
