## Ingredients

- 225g chickpeas, cooked
- 2 onions, grated
- 5 tablespoons olive oil
- 6 cloves garlic, crushed
- 3 tomatoes, peeled, seeded, and crushed
- 1 carrot, peeled and finely grated
- 3 leaves green cabbage, finely chopped
- 3 potatoes, finely grated
- 1 tablespoon ground cumin
- 2 cubes vegetable bouillon
- 1 bunch cilantro, chopped
- Juice of a lemon
- 1L water

## Method

1. Boil the water and dissolve the two cubes of bouillon. Set aside.
2. In a large saucepan, heat the olive oil over medium heat.
3. Brown the onion and garlic for 3 minutes, stirring constantly.
4. Add the tomatoes, cabbage, potatoes, carrot and cumin. Season with salt and pepper.
5. Add the chickpeas and cilantro. Mix well and add the hot reserved broth.
6. Cook for 30 minutes over low heat.
7. Divide the soup in two, and purée one half with an immersion blender.
8. Return the purée to the pan, and stir well. Adjust the seasoning if necessary.
9. Serve the soup with a dash of lemon juice.
