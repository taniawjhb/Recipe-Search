## Ingredients

### For the Kofta

- 3 potato / aloo, boiled & mashed
- 3/4 cup paneer / cottage cheese, grated
- 1 chilli, finely chopped
- 2 tablespoons coriander, finely chopped
- 1/4 teaspoon cumin powder
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 2 tablespoons raisins
- 2 tablespoons cashew / kaju, chopped
- 2 tablespoons maida / plain flour
- oil, for frying

### For the Onion Tomato Puree

- 2 tablespoons oil
- 1 onion, sliced
- 1 teaspoon ginger garlic paste
- 2 tomato, sliced
- 2 tablespoons cashew / kaju

### For the Curry

- 1 tablespoon butter
- 2 tablespoons oil
- 1 teaspoon cumin / jeera
- 2 pod cardamom
- 1 bay leaf
- 1 inch cinnamon
- 2 clove
- 1 teaspoon kashmiri red chilli powder
- 1/2 teaspoon turmeric
- 3/4 teaspoon coriander powder
- 1/4 teaspoon cumin powder
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 cup cream / malai
- 1/2 cup water
- 1 teaspoon kasuri methi, crushed
- 1/4 teaspoon garam masala

## Method

1. In a large mixing bowl take 3 potato and 3/4 cup paneer.
2. Add 1 chilli, 2 tablespoons fresh coriander, 1/4 teaspoon cumin powder and 1/2 teaspoon salt.
3. Add 2 tablespoons raisins and 2 tablespoons cashew to have a crunchy bite in the kofta.
4. Mix well making sure all the spices are well combined.
5. Now add 2 tablespoons flour and mix well forming a soft dough. Maida helps to absorb moisture and bind the mixture well.
6. Prepare small ball sized koftas by greasing hands with oil.
7. Deep fry on medium hot oil, stirring occasionally so the koftas cook uniformly, until they turn golden brown and crisp. Drain off the koftas and keep aside.
8. For the puree, heat 2 tablespoons oil in a pan and sauté 1 onion and 1 teaspoon ginger garlic paste until the onions change colour slightly.
9. Now add 2 tomato and sauté slightly, then add 2 tablespoons cashew and continue to sauté until tomatoes soften completely.
10. Cool completely and transfer to a blender. Blend to a smooth paste adding water if required.
11. Sieve the mixture to get rid of skin and seeds, filtering until a silky smooth onion-tomato puree is attained. Keep aside.
12. For the curry, in a large pan heat 1 tablespoon butter and 2 tablespoons oil.
13. Sauté 1 teaspoon cumin, 2 pod cardamom, 1 bay leaf, 1 inch cinnamon and 2 clove until aromatic.
14. With the flame on low, add 1 teaspoon chilli powder, 1/2 teaspoon turmeric, 3/4 teaspoon coriander powder and 1/4 teaspoon cumin powder. Sauté until the spices turn aromatic.
15. Add the prepared onion tomato puree and 1 teaspoon salt and mix well. Cover and cook until the mixture starts to thicken and oil separates from the sides.
16. Add 1/4 cup cream and mix on low flame until well combined.
17. Add 1/2 cup water and mix well, adjusting consistency as required.
18. Bring the curry to a boil, add 1 teaspoon kasuri methi and 1/4 teaspoon garam masala and mix well.
19. Finally, pour the curry over the kofta and malai kofta is ready to enjoy.
