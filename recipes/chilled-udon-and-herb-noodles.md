## Ingredients

- 340 grams udon noodles
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 1 teaspoon ginger, grated
- 1 teaspoon coconut sugar or brown sugar
- 1/4 teaspoon crushed red pepper flakes
- Zest and juice of 1 lime
- 1 tablespoon rice vinegar
- 1 tablespoon sesame oil
- 1 cup thinly sliced cucumber
- 2 cups thinly sliced radish
- 1 cup corn kernels
- Salt and freshly ground pepper
- 2 scallions, chopped
- 1/4 cup cilantro leaves
- 1 teaspoon black sesame seeds

## Method

1. Boil the udon noodles according to the package directions. Drain and rinse with cold water and set aside.
2. Whisk together the garlic, ginger, sugar, red pepper flakes, lime zest, lime juice, rice vinegar and sesame oil in a small bowl.
3. Add the noodles, dressing, cucumber, radish, corn, scallions and cilantro to a large mixing bowl and toss to combine. Season to taste with salt and pepper and toss again. Transfer to bowls and top with sesame seeds. Chill for at least 1 hour before serving.
