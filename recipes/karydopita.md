## Ingredients

For the syrup:

- 500 millilitres water
- 300 grams sugar
- 5 millilitres freshly squeezed lemon juice
- 1 slice lemon
- 1 cinnamon stick

For the cake:

- 350 grams coarsely crushed walnuts
- 6 large eggs
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cloves (optional)
- 5 millilitres ground cinnamon
- 10 millilitres baking powder
- 300 millilitres milk
- 400 grams granulated white sugar
- 250 millilitres vegetable oil
- 375 grams all-purpose flour

## Method

1. Prepare your syrup by combining all of the ingredients in a small saucepan. Bring to a boil and when the sugar has dissolved, reduce the heat to medium. Allow to cook for approximately 5 minutes and then remove the syrup from the heat. Allow to cool. Note: this produces a cake that is not overly syrupy; however, if you do prefer a syrupy cake, please feel free to increase the quantities for the syrup above.
2. Preheat your oven to 180°C.
3. In a large bowl combine all of the cake ingredients. The order is not particularly important. Stir until very well combined with a rubber spatula or large plastic or wooden spoon. Mix very well until the entire batter is uniform.
4. Grease your baking pan (a 25 centimetres round cake pan) with vegetable oil, being sure to cover the bottom and sides.
5. Pour in your batter. Spread it around so that it is evenly distributed. Bake in the center of the oven for approximately 50-60 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the center of the cake comes out clean.
6. When your cake is done, remove it from the oven and gently poke holes in it, using a skewer or a sharp knife. Pour the cooled syrup all over the cake, about 1/2 cup (125 millilitres) at a time. The syrup will be absorbed by the cake.
7. Allow your cake to cool and serve it directly from your baking pan.
