## Ingredients

### Pâte Sucrée

- 220g flour, plain/all purpose
- 50g soft icing sugar / powdered sugar, sifted
- 15g almond meal / ground almonds
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 100g / 7 tbsp butter, unsalted, softened, cut into 1cm cubes
- 1 large egg, at room temperature

### Curd Filling

- 1 tbsp lemon zest (1 lemon's worth)
- 125ml lemon juice (from 1-2 lemons)
- 150g white sugar
- 170g unsalted butter, cut in 1cm cubes
- 3 whole large eggs
- 3 egg yolks (from large eggs)

## Method

1. **Pâte Sucrée:** Place all the dry ingredients in the bowl of a food processor. Add the cold butter and pulse until incorporated. Add the whole egg and run the machine until the dough starts to come together.
2. Gather the dough and wrap in clingwrap, shape into a 2cm thick disc, and place in the freezer for 10 minutes.
3. Remove the pastry from the freezer and on a lightly floured surface roll out until 3mm thick. Transfer the pastry to your baking tin and gently ease it into the sides; do not stretch the pastry as this will cause shrinking. Dock the pastry thoroughly, then place the pastry shell in the freezer for 10 minutes.
4. Remove from the freezer, add a sheet of baking paper and fill with baking beans. Place in the preheated oven at 200°C (180°C fan). Bake for 15 minutes then remove the beans and baking paper and bake for a further 10 minutes until the base is lightly golden. Remove and allow to cool completely before filling.
5. **Lemon filling:** Preheat the oven to 180°C. Put all the filling ingredients in a medium saucepan and whisk to combine.
6. Place the saucepan on the stove over low / medium-low heat. Whisk constantly, especially as the butter is melting, to ensure it doesn't split. Keep stirring until the mixture thickens enough to visibly mound on the surface when dolloped – about 5 minutes.
7. Strain into a bowl using a fine mesh strainer. Pour into the tart shell and smooth the filling surface using an offset spatula.
8. Bake for 5 minutes. It will still be a soft custard when you touch it but not liquid; it will set more when cooled so it's sliceable. Allow to cool completely before cutting to allow the tart to firm up.
