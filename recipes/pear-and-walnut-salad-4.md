## Ingredients

### Salad

- 200g mixed lettuce (arugula, romaine, etc.)
- 1 ripe pear - unpeeled, cored, sliced
- 55g walnuts, roughly chopped
- 55g Pecorino Romano cheese, shaved

### Vinaigrette

- 2 tablespoons white wine vinegar
- 1/2 teaspoon mustard
- 1/2 tsp finely minced garlic
- 6 tablespoons olive oil

## Method

1. To make the vinaigrette, combine vinegar, mustard and garlic. Whisk till well combined. While whisking constantly, slowly pour in a steady stream of the olive oil.
2. Prepare the remaining ingredients, toss the vinaigrette with them and serve.
