## Ingredients

### Steak

- 6 sirloin or ribeye steaks, 3 centimetres thick
- 1 1/2 tablespoons chopped rosemary
- 1 1/2 tablespoons chopped sage
- Zest of 1 lemon
- Coarse sea salt and freshly ground pepper
- Extra-virgin olive oil
- 1 pack of rocket
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice
- 1 cup shaved parmesan

### Chimichurri

- 30 grams flat leaf parsley
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1 teaspoon dried oregano
- 150 millilitres olive oil
- 1/2 teaspoon dried chilli flakes
- 35 millilitres red wine vinegar
- 1 teaspoon sea salt
- 1 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper

## Method

1. Start by finely chopping the rosemary and sage and add to a bowl.
2. Add a generous teaspoon of freshly crushed black pepper and sea salt.
3. Add the finely zested peel of 1 lemon.
4. Add a splash of olive oil to wet the mixture slightly and coat the steaks with it.
5. To cook the steaks, add a splash of oil to a frying pan and place over medium-high heat until almost smoking.
6. Add the steaks and cook until nicely browned, about 3 minutes per side for rare.
7. Remove the steaks, set aside and allow to stand under some foil.
8. In a separate bowl add the lemon juice and olive oil and mix to combine.
9. Arrange the rocket leaves on the platter, then slice the steaks into strips and arrange over the rocket.
10. Drizzle with the olive oil and lemon dressing before topping with the parmesan shavings.
11. For the chimichurri: Bring a small pan of water to the boil and have an ice bath ready. Blanch the parsley for 1 minute, then drain immediately and plunge into icy water. Drain and pat dry.
12. Place all the chimichurri ingredients into a food processor and pulse. Serve immediately or refrigerate in a sealed container for 3-4 days.
