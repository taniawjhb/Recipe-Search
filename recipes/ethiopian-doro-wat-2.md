## Ingredients

- 4 medium-sized red onions
- 1.6 kilograms chicken
- 3/4 to 1 cup vegetable oil or any good cooking oil of your choice
- 2 tablespoons kibbeh (Ethiopian spiced butter)
- 2 tablespoons tomato paste (optional)
- 6 eggs, hard-boiled
- 2 tablespoons minced garlic
- 2 tablespoons grated ginger
- 1/4 cup water
- salt to taste
- 1/2 cup berbere spice
- 1 cup vinegar and 2 cups water, to clean chicken

## Method

1. Clean the chicken very well and soak it in water and vinegar. Rinse and drain when ready to use.
2. Boil the eggs, peel, and set aside.
3. Finely dice the onions. Use a food processor to save time.
4. Add the onions to a pot over low to medium heat. The onions will sweat and create the water they will simmer in. Continue to stir the onions to prevent burning until they change colour (light pink), about 30 to 40 minutes. When they are done, the water will evaporate and the onions will begin to stick to the bottom of the pot.
5. Stir in the oil, minced garlic, salt and berbere spice. Stir well. Add the butter and the tomato paste and stir.
6. Slash the chicken in two or three places. This step is optional, but it will allow the flavours of the stew to penetrate the chicken. Add the chicken inside the stew and stir.
7. Add the water and cook on medium heat for 30 to 40 minutes, stirring occasionally to prevent burning. Continue to cook until the stew thickens up and the chicken becomes tender.
8. Stir in the eggs and leave to cook for another 15 to 20 minutes.
