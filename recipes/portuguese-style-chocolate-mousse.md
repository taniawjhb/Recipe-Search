## Ingredients

- 200g medium to dark chocolate
- 6 eggs, separated
- 2 tablespoons real vanilla extract
- 60g butter
- 90ml milk

## Method

1. In a double boiler, in a bowl, break the chocolate into pieces, add the milk and then slowly heat it while stirring occasionally, until the chocolate is melted.
2. Remove the bowl from the hot water, let the chocolate cool a little bit, then add the vanilla extract and the butter and stir them into the chocolate, stir until the butter is melted and the chocolate looks nice and glossy.
3. Whisk your egg yolks then add the mixture to the chocolate, stir until well combined.
4. Next, beat egg whites till stiff then gently fold them into the chocolate mixture until they are mixed well in.
5. Divide the mixture into pudding bowls, cover and put into the refrigerator overnight.
