## Ingredients

- 3 tablespoon cornstarch
- 6 teaspoons chili powder
- 3 teaspoon salt
- 3 teaspoon paprika
- 3 teaspoon white sugar
- 1 1/2 teaspoon onion powder
- 1 teaspoon cayenne pepper
- 1 1/2 teaspoon ground cumin

## Method

1. Combine cornstarch, chili powder, salt, paprika, sugar, onion powder, cayenne pepper, and cumin together in a small bowl.
