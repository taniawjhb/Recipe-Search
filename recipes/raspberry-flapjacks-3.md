## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 cups cake flour, less 2 tablespoons
- 4 tablespoons finely ground pistachios
- 2 teaspoons baking powder
- 1 cup raspberries
- 1/2 teaspoon bicarbonate
- 2 tablespoons sugar
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 2 tablespoons oil
- 2 large eggs
- 1 1/2 cups milk, plus a little extra if needed

## Method

1. Sieve together all the dry ingredients. In a small bowl whisk together the oil, eggs and milk. Add this mixture to the flour and without overworking it combine the two; if the batter is too heavy add a little extra milk. Now stir through the raspberries, reserving a few.
2. To cook the pancakes, heat a non stick frying pan and add a little butter to it. Once the pan is hot add enough batter to achieve the flapjack of the size and thickness you want, usually a 1/4 cup of batter gives a nice size. Make sure to lift the batter from the bottom as the raspberries will sink, ensuring each pancake gets a few raspberries.
3. Once the batter starts to bubble it is time to turn the flapjack. Allow it to cook through, remove and keep warm while you finish making the rest.
