## Ingredients

- 500 grams chicken breasts/deboned thighs, cut into bite-sized pieces
- Wood skewers (optional)
- 1 1/2 tablespoons honey
- 1 chipotle pepper in adobo, minced
- 1 clove garlic, grated
- 1/2 tablespoon chili powder
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1/2 teaspoon paprika, preferably smoked paprika
- 1/2 teaspoon ground black pepper
- 1/4 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1/4 teaspoon onion powder
- 1/4 teaspoon dried oregano
- Pinch red pepper flakes, optional
- 1 orange, zest and juice (1-2 teaspoons zest and 1 tablespoon juice)
- 1 teaspoon salt, or more to taste
- 1 tablespoon sunflower oil

## Method

1. If using wooden skewers, start by soaking the skewers in water for 15-30 minutes while you prep the chicken.
2. In a bowl combine all the ingredients for the marinade, mix well and give it a taste, adjust seasoning if needed. Then using your hands or tongs, mix the chicken with the chipotle mix. It'll look very saucy and a bit messy, but in the best possible way.
3. Thread the chicken pieces onto skewers (usually you get 4 skewers with a pound of chicken).
4. Grill the skewers until charred and cooked through; if using fillet be careful not to overcook them. Finish with a drizzle of honey, and if using chicken fillet a brush of melted butter to add to the juiciness.
