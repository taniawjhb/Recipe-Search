## Ingredients

- 2 large tomatoes
- 1 large cucumber
- 1 medium onion
- 1 large green/red pepper
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- A pinch of cumin
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 3 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
- 2 tablespoons red wine vinegar

## Method

1. Chop the onion in small cubes and soak in water.
2. Chop the tomatoes, cucumber and pepper and place in a bowl.
3. Drain the onions and add them to the rest of the vegetables.
4. Place the garlic in a mortar with a pinch of cumin and a pinch of salt and a few drops of olive oil until you have a paste. In another small bowl mix the rest of the olive oil, vinegar and salt, add the garlic paste and mix well.
5. Add the dressing to the salad and mix well. Cover and refrigerate.
6. Serve chilled with some crusty bread.
