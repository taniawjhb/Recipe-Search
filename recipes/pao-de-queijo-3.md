## Ingredients

- 1 cup grated Parmesan cheese
- 1 300 grams round of Queso Fresco, crumbled, or ricotta
- 1 cup full fat milk
- 1/4 cup vegetable oil, plus 1 tablespoon for oiling your hands
- 2 eggs
- 500 grams tapioca flour (also known as tapioca starch)
- 2-3 teaspoons kosher salt

## Method

1. Prepare the dough: Preheat oven to 175°C. Add the tapioca flour and the salt to a large bowl, whisk to combine and set aside.
2. In a medium bowl, combine the cheeses and the eggs and set aside.
3. Add the milk and the oil to a small saucepan and bring to a simmer on the stove top over medium heat.
4. When the liquid simmers, remove the pan from heat, and immediately pour the hot liquid into the tapioca mixture and using a wooden spoon, stir to combine; the mixture will not be homogeneous, it'll be gummy and clumpy.
5. Wait 5-10 minutes until the tapioca mixture is cool to touch, and only then add the cheese and egg mixture to the tapioca mixture. Mix with a wooden spoon, or your hands, to combine. Now you should have a homogeneous mixture and it should come together as a ball. You'll still see some white tapioca specks in the dough; that's totally normal.
6. Oil your hands, then scoop Tbsp to 2 tablespoons size portions, roll them into balls, and arrange them on a baking sheet approximately 2" apart from one another. Bake until golden brown, approximately 30-35 minutes.
