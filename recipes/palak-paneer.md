## Ingredients

- 350 grams spinach
- 1 medium tomato
- 5 large garlic cloves, divided (3 whole and 2 finely chopped)
- 1 inch ginger
- 1 green chili, or more to taste
- 1 tablespoon oil
- 1 large onion, finely chopped
- 1/2 cup water, or as needed
- 3/4-1 teaspoon garam masala
- 1/4 teaspoon turmeric powder
- 1/4 teaspoon red chili powder, or to taste
- salt to taste
- 2-3 tablespoons heavy cream, or adjust to taste
- 225 grams paneer, cut into cubes
- 1/2 tablespoon kasuri methi (crushed dried fenugreek leaves)
- lemon juice to taste

## Method

1. To a pot of boiling water, add a little salt and then spinach leaves. Blanch the spinach for 2 to 3 minutes until wilted. Take them out and put them in ice cold water to retain their green color.
2. To a blender, add the blanched spinach along with tomato, 3 cloves of garlic, ginger and green chili. Puree to a smooth paste and set aside.
3. Heat a pan on medium heat. Once hot, add the oil and then the remaining 2 cloves of chopped garlic. Sauté for a few minutes until it starts changing color.
4. Add the chopped onions. Cook for 2 to 3 minutes until soft and translucent.
5. Add the prepared spinach puree and mix. Also add around 1/2 cup water at this point.
6. Cover the pan and let it cook for 10 minutes on medium heat. Stir at regular intervals to avoid sticking at the bottom.
7. Once the spinach is cooked, add the garam masala, turmeric powder, red chilli powder and salt. Mix and cook for 1 minute. You may also add 1/2 teaspoon of sugar at this point if you like.
8. Then add the heavy cream and mix.
9. Stir in the paneer and mix. Let the curry simmer for 3 to 4 minutes. Switch off the flame, add lemon juice and kasuri methi and mix.
10. Serve palak paneer hot with naan or roti or rice.
