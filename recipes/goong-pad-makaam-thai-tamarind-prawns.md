## Ingredients

- 2 small shallots, roughly chopped
- 3 cloves garlic
- 2 cilantro roots or 5-6 cilantro stems
- 2 Thai chillies, or to taste
- 250 grams large prawns, peeled and deveined
- 1/2 cup (120 millilitres) chicken stock or water
- 3 tablespoons (35 grams) chopped palm sugar
- 1 1/2 tablespoons fish sauce
- 3 tablespoons (45 millilitres) Thai tamarind paste
- 1/4 cup red and green chillies, sliced into rounds or julienned
- Fried shallots, optional
- Cilantro leaves for garnish

## Method

1. Pound the garlic, shallots, cilantro roots and Thai chillies into a rough paste (or pulse in a small food processor).
2. Heat 2 tablespoons vegetable oil in a wok over medium-high. Add the prawns and sear until browned; flip and sear the other side. Turn off the heat, remove the prawns and set aside.
3. If the oil has darkened, switch to a clean wok with fresh oil; otherwise continue. Over medium-low heat, add the herb paste and sauté until aromatic and lightly browned.
4. Add the chicken stock, palm sugar, tamarind paste and fish sauce. Increase the heat to medium-high and cook, stirring, until the sugar dissolves. Reduce until the sauce is thick enough to coat the prawns.
5. Add the prawns and toss in the sauce; if not fully cooked, let them finish cooking in the pan.
6. Add the red and green chillies, then turn off the heat. For a neater presentation, remove the prawns, pour the sauce onto the plate, then arrange the prawns on top.
7. Top generously with fried shallots and garnish with cilantro. Serve with jasmine rice.
