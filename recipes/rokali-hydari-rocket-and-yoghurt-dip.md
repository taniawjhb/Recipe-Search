## Ingredients

- 1 cup plain yogurt (drained until thick)
- Handful fresh rocket leaves, cleaned, dried and sliced into thin ribbons (about 1 cup)
- 1-2 cloves garlic, peeled and minced
- 1 tablespoon fresh mint, minced (replaces 1 teaspoon dried mint)
- 1/2 teaspoon paprika (or the same amount of Aleppo pepper)
- Salt, to taste

## Method

1. Combine all the ingredients in a bowl, reserving the garnishes.
2. Transfer to a serving platter, drizzle with olive oil and sprinkle with cayenne.
3. Serve alongside pide, pita chips or similar.
