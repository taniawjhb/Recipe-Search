## Ingredients

- 100 grams fine corn meal or polenta
- 100 grams plain flour
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 tablespoon baking powder
- 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
- 75 grams caster sugar
- 2 large eggs
- 150 millilitres buttermilk
- 4 tablespoons olive oil, plus extra for greasing
- 200 grams creamed sweetcorn

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 180°C. Lightly grease a 20 centimetres square baking tin with baking paper.
2. Sift all the dry ingredients into a large bowl. Beat the eggs, buttermilk and olive oil together in a separate bowl.
3. Pour the egg mixture into the dry ingredients and fold it through. Finally fold in the creamed sweetcorn, then pour into the prepared tin and spread out evenly.
4. Bake for 20-25 minutes, until it is golden brown and a skewer inserted in the middle comes out clean. Remove and leave to cool slightly.
5. Cut into 16 squares and serve warm.
