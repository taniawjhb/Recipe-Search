## Ingredients

- 5 tbsp butter
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon fresh ground black pepper
- 1/4 teaspoon freshly ground nutmeg
- 1 cup water, plus 1/2 teaspoon water, divided
- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 1 cup grated gruyere cheese
- 5 large eggs, at room temperature (very important)

## Method

1. Add the butter, salt, pepper and nutmeg to 1 cup water in a medium saucepan and bring to a boil over medium-high heat. When the butter melts, reduce the heat to low.
2. Add the flour to the saucepan all at once and beat with a wooden spoon over low heat for about a minute until the mixture pulls away from the sides of the pan. Remove the pan from the heat.
3. Add the cheese to the pan and beat with a wooden spoon until thoroughly mixed.
4. Add 4 eggs, one at a time, beating each into the batter until well mixed before adding the next. Continue beating the mixture until it is smooth, shiny and firm.
5. In a frying pan, heat sufficient oil to deep fry small balls, until 140C.
6. Carefully fry small tsp-size balls of batter until puffed up, golden and cooked through. Remove with a slotted spoon and drain on paper towel, sprinkle with salt and serve warm.

## Notes

Delicious light cheesy puffs!
