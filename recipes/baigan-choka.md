## Ingredients

- 1 large firm eggplant, roasted, peeled and pulp collected
- 2 medium ripe tomatoes, roasted and peeled
- 1 small onion, finely chopped
- 2 cloves of garlic, freshly grated
- 1 habanero pepper/piri piri pepper or 2 green chilies, chopped
- 1 tablespoon hot oil of choice or butter
- Salt to taste
- Lime juice and cilantro to garnish

## Method

1. Wash and dry the eggplant. Pierce the eggplant all over and then roast in the highest shelf of the oven at the highest temperature, until the skin is blackened and collapsing.
2. Place the tomatoes on an outdoor grill or under a grill heated to its highest and cook until the tomatoes become soft and roasted.
3. If using the habanero, you can either roast it or grill it. For green chilies, just use freshly chopped ones.
4. Peel the eggplant and tomatoes and transfer the pulp to a mortar and pestle or small food processor.
5. Add salt, freshly ground garlic, and chili pepper.
6. Mash all the ingredients really well until a creamy mass is formed.
7. Now add hot oil and mix well. Add chopped onions and garnish the dish with cilantro and lime juice. Serve immediately.

## Notes

Serve with Trinidad flatbreads.
