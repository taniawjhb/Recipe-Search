## Ingredients

- 500 grams minced beef
- 30 grams dried breadcrumbs
- 3 to 4 tablespoons chilli cumin marinade
- mini bread rolls
- 4 tablespoons chilli mayo
- 3 plum tomatoes, sliced
- 4 teaspoons chimichurri marinade
- Salt and black pepper

## Method

1. First make the burgers: place the beef mince and breadcrumbs in a large bowl, add the chilli and cumin marinade, season with salt and pepper and mix well. Divide the mince into equal size portions (around golf ball sized), then flatten into patties and refrigerate to firm them up.
2. Heat a griddle pan to very hot, then reduce the heat to medium. Lightly oil both sides of your patties, and cook for 4 minutes a side (make sure to brown the patty). Remove from heat and let stand for a few minutes (you can opt to do this on a braai following the same procedure).
3. Slice, brush lightly with olive oil and toast the burger buns. In the meanwhile, warm up the black beans in a pan.
4. To assemble, put the bottom halves of the buns onto a serving plate. Spread the top halves with chilli mayo. Arrange sliced tomato on the bun bases and place a burger on top. Spoon a little chimichurri marinade, followed by a tablespoon of black beans (optional). Close the burger and serve immediately.
