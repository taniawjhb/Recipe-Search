## Ingredients

- 250gms green beans, trimmed and cut into 5cm pieces
- 6 cups water
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 2 tomatoes, diced
- 1/4 onion, diced
- 1 jalapeno, stem and seeds removed, diced
- 1 garlic clove, finely diced
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon ground pepper
- 1/8 teaspoon ground oregano
- 1/4 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1/4 bunch chopped cilantro

## Method

1. Trim the ends off the green beans and cut into 2-inch pieces. Rinse and add to a stock pot with water and salt.
2. Simmer for 3-4 minutes.
3. Drain and set aside until ready to use.
4. Heat olive oil in a large skillet.
5. Add the tomatoes and cook for 2 minutes.
6. Add the onion, jalapeno, garlic, salt, pepper, oregano, and cumin.
7. Cook for 4-5 minutes, or until the liquid has evaporated and a thick tomato paste has formed. Add the green beans. Mix to combine. Add the cilantro. Serve and enjoy.
