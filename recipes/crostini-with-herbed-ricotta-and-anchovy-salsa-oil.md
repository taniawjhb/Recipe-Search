## Ingredients

- 1 baguette
- Olive oil, for drizzling

### For the herbed ricotta

- 400 grams fresh ricotta
- 1 small clove garlic
- Grated zest and juice of 1 lemon
- 50 millilitres olive oil

### For the anchovy salsa

- Handful fresh flat-leaf parsley
- 50 grams good quality anchovy fillets in olive oil, drained
- 2 tablespoons capers, drained
- 1/2 garlic clove, finely grated
- Grated zest and juice of 1 lemon
- 2-3 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 160°C. Cut the baguette diagonally into thin slices and arrange on a baking sheet. Drizzle with olive oil and bake for 10-12 minutes until crisp. Set aside to cool.
2. Put the ricotta, garlic, lemon zest and half the juice in a food processor. Pulse briefly while drizzling in the 50 millilitres olive oil to give a chunky purée. Add salt and pepper, along with more lemon juice if needed.
3. For the salsa: place the parsley, anchovies, capers, garlic, lemon zest and juice in the bowl of a food processor. Add the olive oil to cover and run until the mixture is a smooth green oil. Season with salt, pepper and more lemon juice if needed.
4. Dollop the ricotta purée onto the crostini and drizzle generously with anchovy salsa oil to serve.
