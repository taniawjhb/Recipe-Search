## Ingredients

### Meat Filling

- 2 tablespoons dried breadcrumbs
- 1 packet (500 grams) macaroni elbow pasta
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 kilogram beef mince
- 2 red paprika peppers, finely chopped
- 1 brown onion, coarsely chopped
- 1 clove garlic, crushed
- 1 bottle (400 grams) passata
- 1 tin peeled plum tomatoes, chopped
- 1 teaspoon dried oregano
- 1 teaspoon fresh chopped oregano
- 80 millilitres (1/3 cup) water
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 70 grams (1 cup) shredded parmesan

### White Sauce

- 80 grams butter
- 50 grams (1/3 cup) plain flour
- 1 litre (4 cups) milk
- 4 eggs, lightly whisked

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 180°C. Brush a 6.5 centimetre deep, 26 x 33 centimetre ovenproof dish with oil to grease. Add the breadcrumbs and shake to coat.
2. Cook the pasta in a large saucepan of salted boiling water following the packet directions or until al dente. Drain and return to the pan.
3. Meanwhile, heat half the oil in a frying pan over medium-high heat. Add half the mince and cook, stirring, for 3-4 minutes or until the mince changes colour. Transfer to a heatproof bowl. Repeat with the remaining mince, reheating the pan between batches.
4. Heat the remaining oil in the pan. Add the onion, peppers and garlic and cook, stirring, for 5 minutes or until soft. Add the mince, passata, plum tomatoes, oregano, water, cumin and cinnamon and cook, stirring, for 5-8 minutes or until the sauce thickens slightly.
5. To make the bechamel sauce, melt the butter in a saucepan over medium heat until foaming. Add the flour and cook, stirring, for 1 minute or until the mixture bubbles. Remove from the heat. Gradually add the milk, stirring constantly until smooth. Place the pan over medium heat and cook, stirring constantly, until the sauce thickens. Remove from the heat and whisk in the egg. Season with salt and pepper.
6. Add 310 millilitres (1 1/4 cups) of the bechamel sauce to the pasta and stir until well combined. Place half the pasta mixture in the prepared pan. Spoon over the mince mixture. Top with the remaining pasta mixture. Spread the remaining bechamel sauce over the top. Sprinkle with parmesan. Bake for 45-50 minutes or until golden. Serve.
