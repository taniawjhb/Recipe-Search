## Ingredients

- Large knob of butter
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 2 punnets portabellini mushrooms
- 2 cloves garlic, finely chopped
- Pinch of dry red chili
- 1 red onion
- 2 tsp finely chopped parsley
- 50ml brandy/cognac
- 100ml cream
- 1/2 tsp good quality truffle oil
- 1 baguette, cut into .5cm slices

## Method

1. In a large frying pan add the butter and olive oil. Once it is hot add the finely chopped red onion, cook until tender. Now add the finely minced garlic and the dry red chili. Add the mushrooms, stirring occasionally; if needed add a little extra olive oil or butter. Add salt and pepper. Once the mushrooms have reduced down and cooked through add the brandy, being careful in case the flame catches. Cook off the brandy, then add the cream, truffle oil, and cold butter and the fresh parsley. Stir well, check and adjust seasoning.
2. While the mushrooms are cooking, slice your baguette and lightly brush with olive oil, before grilling until just golden, then flip and repeat on the other side. Once the mushrooms are cooked, arrange the slices of toasted baguette on a platter and then add a spoonful of the mushroom mixture. Serve topped with freshly chopped parsley while still warm.
