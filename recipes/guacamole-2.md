## Ingredients

- 6 ripe avocados
- 3 limes, juiced
- 1 medium yellow onion, chopped
- 2 serrano chiles, cut into rounds
- 1 big handful fresh cilantro with stems, about 1/2 cup, finely chopped
- Extra-virgin olive oil
- Coarse salt
- Freshly ground black pepper

## Method

1. Halve and pit the avocados. With a tablespoon, scoop out the flesh into a mixing bowl. Mash the avocados using either a fork or potato masher, leaving them still a bit chunky.
2. In the bowl of a food processor add the remaining ingredients, and process until smooth. Fold everything together.
3. Drizzle with a little olive oil, adjust seasoning with salt and pepper and give it 1 final mix with a fork.
4. Lay a piece of plastic wrap tight on the surface of the guacamole so it doesn't brown and refrigerate for at least 1 hour before serving.
