## Ingredients

- 2 teaspoons turmeric powder, divided
- 1 1/2 teaspoons red chili powder (Kashmiri chili powder is potent; adjust to taste)
- 4 tablespoons chickpea flour
- 2 teaspoons salt
- 4 teaspoons water, to form slurry with chickpea flour
- 2 tablespoons vegetable oil (you can add extra if you like)
- 1 teaspoon mustard seeds (skip if unavailable)
- 1 1/2 teaspoons cumin seeds (can substitute with cumin powder)
- 6 medium potatoes, Yukon Gold

## Method

1. Bring a pot of water to boil along with 1 teaspoon of turmeric powder. Quarter the potatoes (no need to peel) and drop them into the water once you start to see bubbles appear. Boil for about 10 to 15 minutes.
2. At the 10 minute mark, take a fork and poke your potatoes. If the fork goes through cleanly, you're good to go. If not, let them cook a bit more, checking every minute. You don't want to over boil them.
3. In a medium sized bowl add the chickpea flour, remaining turmeric, salt and red chili powder and give it a good whisk.
4. Once the potatoes are done boiling, remove from heat using a colander or slotted spoon. Rinse the potatoes under cold water to stop them cooking and peel the potatoes.
5. Drop the peeled potatoes into the bowl with the spice mix and give it a good toss to coat them evenly. Add about 4 teaspoons of water to make sure the spices stick to the potatoes. Don't add too much water.
6. In a skillet, heat vegetable oil on medium heat. Once the oil starts to glisten, add mustard seeds. Once the mustard seeds start sputtering, add cumin seeds. Toast for about 20 seconds taking care not to burn the seeds.
7. Toss the potatoes into the skillet, taking care not to overcrowd them. Give them a good stir and let them cook for about 3 to 4 minutes. Then give them one more good stir and let them cook another 3 to 4 minutes. Your potatoes should be golden brown by this time. If not, give it a few more minutes.
8. Once golden brown, turn the heat off, garnish with some curry leaves or cilantro if you wish, and serve hot with naan, rice or any other main dish of choice.
