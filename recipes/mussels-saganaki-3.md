## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 kilograms mussels, washed and debearded
- 1 glass white wine (dry)
- 20 cherry tomatoes
- 1 pepper
- 1 red onion
- 1 spring of celery
- 1 carrot
- 2 cloves of garlic
- 1 bunch fresh parsley
- 100 grams feta cheese (optional)
- 1/2 cup olive oil

## Method

1. Chop the red onion, pepper, celery, and carrot into tiny (0.5 centimetre x 0.5 centimetre) chunks.
2. Heat a large saucepan over high heat and add the olive oil. Pour in the vegetable mixture and sauté, until tender.
3. Cut the cherry tomatoes into halves and add them into the pan along with the mussels and the feta cheese (crushed).
4. Pour in the white wine and cook at high heat, with the lid on, for 8-10 minutes.
