## Ingredients

- 3 large tomatoes, firm and fresh
- 1 medium white onion
- 1 green bell pepper
- 1 red bell pepper
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1/2 cup fresh parsley, finely chopped
- 1 tablespoon tomato paste
- 1 lemon, juice of
- 2 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
- 1/2 teaspoon Aleppo pepper
- 1 teaspoon sumac

## Method

1. Finely dice the tomatoes and place them in a bowl. Mince the onions and peppers just as finely, then fold them into the tomatoes.
2. Stir in the minced garlic and chopped parsley, ensuring the flavours blend evenly.
3. Add the tomato paste, olive oil, lemon juice, salt, black pepper, Aleppo pepper and sumac, mixing thoroughly until every bite is infused with richness.
4. Cover and refrigerate for 30 minutes to allow the flavours to meld.
5. Serve chilled as a mezze, sauce or vibrant salad alongside your favourite dishes.
