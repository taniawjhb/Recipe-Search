## Ingredients

### Galette Pastry

- 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 cup unsalted butter
- 5-6 tablespoons ice-cold water

### Cherry Tomato Filling

- 500 grams cherry tomatoes
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 2 tablespoons balsamic vinegar
- 7-8 cloves garlic, adjust to taste
- 1 tablespoon fresh chopped thyme
- 1/4 teaspoon salt

## Method

1. For the dough: Mix together the flour and salt in a large mixing bowl. Using a pastry cutter or your fingers, cut in the cubes of butter until crumbly and pea-sized.
2. Mix in the ice-cold water one tablespoon at a time, stirring with a wooden spoon until a cohesive ball of dough forms. The dough will still be slightly crumbly, which is fine. Wrap tightly in plastic wrap and rest in the fridge for 30 minutes.
3. Preheat the oven to 220°C.
4. For the filling: Roughly chop the garlic. Heat the olive oil over medium heat in a large skillet. Add the garlic, cherry tomatoes, balsamic vinegar, thyme and salt. Sauté just until the cherry tomatoes burst, about 8-10 minutes, stirring frequently. Set aside.
5. To assemble: On a lightly floured surface, roll out the pastry dough into a 30 centimetre circle. Place on a parchment-lined sheet pan or on a preheated pizza stone.
6. Pour the cherry tomato filling into the centre of the galette, leaving about a 5 centimetre border. Fold the border in over just the edges of the cherry tomatoes.
7. Bake for 25-30 minutes at 220°C on the middle oven rack, until the galette is golden brown. Remove from the oven and allow to cool for 10 to 15 minutes before cutting.
