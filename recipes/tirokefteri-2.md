## Ingredients

- 4 red bell peppers
- 1/4 cup extra-virgin olive oil
- 2 tablespoons red wine vinegar
- 2 garlic cloves, peeled
- 2 cups crumbled feta cheese
- 1-2 red chili (optional)

## Method

1. Char peppers directly over gas flame or in broiler until blackened on all sides. Enclose in paper bag 10 minutes. Peel, seed, and quarter peppers.
2. Puree peppers, oil, garlic, red wine vinegar and cheese in processor (add the chili at this stage if using). Season with salt and pepper. Chill at least 3 hours or overnight.
