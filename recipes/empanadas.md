## Ingredients

- 375 grams plain/all-purpose flour
- 175 grams cold unsalted butter, cut into 1 centimetre cubes
- 1 teaspoon cooking/kosher salt
- 1 egg yolk
- 125 millilitres warm full-fat milk
- 1 1/2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1/2 onion, finely chopped
- 1 garlic clove, minced
- 300 grams beef mince
- 120 grams chorizo
- 2 teaspoons tomato paste
- 1 teaspoon fresh thyme, finely chopped
- 1/2 teaspoon dried oregano
- 1/2 teaspoon cumin
- 1/2 teaspoon paprika
- 1/4 teaspoon cayenne pepper
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 180 millilitres chicken stock
- 125 millilitres tomato passata
- 150 grams potato, peeled, cut into 8 millimetre cubes
- 1/3 cup green onion, finely sliced

## Method

1. For the filling, roughly chop the chorizo into small pieces. Blitz using a food processor until it becomes a paste.
2. Heat the oil in a large non-stick pan over high heat. Cook the onion and garlic for 2 minutes. Add the chorizo and cook for 2 minutes. Add the beef and cook until you no longer see raw beef. Add the tomato paste and cook for 1 minute. Then add everything else except the green onion, then stir.
3. Bring to a simmer then lower heat so it's simmering gently. Cook for 15 minutes, stirring every now and then. The goal is a juicy but not watery filling, with cooked potato. Stir through the green onion. Transfer to a bowl and fully cool before using (about 2 hours).
4. For the pastry, whisk the milk and yolk in a small jug. Pulse the flour, salt and butter in a food processor until the butter becomes fine crumbs, with the largest lumps no bigger than peas (about 4 to 6 times). Add the egg-milk mixture, then blitz on low until it comes together into a ball of dough, about 10 seconds.
5. Turn out onto a lightly floured work surface, then bring together into a smooth log about 20 centimetres long. Cut in half, shape into 2 centimetre thick round discs. Wrap in cling wrap then refrigerate for 30 minutes to 1 hour.
6. Place one chilled disc on a lightly floured work surface. Roll out to 3 millimetres thickness. Cut 15 centimetre rounds, or another size as desired. Keep covered in the fridge, stacked on paper, as you continue to roll and cut. You should get 10 or 11 rounds.
7. Preheat the oven to 220°C (200°C fan). The filling should be cold but spreadable. Place 3 tablespoons of filling on the top half of a round of pastry. Spread flat in a half-moon shape, leaving a 1.5 centimetre border.
8. Brush the edge with egg white, fold to enclose, pressing out air and sealing the edge. Then crimp with a fork or leave plain.
9. Place the empanadas on 2 baking trays. Brush with egg yolk. Bake for 20 minutes or until golden brown all over and crispy on the base.
10. Serve hot with a dipping sauce of choice.
