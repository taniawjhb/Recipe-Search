## Ingredients

- 4 vine ripe tomatoes, seeded and chopped
- 1 jalapeno pepper, seeded and finely chopped
- 1 small red onion, chopped
- 1/4 cup (2 handfuls) cilantro leaves, finely chopped (substitute parsley if preferred)
- 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil
- 1 pound ground sirloin
- 2 cloves garlic, chopped
- 1 small onion, chopped
- 1 jalapeno pepper, seeded and chopped
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 1/2 teaspoons dark chili powder
- 1 1/2 teaspoons ground cumin
- 2 teaspoons to 1 tablespoon cayenne pepper sauce
- 1 can black beans (15 ounces), drained
- 2 tablespoons butter
- 2 tablespoons all-purpose flour
- 2 cups milk
- 300 grams gouda, shredded (about 2 1/2 cups)
- 2 varieties of corn chips

## Method

1. Arrange a mixture of 2 varieties of corn chips on a very large platter or use your broiler pan as a platter.
2. Combine the salsa ingredients (tomatoes, jalapeno, red onion and cilantro) in a bowl and set aside for the flavours to marry.
3. Heat a medium nonstick skillet over medium-high heat. Add the oil, garlic, onion and peppers to the pan and sauté 2 minutes, then add the meat and crumble with a wooden spoon. Season the meat with salt, chili powder, cumin and cayenne pepper sauce. Cook the meat 5 minutes, then stir in the beans and reduce heat to low.
4. In a medium sauce pot, melt the butter and add the flour to it. Cook the flour and butter 1 to 2 minutes over moderate heat, then whisk in the milk. When the milk comes to a bubble, stir in the cheese with a wooden spoon. Remove the cheese sauce from the heat.
5. Pour the cheese sauce evenly over the spread of chips and top evenly with the beef and beans and the pico de gallo. Serve immediately as is or garnish with your choice of extra toppings.
