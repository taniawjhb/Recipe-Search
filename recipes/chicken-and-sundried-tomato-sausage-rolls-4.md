## Ingredients

- 1 kilogram chicken thighs, deboned and skinless, minced
- 1 medium brown onion, chopped finely
- 1/2 cup breadcrumbs
- 1 egg
- 1/4 cup finely chopped fresh basil
- 1/2 teaspoon dried oregano
- 1/2 cup drained semi-dried tomatoes in oil, finely chopped
- 2 tablespoons tomato paste
- 5 sheets puff pastry
- 1 egg, extra to glaze

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 220°C/200°C fan forced. Line oven trays with baking paper.
2. Combine mince, onion, breadcrumbs, egg, basil, semi-dried tomato and paste in a large bowl.
3. Cut pastry sheets in half lengthways. Place equal amounts of chicken filling mixture along the centre of each pastry piece; roll pastry to enclose the filling.
4. Cut each into 6 equal pieces, place seam side down, on trays. Brush with extra egg.
5. Bake rolls for about 30 minutes. Serve warm with a home made tomato sauce.
