## Ingredients

- 600 grams piece of chicken breast
- salt and pepper
- Stuffing:
- 3 king oyster mushrooms, cleaned and chopped
- 10 crimini mushrooms, cleaned and chopped
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 1 shallot, minced
- 3 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
- pinch of chilli flakes and rosemary
- 1/2 teaspoon each of dried marjoram, thyme, oregano
- 1/4 cup white wine
- 1/2 cup panko breadcrumbs
- 1/4 cup fine Italian breadcrumbs
- 1/2 teaspoon white truffle oil
- 113 grams plain good quality cream cheese
- Wrapping:
- 8 slices Italian prosciutto crudo
- Roasting:
- 1 tablespoon extra virgin olive oil
- 2 carrots, peeled and cut into wedges
- 1/4 onion, chopped
- Garnish:
- a drizzle of olive oil
- 6 lemon slices

## Method

1. Cut the chicken in half lengthwise, cover with plastic wrap on both sides and pound with a meat cleaver until somewhat flat. Do the same for the other fillet of chicken. Season with salt and pepper lightly, then set aside in the fridge.
2. Stuffing: In a heavy based pan drizzle in olive oil. Sprinkle in chilli flakes and toss in garlic and shallot.
3. Saute for about 5 minutes on low to medium heat.
4. Throw in the chopped mushrooms and season with salt and pepper.
5. Add the marjoram, thyme and oregano. Stir well and let cook for 5 minutes. Pour in the white wine and cook for an additional 3-4 minutes or until the wine has evaporated.
6. Take off the heat and stir in the breadcrumbs until well incorporated and set aside. If it is a bit dry add some vegetable stock.
7. Chicken: Take the two fillets of chicken and lay on a wood board. Spread the seasoned cream cheese all over the inside of the chicken in a thin layer.
8. Spoon the mushroom stuffing on top of the cream cheese, then start rolling the chicken to form a round roll.
9. Wrap the mushroom chicken roll in the prosciutto crudo slices. Do the same for the remaining chicken fillet.
10. Roasting: Pre-heat the oven to 190°C. In a medium roasting dish place the carrots onto the bottom of the pan. Sprinkle the onion on top.
11. Place the chicken rolls perpendicular to the carrots. Cover with foil wrap and place in the middle rack of the oven.
12. Bake for 35 minutes then uncover and cook for another 25 minutes. Make sure to check different areas of the roll. Take out of the oven and let rest 10 minutes.
