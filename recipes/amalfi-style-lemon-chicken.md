## Ingredients

- 6 chicken fillets
- 1/2 cup all purpose flour, for coating
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp freshly ground black pepper
- 2 tbsp lemon olive oil
- 2 cloves garlic, finely chopped
- Zest of 1 lemon
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp finely chopped rosemary
- 1/2 cup white wine (or chicken stock)
- 1/4 cup white balsamic
- 1/4 cup lemon juice
- 1/4 cup water
- Butter (optional)

## Method

1. Pat dry the fillets with a paper towel, butterfly them, and with a meat mallet gently flatten to an even thickness. In a shallow dish, combine the flour, salt and black pepper. Lightly dredge each chicken fillet in the flour mixture, shaking off any excess for a thin coating.
2. Heat oil, rosemary and garlic in a large non-stick skillet on medium-high. Place the fillet and sear 1 to 2 minutes on each side until lightly golden brown. Remove the chicken and set aside. Deglaze the pan with wine or chicken stock.
3. Add the balsamic, lemon juice and lemon zest. Gently simmer for two minutes. Reduce the heat to low, cover the pan and cook for 5 minutes, then add the chicken and cook a further 5 minutes until the chicken is cooked through, occasionally basting. Swirl in 2 tablespoons of butter for a rich and glossy finish. Serve with spoonfuls of sauce and a drizzle of olive oil.
