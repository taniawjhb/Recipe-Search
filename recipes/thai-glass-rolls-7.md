## Ingredients

### Spring Rolls

- 8 rice wrappers (21 centimetre diameter)
- 1 red pepper, thinly sliced
- 1 carrot, julienned
- Handful bean sprouts
- 1 1/3 tablespoons chopped fresh Thai basil
- 3 tablespoons chopped fresh mint leaves
- 3 tablespoons chopped fresh cilantro
- 2 leaves lettuce, chopped
- Rice vermicelli noodles

### Nuoc Cham

- 4 teaspoons fish sauce
- 1/4 cup water
- 2 tablespoons fresh lime juice
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 2 tablespoons white sugar
- 1/2 teaspoon garlic chilli sauce

### Hoisin Peanut Sauce

- 3 tablespoons hoisin sauce
- 1 teaspoon finely chopped peanuts

## Method

1. Bring a medium saucepan of water to the boil. Boil the rice vermicelli 3 to 5 minutes, or until al dente; drain and cool.
2. Fill a large bowl with warm water. Dip one wrapper into the water for 1 second to soften.
3. Lay the wrapper flat. In a row across the centre, place a tablespoon of vermicelli noodles, some julienned carrot, strips of red pepper, some bean sprouts, basil, mint, cilantro and lettuce, leaving about 5 centimetres uncovered on each side. Fold the uncovered sides inward, then tightly roll the wrapper, beginning at one end. Cut the spring rolls in half at a slight angle.
4. Serve on a platter with the two dipping sauces. For the nuoc cham, stir together all its ingredients. For the hoisin peanut sauce, combine the hoisin and chopped peanuts.
