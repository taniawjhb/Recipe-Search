## Ingredients

### Crust

- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 1/3 cup granulated sugar
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1 cup butter, chilled and cut into small cubes

### Filling

- 240 grams cream cheese, softened
- 1 1/2 cups granulated sugar
- 1/4 cup flour
- 3/4 cup fresh-squeezed passion fruit juice
- 1/4 cup powdered sugar, plus more for topping
- 6 large eggs
- 1 teaspoon vanilla

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 175°C and line a 23 x 33 centimetre pan with foil or parchment, with plenty of overhang around the edges to lift the bars out of the pan before cutting.
2. Prepare the crust by combining the flour, sugar, salt and butter cubes in a food processor or blender. Cover and pulse until fine crumbs form. (You can also do this with a pastry cutter, fork, or with your fingers in a large bowl.)
3. Press the crust into the prepared pan and bake for 10 minutes while you prepare the filling.
4. Prepare the filling by combining the cream cheese and sugar in a large bowl. Cream together until smooth. Mix in the flour, powdered sugar, passion fruit juice, eggs and vanilla until completely incorporated. Pour the filling over the crust and return to the oven for 30 minutes.
5. Allow to cool for about 20 minutes, then cover and chill in the fridge for 4 hours or overnight. Lift the foil edges to remove the bars from the pan, slice into squares, sprinkle with additional powdered sugar and serve.
