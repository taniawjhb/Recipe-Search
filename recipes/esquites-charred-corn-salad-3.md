## Ingredients

- 4 cups corn (about 5 ears)
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1/2 red bell pepper, chopped
- 1/2 small red onion, finely chopped
- 1/2 cup fresh cilantro, chopped
- 6 spring onions, chopped
- 1 jalapeno pepper, diced
- 1/2 avocado, chopped
- 4 tablespoons lime juice (from about 2 limes)
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika
- 1/4 teaspoon ground black pepper
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 2 tablespoons sour cream (or yogurt)
- 2 tablespoons mayonnaise
- 1/2 cup feta, crumbled

## Method

1. Start by charring the corn over a hot braai or in a very hot griddle pan. Allow to cool until it can be handled, then cut the corn off the cob and set aside in a large bowl. (You can also use canned or frozen corn. If using frozen, no need to thaw it; char it in a very hot dry pan.)
2. Transfer the corn to a large bowl and let it cool for a couple of minutes.
3. To the same bowl, add the remaining ingredients. Stir everything together (excluding the diced avocado) until well combined. Now add the avocado, mix gently, taste and then adjust lime juice, salt and pepper as necessary.
4. To serve, garnish with additional cheese and cilantro, if preferred.
