## Ingredients

- 900 grams starchy potatoes
- 2 tablespoons tomato paste
- 1 tablespoon smoked paprika
- 2-3 tablespoons fresh diced parsley
- 2 teaspoons dried thyme
- 1/2 teaspoon salt, plus a bit extra for salting your water
- 1/2 teaspoon pepper
- 2 1/2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1/2 fresh lemon
- extra parsley for garnish

## Method

1. Preheat your oven to 200°C, and line a baking sheet with aluminum foil. Set aside.
2. Bring a pot of salted water to a boil while you dice your potatoes. Cook the potatoes 8-10 minutes until just al dente, you don't need to cook them through, but a fork should be able to pierce a cube.
3. While the potatoes are cooking, combine your spices, tomato paste, and olive oil in a large mixing bowl. Drain the potatoes and then add them to the mixing bowl and mix until well coated.
4. Place the seasoned potatoes on your baking sheet and bake 40-50 minutes, turning halfway, until cooked through and crispy.
5. Top with juice from half a lemon and fresh parsley just before serving. Serve immediately and enjoy!
