## Ingredients

- 250g bacon slices
- 20 pearl onions, peeled, or 1 large yellow onion, sliced
- 1.5kg chicken thighs and legs, excess fat trimmed, skin on
- 6 garlic cloves, peeled
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 2 cups chicken stock
- 2 cups red wine (pinot noir, burgundy, or zinfandel)
- 2 bay leaves
- Several fresh thyme sprigs
- Several fresh parsley sprigs
- 250g button mushrooms, trimmed and roughly chopped
- 2 tbsp butter
- Chopped fresh parsley for garnish

## Method

1. Brown the bacon on medium-high heat in a dutch oven big enough to hold the chicken, about 10 minutes. Remove the cooked bacon and set aside, keeping the bacon fat in the pan.
2. Working in batches if necessary, add the onions and chicken, skin side down. Brown the chicken well on all sides, about 10 minutes.
3. Halfway through the browning, add the garlic and sprinkle the chicken with salt and pepper. (It is best to add salt while cooking, not just at the very end, as it brings out the flavour of the chicken.)
4. Spoon off any excess fat. Add the chicken stock, wine, and herbs, then add back the bacon. Lower the heat to a simmer.
5. Cover and cook for 20 minutes, or until the chicken is tender and cooked through. Remove the chicken and onions to a separate platter. Remove and discard the bay leaves, herb sprigs, and garlic.
6. Add the mushrooms to the remaining liquid and turn the heat to high. Boil quickly and reduce the liquid by three fourths until it becomes thick and saucy.
7. Lower the heat and stir in the butter. Return the chicken and onions to the pan to reheat and coat with sauce. Adjust the seasoning, garnish with parsley, and serve.
