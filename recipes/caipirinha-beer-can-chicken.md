## Ingredients

- 1 whole chicken
- Olive oil for drizzling
- 5 sage leaves, finely chopped
- 2 tablespoons cachaça (or cane)
- 1 tablespoon soft brown sugar
- Salt and black pepper

For the steam marinade:

- Juice of 2 large limes
- 2 tablespoons soft brown sugar
- 3 tablespoons cachaça (or cane)
- 3 garlic cloves, finely chopped or crushed
- 1/2 teaspoon fine salt
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 5-6 large sage leaves, chopped

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 200°C. Rub the entire chicken with some olive oil, chopped sage, salt and pepper, making sure to get into the cavity of the bird.
2. Place all the ingredients for the steam marinade into a bowl and stir till the sugar has dissolved.
3. Take an empty can, making sure all labels are peeled off and the can is washed thoroughly, and add your steam marinade to it.
4. Wrap the outside of a heavy-based roasting tin with kitchen foil, then place the can and marinade into the middle. Sit the chicken on top of the can so that the can enters the cavity. Spread out the legs to support the bird's weight. Take a squeezed lime, and pop it into the neck to form a plug, preventing steam escaping (alternatively, wrap the neck in tin foil).
5. Roast for 45 minutes, until the chicken is almost cooked. Mix the remaining cachaça and brown sugar and carefully brush over the chicken. Cook for a further 10-15 minutes until golden and the juices from the chicken run clear.
6. Transfer to a serving plate and drizzle with the thick sticky marinade.
