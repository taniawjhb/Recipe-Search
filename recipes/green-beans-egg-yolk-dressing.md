## Ingredients

- 1 tablespoon fine sea salt
- 160 grams fine green beans
- 3 hard-boiled eggs, yolks separated
- 1/3 cup extra virgin olive oil
- 1–2 tablespoons white spirit vinegar
- Salt and pepper, to taste

## Method

1. Bring 2 litres of water to the boil in a large saucepan, then stir in the fine salt.
2. Add the beans, return to the boil and cook 3–4 minutes until just tender. Drain and plunge into ice water.
3. When cool, drain well and pat dry.
4. Separate the hard-boiled egg yolks from the whites; reserve the whites. Press the yolks through a fine sieve into a small bowl. Add 1 tablespoon of the vinegar and whisk to loosen, then whisk in the olive oil in a slow stream to create a mayonnaise. Taste and adjust salt, pepper and vinegar.
5. To assemble, place the beans on a platter, drizzle with the egg yolk dressing and top with the reserved egg whites cut into small pieces.
