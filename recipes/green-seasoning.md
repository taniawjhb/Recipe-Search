## Ingredients

- 1 bunch of cilantro
- 1/2 bunch of parsley
- 4 scallions (chives), chopped
- 1 head of garlic, peeled
- 1 bunch of thyme, about 8 sprigs
- 1 stalk of celery
- 2 pimento peppers
- Pinch of Himalayan pink salt
- 1 small onion, sliced
- 1/2 red bell pepper

## Method

1. Transfer all of the ingredients into a colander and rinse thoroughly.
2. Place all of the ingredients into either a blender or food processor. If using a food processor, blitz on high speed until the bulk of the herbs have broken down into a coarse texture, remembering to pulse and scrape the sides.
3. If using a blender, blitz until a puree is formed; feel free to add a tbsp or two of olive oil to help things move along quickly.
4. Store in a container in a cool place.
