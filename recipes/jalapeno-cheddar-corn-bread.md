## Ingredients

- 2 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 1/2 cups yellow cornmeal/polenta
- 1/3 cup granulated sugar
- 2 tablespoons baking powder
- 2 1/2 teaspoons salt
- 1 1/2 teaspoons garlic powder
- 2 cups buttermilk
- 4 large eggs
- 1 cup unsalted butter, melted
- 300 grams frozen sweet corn, thawed
- 3 cups grated sharp cheddar cheese
- 3-4 large jalapenos, diced (about 3/4 cup)
- 2 tablespoons chopped cilantro (optional)

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 175°C. Set out a 9 x 13 inch baking dish and spray it with nonstick cooking spray.
2. Dice the jalapenos and grate the cheese.
3. Set out a large mixing bowl. Add the flour, cornmeal, sugar, baking powder, salt, and garlic powder. Mix well. Then whisk in the buttermilk, eggs, and melted butter. Whisk until smooth.
4. Fold the thawed sweet corn, 2 cups of cheddar cheese, cilantro and the diced jalapenos into the batter. Mix gently until just well combined.
5. Scoop the batter into the baking dish and sprinkle the remaining 1 cup shredded cheese over the top.
6. Bake for 40-50 minutes. The center should no longer jiggle when you shake the pan. (You can insert a toothpick into the center to make sure it's fully baked. If the toothpick comes out dry, remove from the oven.)
