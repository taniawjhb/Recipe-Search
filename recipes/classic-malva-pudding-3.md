## Ingredients

- 60 grams butter
- 1 cup white sugar
- 2 extra large eggs
- 2 tablespoons apricot jam
- 2 teaspoons bicarbonate of soda
- 1 cup milk
- 2 cups cake flour
- Pinch of salt
- 30 millilitres vinegar
- 250 grams butter
- 1 1/2 cups white sugar
- 1/2 cup water
- 1 1/2 cups cream
- 2 teaspoons vanilla essence

## Method

1. Cream butter and sugar together, beat in the egg until light and fluffy. Beat in the apricot jam.
2. Dissolve the bicarb in the milk.
3. Sift the flour and salt together and add to the creamed mixture alternately with the milk. Lastly stir in the vinegar.
4. Pour into a deep round dish about 19cm diameter. Cover the dish with a lid or foil and then bake for 1 hour at 180°C.
5. Bring the butter, sugar and water to the boil and simmer, stirring all the time for 2 minutes.
6. Remove from the stove and then add the cream and the vanilla. Pour over the hot baked pudding.
