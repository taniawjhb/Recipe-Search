## Ingredients

- 70 grams red chillies, deseeded and roughly chopped
- 5-6 garlic cloves
- 3 tablespoons rice wine vinegar
- 2 tablespoons red wine vinegar
- 2 teaspoons fine salt
- 1 heaped teaspoon toasted cumin seeds
- 1/2 teaspoon dry oregano
- 60 millilitres olive oil

## Method

1. Place all your ingredients into a small food processor or mortar and pestle and blend till smooth.
