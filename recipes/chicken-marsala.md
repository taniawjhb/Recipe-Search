## Ingredients

### For the chicken

- 1 cup all-purpose flour, divided
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 4 large boneless, skinless chicken breasts
- 3 tablespoons olive oil
- 2 tablespoons unsalted butter

### For the Marsala sauce

- 2 tablespoons unsalted butter, as needed
- 1 pint (8 ounces) portabellini mushrooms, quartered
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 3/4 cup dry Marsala wine (you can use sherry)
- 3/4 cup chicken stock
- 1/4 cup heavy cream

## Method

1. To start, pound the chicken breast until thin. Place the chicken breast between two pieces of cling wrap and flatten until the breast is evenly thin.
2. Now lightly dredge the chicken breasts in the seasoned flour.
3. Heat the oil in a 12-inch straight-sided skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering. Add the butter, followed by 2 of the chicken breasts. Fry until golden-brown on both sides, 3 to 4 minutes per side. Remove the browned chicken to an ovenproof baking dish. Repeat with the remaining chicken breasts, working in batches of 2.
4. Keep the chicken warm by covering it with aluminium foil and placing it in an oven to keep warm.
5. Keep the heat on medium-high; if there isn't butter left from frying the chicken, add up to 2 tablespoons more butter. Add the mushrooms and cook until their juices start to release, about 3 minutes.
6. Add the garlic and cook until fragrant, about 1 minute. Add the Marsala and scrape the bottom of the pan to remove any browned pieces. Add the broth and simmer until reduced by half and starting to thicken, about 15 minutes.
7. Add the cream and return the chicken to the sauce. Cook until the sauce thickens and the chicken is well-coated, 3 to 5 minutes.
