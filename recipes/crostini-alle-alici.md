## Ingredients

- 1 baguette
- 2 large fresh mozzarella balls
- 4 desalted and boned anchovies
- 50 grams butter
- salt
- pepper

## Method

1. Slice the baguette into finger thick slices. Brush lightly with olive oil, then in a non-stick pan over medium high heat, toast lightly.
2. Top each slice of crostini with a finger thick slice of mozzarella and place on a baking sheet. Bake in a 200°C oven until the cheese begins to melt and the crostini has deepened in colour.
3. While the crostini are toasting, melt the butter with the anchovies in a small pan. Do this gently until the anchovies have melted.
4. Remove the toasted crostini, arrange on a serving platter, and then drizzle with the anchovy butter.
