## Ingredients

- 1 cup grated Parmesan cheese
- 1 x 300 grams ricotta, crumbled
- 1 cup full fat milk
- 1/4 cup vegetable oil, plus 1 tablespoon for oiling your hands
- 2 eggs
- 500 grams tapioca flour (also known as tapioca starch)
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt

## Method

1. Prepare the dough: Preheat the oven to 175°C.
2. Add the tapioca flour and the salt to a large bowl, whisk to combine and set aside.
3. In a medium bowl, combine the cheeses and the eggs and set aside.
4. Add the milk and the oil to a small saucepan and bring to a simmer on the stovetop over medium heat.
5. When the liquid simmers, remove the pan from heat, and immediately pour the hot liquid into the tapioca mixture and, using a wooden spoon, stir to combine — the mixture will not be homogeneous, it'll be gummy and clumpy.
6. Wait 5-10 minutes until the tapioca mixture is cool to touch, and only then add the cheese and egg mixture to the tapioca mixture. Mix with a wooden spoon, or your hands, to combine. Now you should have a homogeneous mixture and it should come together as a ball. You'll still see some white tapioca specks in the dough — don't worry, that's totally normal!
7. Oil your hands, then scoop 1 to 2 tablespoon size portions, roll them into balls, and arrange them on a baking sheet approximately 2 inches apart from one another. Bake until golden brown, approximately 30-35 minutes.
