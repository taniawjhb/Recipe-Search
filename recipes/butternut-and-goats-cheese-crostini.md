## Ingredients

- 1 butternut squash (about 7 cups)
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 30-40 sage leaves, divided (20 leaves for roasting, the rest for frying for garnish)
- 2 teaspoons fresh thyme leaves, finely chopped
- 1 teaspoon dried rosemary
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 1 teaspoon paprika
- Sea salt and pepper to taste
- 1 baguette, thinly sliced (1-2 days old is the easiest to slice)
- 1 clove garlic (optional)
- 1 tablespoon butter
- Creamy spreadable goat cheese
- Fig balsamic glaze / or plain balsamic glaze

## Method

1. Start by preheating the oven to 230°C.
2. To make the fig vinaigrette, add the olive oil, fig preserves, balsamic glaze, and a little sea salt and pepper to a small jar and stir until combined. Set aside until ready to serve.
3. Peel the butternut squash, remove the seeds and chop into 1 centimetre bite-size pieces.
4. Place the butternut squash on a big baking pan or divide it between two to give it enough space to roast. Drizzle with the oil and toss to coat.
5. Finely chop about 20 sage leaves and add them to the butternut. Sprinkle with the rest of the spices - thyme, rosemary, minced garlic, paprika, sea salt, and pepper. Toss to coat evenly. Roast the butternut squash for 20 minutes, tossing at the 10 minutes mark.
6. While that's roasting, thinly slice the baguette. Lay the slices on a baking sheet in one layer and lightly brush with the oil on both sides.
7. Take the butternut squash out of the oven, lower the temperature to 200°C and toast the bread for about 10 minutes or until golden brown around the edges and crispy.
8. After you take the bread out of the oven, carefully rub each slice on one side with the clove of garlic for added flavor.
9. Lastly, heat a pan over high heat. Add the butter, when it melts add the sage leaves and fry till they change color and turn crispy. Be sure not to burn them. Take them out and place them on a napkin to soak up any excess butter.
10. Take a slice of bread, spread some goat cheese on it, add a few pieces of the squash, drizzle with the balsamic reduction, and top with a fried sage leaf. Best enjoyed immediately.
