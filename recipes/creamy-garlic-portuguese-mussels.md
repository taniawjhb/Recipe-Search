## Ingredients

- 1.5kg fresh mussels
- 3 tablespoons butter
- 3 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
- 1 onion, chopped fine
- 3 tablespoons fresh garlic, coarsely chopped
- 1 tablespoon chopped chillies
- 2 plum tomatoes, seeded and diced small
- Zest from half a lemon
- 1 tablespoon fresh thyme
- 2 tablespoons fresh parsley, minced, divided
- Juice from half a lemon
- 1 cup white wine
- 2 cups fish stock
- 2 tbsp butter (extra)
- 1 cup cream
- Crusty bread for serving

## Method

1. In a large pot, melt butter and olive oil over medium heat, then add onion and cook for 2 minutes.
2. Add garlic, chilli, chopped tomatoes, lemon zest, thyme and one tbsp parsley. Cook for 5 minutes.
3. Add lemon juice and stir mixture, then add white wine and cook till liquid is reduced by half.
4. Add stock, bring to a boil and reduce to a simmer. Simmer for five minutes.
5. Increase heat to medium high, add mussels, cover and cook for two to three minutes or until mussels open.
6. Remove from heat and remove mussels to a serving bowl using a spider or strainer, leaving liquid and solids behind in the pan.
7. Return the pan to the heat and add cream and cook over medium high for two minutes. Remove from heat and stir in the two tablespoons of butter.
8. Pour this mixture over the mussels and sprinkle the remaining parsley over the finished dish.
9. Serve hot with toasted French bread slices that have been rubbed with garlic. Discard any mussels that do not open.
