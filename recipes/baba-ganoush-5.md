## Ingredients

- 1 large aubergine
- 1/4 cup tahini
- 3 garlic cloves
- 1/4 cup fresh lemon juice
- 1 pinch ground cumin
- Salt, to taste
- 1 tablespoon extra virgin olive oil
- 1 tablespoon fresh flat-leaf parsley

## Method

1. Roast the eggplant: Pierce the eggplant with a fork, then bake at high heat for 25-40 minutes until blistered and soft.
2. Peel and mash: Let cool slightly, then remove and discard the skin. Mash the flesh with a fork into a smooth paste.
3. Mix in flavours: Stir in the tahini, minced garlic, lemon juice and cumin until well combined.
4. Adjust seasoning: Add salt to taste, and adjust with extra tahini or lemon juice if needed.
5. Finish and serve: Transfer to a serving bowl, create a shallow well, drizzle with olive oil and sprinkle with parsley.
