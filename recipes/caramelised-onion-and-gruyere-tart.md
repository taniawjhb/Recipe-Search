## Ingredients

### Pastry

- 175g plain flour
- 100g cold butter, cut into pieces
- 1 egg yolk

### Filling

- 4 large onions, thinly sliced
- 50g Gruyère
- 200ml carton crème fraîche
- 200ml double cream
- 3 large eggs, well beaten
- 3 sprigs of thyme
- Salt & pepper to taste

## Method

1. For the pastry, put the flour, butter, egg yolk and 4 tsp cold water into a food processor. Using the pulse button, process until the mix binds. Tip the pastry onto a lightly floured surface, gather into a smooth ball, then roll out as thinly as you can. Line a 23 x 2.5cm loose-bottomed, fluted flan tin, easing the pastry into the base. Trim the pastry edges with scissors (save any trimmings) so it sits slightly above the tin. Press the pastry into the flutes, lightly prick the base with a fork, then chill for 10 mins. Put a baking sheet in the oven and heat oven to 200C. Line the pastry case with baking paper, fill with dry beans and bake on the hot sheet for 15 mins. Remove paper and beans and bake for 4-5 mins more until the pastry is pale golden. If you notice any small holes or cracks, patch up with pastry trimmings. You can make up to this point a day ahead. While the pastry cooks, prepare the filling.
2. Meanwhile, melt the butter in a frying pan over medium heat and add the sliced onions and fresh thyme. Turn the heat to low and cook for 20-25 minutes until caramelised. Stir from time to time to make sure the onions caramelise evenly.
3. In a medium bowl, mix the eggs, double cream, crème fraîche and Gruyère. Season with salt and pepper.
4. Stir the onions into the egg mix and pour everything into the tart case. Sprinkle over the cheese and cook for 25 minutes or until set and golden brown.
5. Allow the tart to cool for five minutes before cutting into slices.
