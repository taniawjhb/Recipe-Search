## Ingredients

- 2 tablespoons unsalted butter or olive oil
- 1 pound gnocchi
- 10 spears asparagus, give or take, cut into 1 inch lengths
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 1/8 teaspoon red pepper flakes
- juice of 1/2 lemon
- 2 tablespoons cream
- 1 cup fresh or frozen peas
- 100 grams baby spinach (couple of big handfuls)
- salt and pepper, to taste

## Method

1. In a large pot or frying pan with a lid, heat the butter or oil on low heat.
2. Add the garlic and the red pepper flakes, and fry until just fragrant.
3. Now add the peas and asparagus and fry for 1 minute.
4. Add the lemon juice and cream and cook until slightly thickened.
5. Now add the baby spinach and allow to collapse before adjusting the seasoning.
6. Add the gnocchi and some of the reserved pasta water, toss to coat and serve.
