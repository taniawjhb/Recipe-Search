## Ingredients

- 8 cups vegetable stock
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 medium onion, diced
- 1 stick celery, finely chopped
- 1 carrot, finely chopped
- 200 grams diced pancetta
- 2 cups arborio rice
- 1 cup white wine
- 1 tablespoon flat leaf parsley, chopped
- 4 tablespoons cold butter
- 200 grams asparagus
- 150 grams grated pecorino
- Salt and pepper

## Method

1. Bring your vegetable stock to the boil, then blanch the asparagus spears briefly in it. Remove and set aside. Trim the bottom 1 centimetre from the asparagus and set aside to use halfway through the cooking of the risotto. Cut the remaining asparagus in half; the tips will be added at the very end. To make the asparagus puree, blend the middle third of the asparagus spears with a few tablespoons of stock in a small processor until completely smooth. Reserve.
2. Heat the oil in a large saucepan. Add the diced pancetta and fry until golden and crispy. Remove half the pancetta and reserve for later.
3. Add the onion, carrot and celery and season with salt and pepper.
4. Add the rice and stir to coat with the pork fat and oil; cook until the rice is slightly translucent. The rice should be hot and sizzling; stir carefully to ensure it does not stick.
5. Add the wine to the hot rice; the liquid should hiss and be quickly absorbed. Stir gently until almost all the liquid has cooked off. Add the hot stock mixture a ladleful at a time, stirring in the liquid each time.
6. Remember at this stage you are trying to coax the starches out of the rice by gently and continuously stirring. After about 10 minutes add the reserved asparagus trimmings.
7. Keep adding stock as needed so there is always a 1/2 centimetre liquid layer over the rice. After 15 to 20 minutes, the rice should be close to al dente.
8. At this stage add the reserved pancetta, asparagus, asparagus puree, cold butter and the finely grated cheese. Taste and adjust seasoning.
9. Cover the pan and allow it to stand for 2-3 minutes before serving.
