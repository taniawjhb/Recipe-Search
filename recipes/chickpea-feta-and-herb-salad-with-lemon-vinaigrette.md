## Ingredients

- 2 (15-ounce) cans chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- 1 large cucumber, diced
- 1 red bell pepper, diced
- 2 cups cherry tomatoes, halved
- ¼ cup red onion, diced
- 4 ounces feta cheese, crumbled
- ¼ cup finely chopped parsley

### Lemon dressing

- ⅓ cup olive oil
- ¼ cup lemon juice
- 1 teaspoon Dijon mustard
- ½ teaspoon honey, or maple syrup
- 1 garlic clove, minced
- Salt and pepper, to taste

## Method

1. Prepare all the salad ingredients. Toss all of the ingredients into a large mixing bowl.
2. To make your dressing, whisk all of the dressing ingredients in a bowl.
3. Combine the salad and the dressing, toss to coat and serve.
