## Ingredients

- 50 grams unsalted butter, divided
- 750 grams chicken thighs, skinless, boneless, cut into bite size pieces
- 1/4 teaspoon each black pepper and cooking salt

### Pie filling

- 80 grams streaky bacon, chopped into 1 centimetre
- 2 leeks
- 2 celery stems, sliced 4 millimetres thick (cut thick end in half lengthways first)
- 2 garlic cloves, finely minced
- 80 millilitres chardonnay or other dry white wine
- 35 grams flour (plain / all-purpose)
- 1 tablespoon Dijon mustard
- 2 thyme sprigs
- 2 bay leaves
- 250 millilitres chicken stock
- 250 millilitres heavy cream
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon black pepper

### Pie

- 2 x 20 centimetres frozen butter puff pastry sheets, or single sheet large enough to cover baking dish, 360 grams
- 1 egg, yolk and whites separated, lightly whisked

## Method

1. Trim the reedy dark green ends and root off the leeks, then cut in half. Cut each piece in half lengthways. Remove the outer 5 to 6 layers (keep them together), lay them flat, cut in half lengthways then into 1.8 centimetres squares. Cut the inner core into 2 centimetres pieces.

### Filling

1. Melt half the butter in a large pan over high heat. Add the chicken, salt and pepper. Cook until the surface changes from pink to white (inside still raw), then remove into a bowl using a slotted spoon. Leave residual butter and chicken juices in the pan.
2. In the same pan, cook the bacon for 1 minute. Add the leek, celery and garlic. Cook for 5 minutes until the celery is softened. Don't let the leek brown – lower the heat if needed.
3. Add the wine and simmer rapidly on high heat, stirring, until it is mostly evaporated.
4. Lower the heat to medium. Add the remaining butter. Once it melts, sprinkle the flour across the surface (don't dump in one place) and stir for 1 minute.
5. While stirring, slowly pour the stock in. The mixture will thicken into a thick sauce once combined. Then add the mustard, cream, thyme, bay leaves, salt and pepper. Stir to combine, then add the chicken including all the juices accumulated in the bowl.
6. Increase the heat, stir to combine. Once it comes to a simmer, reduce the heat to medium high. Simmer for 5 minutes, stirring regularly, or until the sauce is a thick creamy consistency you want in the finished pie. Remove the pan from the stove and cool for 1 hour.

### Assembling and baking

1. Preheat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan-forced). Use a 1.5 litre baking pan.
2. Thaw the puff pastry. Cut out the pie lid (use the dish as a cutting guide).
3. Fill the pie dish with the cooled filling and smooth the surface. Brush the pastry strips on the rim with egg white (if not using strips, don't do this step) then cover with the puff pastry lid.
4. Brush the lid with egg yolk. Cut a small X in the middle.
5. Bake 45 minutes or until the surface is a deep golden brown. Rest 5 minutes then serve!
