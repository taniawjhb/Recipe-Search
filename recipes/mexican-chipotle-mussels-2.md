## Ingredients

- 1 kilogram fresh mussels
- 1 medium onion, finely chopped
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 2 tomatoes, chopped
- 1 jalapeno, deseeded and chopped (optional)
- 1/2 tablespoon. olive oil
- 1 teaspoon. chipotle paste
- 1 x 330 millilitres bottle of Corona or Sol beer
- Zest and juice of 1 or 2 limes, plus extra wedges to serve
- Crusty bread, to serve

## Method

1. Heat 1 tablespoon oil and add the onions and fry until just softened, now add the chopped jalapeno and garlic, cook for 2 minutes until fragrant.
2. Now add the chipotle paste and the chopped tomato and cook for 1-2 minutes.
3. Now add the beer and bring to a simmer, cook for 2 minutes before adding the mussels and covering with a tight fitting lid.
4. Stir in the lime zest and juice and check the seasoning. Divide among 4 soup bowls and serve with extra lime wedges and crusty bread to mop up the juices.
