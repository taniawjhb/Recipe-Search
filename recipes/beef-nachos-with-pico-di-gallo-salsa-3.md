## Ingredients

- 4 ripe tomatoes, seeded and chopped
- 1 jalapeno pepper, seeded and finely chopped, for medium to hot heat level
- 1 small red onion, chopped
- 1/4 cup (2 handfuls) cilantro leaves, finely chopped (substitute parsley if cilantro is not to your liking)
- 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil
- 500g beef mince
- 2-3 cloves garlic, chopped
- 1 onion, chopped
- 1 jalapeno pepper, seeded and chopped
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 2 teaspoons dark chili powder
- 2 teaspoons ground cumin, half a palmful
- 2 teaspoons paprika
- 1 can black beans, 400g, drained
- 1-2 cups cooked tomato sauce (sub for canned chopped tomato and extend cook time)
- 2 tablespoons butter
- 2 tablespoons all-purpose flour
- 1 1/2 - 2 cups milk
- 300gms gouda, shredded, about 2 1/2 cups
- 2 varieties of corn chips

## Method

1. Arrange a mixture of 2 varieties of corn chips on a very large platter or use your broiler pan as a platter.
2. Combine salsa ingredients (tomatoes, finely chopped jalapeno, red onion, cilantro and olive oil) in a bowl and set aside for flavors to marry.
3. Heat a medium nonstick skillet over medium high heat. Add oil, garlic, onion and peppers to the pan and saute 2 minutes, then add meat and crumble with wooden spoon; allow to brown. Season meat with salt, chili powder, cumin and paprika. Add tomato.
4. Cook meat 5 minutes, then stir in beans and reduce heat to low.
5. In a medium sauce pot, melt butter and add flour to it. Cook flour and butter 1 to 2 minutes over moderate heat, then whisk in milk. When milk comes to a bubble, switch off. Stir in cheese with a wooden spoon. Remove cheese sauce from the heat.
6. Pour cheese sauce evenly over the massive spread of chips and top evenly with beef and beans and the pico de gallo. Serve immediately as is or garnish with your choice of extra toppings.
