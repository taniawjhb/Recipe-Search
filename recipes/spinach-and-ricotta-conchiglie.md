## Ingredients

- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 small onion, finely chopped
- 4 garlic cloves, finely minced
- 1 bay leaf, fresh (or dried)
- 1/2 teaspoon dried thyme
- 1/2 teaspoon dried oregano
- 100 grams tomato paste
- 700 grams passata
- 85 millilitres white wine
- 1 litre vegetable stock, low sodium
- 3/4 teaspoon salt, or more as needed
- 1 1/2 teaspoons sugar, if needed
- 1/3 teaspoon black pepper
- 500 grams fresh chopped spinach
- 500 grams ricotta, full fat
- 50 grams parmesan, finely grated
- 100 grams grated mozzarella
- 1 egg
- 1 large garlic clove, pasted
- Grated fresh nutmeg (just a sprinkling) or 1/8 teaspoon nutmeg powder (optional)
- 3/4 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
- 250 grams jumbo conchiglie
- 150 grams grated mozzarella
- 50 grams grated parmesan
- Fresh basil
- Extra parmesan, for garnish (optional)

## Method

1. Heat the oil in a small pot over medium-high heat. Add the garlic, onion, bay leaf, thyme and oregano. Cook for 3-4 minutes until the onion is translucent. Add the tomato paste and cook for 1 minute.
2. Add the wine, increase the heat to high and let it simmer rapidly until mostly evaporated.
3. Add the passata, stock, sugar, salt and pepper. Stir then simmer on low, uncovered, for 20 minutes. Use while hot.
4. For the filling, sauté the chopped spinach with a little oil to wilt down, then remove and squeeze to remove excess liquid. Cool then proceed. Place the spinach in a bowl with the remaining filling ingredients (ricotta, parmesan, mozzarella, egg, garlic, nutmeg, salt and pepper). Mix well.
5. Preheat the oven to 200°C or 180°C fan. Stuff the uncooked shells with the spinach ricotta filling. Stuff them full.
6. Pour the hot tomato sauce into a 23x33 centimetre dish. Gently place the stuffed shells in; most will be submerged, some may poke above the surface.
7. Cover with foil or a baking tray then bake for 70 minutes. Check the shells are cooked; they should be al dente (if not, return to the oven, covered).
8. Sprinkle with the mozzarella then parmesan. Bake 15 minutes until melted and just starting to go golden. Garnish with fresh basil and extra parmesan if desired.
