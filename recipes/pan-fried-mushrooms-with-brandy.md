## Ingredients

- 250 grams Portabelini or wild mushrooms
- 1 clove garlic, finely minced
- 2 tablespoons butter
- 3 tablespoons olive oil
- 60 millilitres brandy or sherry
- 1/4 teaspoon finely chopped red chili
- 1 teaspoon fresh thyme leaves
- 1 tablespoon finely chopped flat leaf parsley
- 2 tablespoons cold butter, cut into pieces
- 250 millilitres cream (optional)

## Method

1. In a pan add the olive oil, butter, garlic, thyme and chili then bring the pan up to heat until the thyme is popping and the garlic fragrant.
2. Now add the sliced mushrooms (brush and remove tough parts first).
3. Cook the mushrooms for a few minutes, add a crack of salt to help draw the moisture from the mushrooms.
4. Once the liquid from the mushrooms has evaporated and the mushrooms are cooked add the brandy and burn off the alcohol. Add the cream, if using, then add the flat leaf parsley. Now add the cold butter and stir through before serving.
