## Ingredients

- 250 grams green beans (French beans)
- 1/2 red onion, finely chopped
- 1 clove garlic, finely minced
- 1/2 teaspoon cumin seeds (jeera)
- 1 green chilli, finely chopped
- 1 teaspoon red chilli powder
- 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder (haldi)
- 1 teaspoon coriander powder (dhania)
- oil, as required
- salt, as required

## Method

1. To prepare green beans fry, clean the beans and chop them finely, chop the red onion and garlic and set aside.
2. Heat oil in a heavy bottomed pan and add the onion and cook until starting to colour and soften, now add the cumin seeds. Saute for 15 to 20 seconds.
3. After 15 to 20 seconds, add green chillies and beans.
4. Add salt and turmeric powder and let it cook for 3 to 5 minutes.
5. After 3 to 5 minutes, add in the red chilli powder and coriander powder.
6. Cover the lid and cook it till the beans become soft and cooked; if necessary add a tablespoon or two of water.
7. Keep stirring in between so that the beans don't get burned.
8. Once done, switch off the stove and serve with boiled/steamed rice and mixed vegetable sambar to make a wholesome meal.
