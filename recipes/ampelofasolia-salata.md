## Ingredients

- Water
- 450 grams green beans, trimmed
- Salt and pepper, to taste
- Crushed red pepper flakes, optional, to taste
- 100 grams quality crumbled feta cheese, to taste
- 2 tablespoons (or more to your liking) pitted olives, chopped
- Handful of cherry tomatoes, cut in half

For dressing

- 2 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
- 2 tablespoons freshly squeezed lemon juice
- 1 to 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 tablespoons chopped fresh dill
- 1/2 teaspoon sweet Spanish paprika
- Salt and pepper, to taste

## Method

1. Prepare dressing. In a small bowl, whisk together extra virgin olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, fresh dill, Spanish paprika, pinch of salt and pepper. Set aside for now.
2. Bring a large pot filled with water to a boil. Add green beans. Cook for 5 minutes, then, keeping the pot of boiling water going, use tongs to transfer green beans to an ice bath (a bowl of ice and ice cold water) for 5 minutes. Return beans to boiling water to finish cooking until tender, about 10 more minutes or so. Drain well.
3. Transfer warm beans to a mixing bowl. Add the chopped tomatoes, toss with a little salt and pepper. Give the dressing a quick stir to combine well, then pour over the green beans. Toss again to coat.
4. Transfer to a serving platter. Sprinkle crushed pepper flakes and crumbled feta. Top with chopped olives. Serve at room temperature.
