## Ingredients

- 710 millilitres whole milk
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- 1 strip lemon peel
- 6 egg yolks
- 150gms granulated white sugar
- 45gms all purpose flour
- 60 grams dark chocolate, chopped
- 90 millilitres Amaretto (for assembly)
- 2 tablespoons water (for assembly)
- 18 savoiardi cookies or Madeira cake
- 200 millilitres cream, cold (optional)
- 100gms flaked almonds, toasted till lightly golden

## Method

1. Place the vanilla, milk, and a strip of lemon rind in a medium sized pot. Heat over low heat until the milk is just before simmering point. Don't boil.
2. In a bowl whisk egg yolks, granulated white sugar and all purpose flour until smooth. Take care that there are no lumps. Remove lemon rind from the milk.
3. Slowly pour the hot milk onto the egg yolk mixture, whisking constantly. Strain the mixture back to the pot and place over medium low heat. Whisk constantly until the mixture comes to a boil and thickens. Simmer for 1 minute to cook the flour.
4. Divide the pastry cream between two heat proof bowls. Cover one bowl with plastic wrap ensuring the plastic is in complete contact with the pastry cream. Add the chopped chocolate to the second bowl and stir until the chocolate has melted and is thoroughly combined. Cover with plastic wrap ensuring the plastic is in complete contact with the chocolate pastry cream. Refrigerate until thoroughly chilled. Before using, stir until smooth.
5. To assemble, combine the amaretto with the water in a medium bowl. Cut the savoiardi in half and dip quickly but completely into the amaretto mixture. If using cake, slice 1 centimetre thick and line your serving dish, then brush generously with the amaretto mixture.
6. Layer a third of your cake or biscuits at the bottom of the serving bowl. Top with your chocolate pastry cream followed by a second layer of soaked biscuits or cake, then your vanilla pastry cream and a final layer of soaked cake / biscuits. Cover with plastic wrap and refrigerate for at least 2 hours to set.

## Notes

Optional: Serve topped with slightly sweetened whipped cream and toasted flaked almonds.
