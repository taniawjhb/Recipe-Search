## Ingredients

### Chicken

- 6 large chicken breasts
- 2 cloves of garlic
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 2 teaspoons soy sauce
- 1/2 teaspoon ground black pepper
- 1 teaspoon paprika
- 1 teaspoon finely chopped fresh thyme
- 250 millilitres buttermilk
- 6 tablespoons cake flour
- 4 tablespoons corn flour
- 3 eggs
- 300 grams golden bread crumbs
- Sunflower oil, for shallow frying

### Fiery Dipping Mayo

- 8 tablespoons good mayo
- 2 tablespoons chili sauce, Tabasco or any chili hot sauce
- 2 teaspoons lemon/lime juice
- 1/2 teaspoon soy sauce (optional)

## Method

1. Cut the chicken into equal sized strips. In a large bowl add all the remaining ingredients for the chicken to the buttermilk. Add the chicken strips to the buttermilk mixture and set aside for about 20 minutes.
2. Whilst the chicken is in the marinade prepare your mayonnaise: in a bowl, whisk together the mayo, chili sauce, lemon/lime juice and soy sauce until smooth.
3. To prepare the fry coating, place the eggs in a flat bowl and whisk lightly. In a second dish add the cornflour, flour and season well with salt and pepper. In a third dish add the bread crumbs.
4. In a large frying pan add enough sunflower oil to shallow fry the chicken strips. Place the oil over a medium high heat.
5. Remove each strip of chicken from the marinade, and gently lay them one at a time into the flour mixture. Dust generously on both sides and then dip into the egg wash before pressing gently into the bread crumbs. If the strips have too much buttermilk you can remove a little with your fingers.
6. To test the oil is ready add a few bread crumbs, they should fizzle. Now carefully add the strips one at a time into the hot oil, allow time for the strip to brown before turning and finishing cooking. Place the strips on some paper towel before keeping warm in the oven.
7. Serve with the hot dipping mayo.
