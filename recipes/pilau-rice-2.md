## Ingredients

- 60gms butter
- 2 cup basmati rice
- 2 teaspoon turmeric
- 2 bay leaf
- 6 green cardamom pods
- 8 cloves
- 1 teaspoon fennel seed
- 2 teaspoon salt
- 3 cups boiling water

## Method

1. Melt the butter in a saucepan.
2. Add the spices and toast.
3. Stir in the rice and salt, stir until it is coated in the butter.
4. Add the boiling water.
5. Cover with a tight fitting lid, and simmer on the lowest heat for 15 minutes -- do not stir in the meantime.
