## Ingredients

- 3-4 tablespoons oil
- 3 Thai bird chillies (deseeded if desired and thinly sliced)
- 3 shallots, thinly sliced
- 5 cloves garlic, sliced
- 500 grams minced chicken
- 2 teaspoons sugar (or honey)
- 2 tablespoons soy sauce
- 1 tablespoon fish sauce
- 1/3 cup chicken stock (or water)
- 1 bunch holy or Thai basil leaves

## Method

1. In a wok over high heat, add the oil, chillies, shallots and garlic, and fry for 1-2 minutes.
2. Add the minced chicken and stir-fry for 2 minutes, breaking up the chicken into small bits.
3. Add the sugar, soy sauce and fish sauce. Stir-fry for another minute and deglaze the pan with the broth. Because the pan is over high heat, the liquid should cook off very quickly.
4. Add the basil and stir-fry until wilted. Serve over rice.
