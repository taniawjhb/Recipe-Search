## Ingredients

- 250 grams mango chunks fresh or frozen
- 1 tablespoon light brown sugar
- Pulp of 2 or 3 passion fruit, depending on size
- juice of 1/2 lime
- juice of 1/2 lemon

## Method

1. In a small saucepan, mix the mango, sugar, lime and lemon juice. Cook over a medium heat, allowing it to bubble and reduce, for 10 minutes, stirring often.
2. Remove from the heat and stir in the passion fruit pulp.

## Notes

Can be served warm or cold on waffles, pancakes, ice cream, yogurt, granola etc.
