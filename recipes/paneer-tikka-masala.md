## Ingredients

- 2 tablespoons ghee (clarified butter)
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tablespoon ground cumin
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 teaspoon ground ginger
- 1 teaspoon Kashmiri chilli powder
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1/4 teaspoon ground turmeric
- 1 (14 ounce) can tomato sauce
- 1 cup heavy whipping cream
- 2 teaspoons paprika
- 1 tablespoon white sugar
- 1 tablespoon vegetable oil
- 1 clove garlic, crushed
- 1 teaspoon crushed ginger
- 500 grams paneer, cut into bite-size chunks
- 1 teaspoon curry masala powder
- 1/2 teaspoon salt, or to taste (optional)
- 1 teaspoon white sugar, or to taste (optional)

## Method

1. Heat ghee in a large skillet over medium heat, add the onion and cook for about 5 minutes.
2. Stir in garlic; cook until fragrant, about 1 minute.
3. Stir cumin, 1 teaspoon salt, ginger, cayenne pepper, cinnamon and turmeric into the onion mixture; fry until fragrant, about 2 minutes.
4. Stir tomato sauce into the onion and spice mixture, bring to a boil, and reduce heat to low. Simmer sauce for 10 minutes, then mix in cream (yoghurt), paprika and 1 tablespoon sugar. Bring sauce back to a simmer and cook, stirring often, until sauce is thickened, 10 to 15 minutes.
5. Add the crushed garlic and ginger to a bowl, add the masala powder, salt, sugar and a few tablespoons of oil. Make a paste. Coat the paneer with it, then thread onto skewers.
6. To cook, heat your grill/braai to high then cook the paneer until slightly charred. Once charred, add to the curry.
7. Add the grilled paneer and cook for a further 15 to 20 minutes.
