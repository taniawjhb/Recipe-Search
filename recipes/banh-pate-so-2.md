## Ingredients

- 700g puff pastry
- ¾ cup chopped shallot or red onion
- 1 tablespoon minced garlic
- 300g ground pork
- 15g woodear or shiitake mushrooms, finely minced
- 1 spring onion, finely chopped
- 1 tbsp finely chopped coriander (optional)
- 1 tsp finely grated ginger
- 1 tablespoon sugar
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 teaspoon freshly ground pepper
- 1 large egg, lightly beaten for sealing and brushing

## Method

1. Cut the puff pastry into 9cm rounds. You will need 24 rounds. Place them on a lined baking sheet and refrigerate until ready to use.
2. Finely chop the shallots or red onions and fry until tender in a little neutral oil. Once the onions are tender add the grated ginger and garlic and fry for 1 minute, remove and set aside.
3. Mix the sautéed shallot mixture with ground pork, chopped mushrooms, sugar, salt, freshly ground pepper and the finely chopped spring onion and coriander if using.
4. Take 2 tablespoons of pork mixture and place it in the center of a puff pastry round.
5. Brush the edges with beaten egg, place another pastry round on top. Use a fork to press around the edges of the pastry to seal.
6. Place the unbaked pate so on a lined baking sheet and freeze for 15 minutes.
7. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
8. Brush the pastries with egg. Place the tray on the middle rack and bake for 25 minutes until golden brown.
