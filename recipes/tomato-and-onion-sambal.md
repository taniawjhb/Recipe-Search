## Ingredients

- 4 large tomato, roughly diced
- 2 onion, diced
- 3 sprig spring onion, finely chopped
- 4 teaspoons chives, finely chopped
- 4 teaspoon lemon juice
- A few sprigs of freshly chopped coriander
- 3 green chillies, sliced
- Salt to taste

## Method

1. Combine the diced tomato into a large salad bowl. Add in the finely chopped onion.
2. Stir in the sliced spring onion.
3. Toss in the thinly chopped chives, coriander and green chillies.
4. Stir in the salt and lemon juice.
5. Mix well to ensure an even distribution of seasoning.

## Notes

Serve chilled as an accompaniment to any curry. A cucumber-free version omits the chives and uses a single diced onion.
