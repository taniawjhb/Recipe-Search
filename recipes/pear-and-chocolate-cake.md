## Ingredients

- 4 large, very firm and hard pears
- 170 grams "00" flour
- 30 grams bitter cocoa powder
- 170 grams sugar
- 70 grams sunflower seed oil or melted butter
- 2 eggs
- 125 grams berry yogurt (or classic plain yogurt) at room temperature
- 130 grams dark chocolate (in flakes or drops)
- 1 sachet of vanilla or 1 teaspoon of extract
- 1 sachet of baking powder

## Method

1. To start beat the eggs, sugar and vanilla for 2 minutes in a stand mixer with a whisk attachment, until the mixture becomes frothy and light. Then with your machine running add the oil in a thin stream.
2. In a second bowl, sift the flour, baking powder and cocoa and then set aside.
3. Now add a third of the flour mixture to the egg mixture and stir until all the flour has been absorbed, then add half of the yoghurt mixture and stir until combined.
4. Repeat with the last half of the flour and then the balance of the yoghurt. Finally add the chocolate pieces, reserving a few to use on top.
5. Now peel your pears and cut them into 3 millimetre thick slices, and set aside, it is important that the pears are firm.
6. Now add your cake batter into a greased and lined 24 centimetre baking tin.
7. Now add the sliced pears into the batter, place them in upright so that they create interest, some slices can be pushed all the way into the batter and others you can keep sticking out partially.
8. The trick to creating a beautiful design of pear and chocolate cake is to not push the pears below the surface.
9. Finally add the reserved chocolate pieces over the top.
10. Bake in a preheated oven at 180°C for 40-45 minutes. The time is indicative and may vary depending on the oven!
11. The pear and chocolate cake is ready when it has risen and a toothpick inserted comes out dry!
