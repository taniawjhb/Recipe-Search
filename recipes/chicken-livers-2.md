## Ingredients

- 250g chicken livers
- Seasoned flour for dusting (salt, pepper, paprika)
- 1/2 onion diced
- 2 cloves garlic
- Dry red chili
- 75ml white wine
- 100ml cream
- 2 tbsp fresh chopped parsley
- Olive oil
- Thyme
- Salt & pepper

## Method

1. Rinse and clean the livers, then pat dry.
2. Lightly dust the livers in the seasoned flour (1 tbsp flour; 1 tsp paprika, salt & pepper to taste).
3. Add olive oil to the pan and brown livers on medium-high heat, until edges are crisped but liver still rare.
4. Remove livers and set aside, then add onion and minced garlic to the pan. Cook until softened over medium heat.
5. Add the white wine to the pan and cook over medium-high heat until wine begins to reduce.
6. Add the cream and a sprinkling of thyme, then add the livers back to the pan and cook until the sauce thickens and the livers are just cooked.
7. Check and adjust seasoning and sprinkle with parsley.
8. Serve immediately with some sliced baguette.
