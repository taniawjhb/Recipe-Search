## Ingredients

- 5 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
- 1 medium yellow onion, chopped
- 3 garlic cloves, minced, divided
- 2-3 carrots, peeled and chopped
- 1 medium russet potato, peeled and small diced or chopped
- Kosher salt
- 2 tablespoons tomato paste
- 6 cups low-sodium vegetable broth
- 2 teaspoons Aleppo pepper, divided
- 1 teaspoon cumin
- 1/2 teaspoon coriander
- 1 cup red lentils, picked over and rinsed
- 1 large lemon, cut into wedges

## Method

1. Sauté the vegetables: Heat the extra virgin olive oil in a Dutch oven over medium-high. Add the onions, garlic, carrots and potatoes, season with salt, and cook 5-7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until softened.
2. Build the base: Stir in the tomato paste to coat the vegetables, then add the broth, Aleppo pepper and spices. Mix in the lentils and bring to a rolling boil for 4-5 minutes.
3. Simmer: Reduce the heat, cover partially and let simmer 15-20 minutes, stirring occasionally until the lentils and vegetables are fully tender. If too thick, add 1/2-1 cup more stock.
4. Blend: Remove from the heat and blend with an immersion blender until smooth. If using a standard blender, work in batches, allowing steam to escape.
5. Finish: In a small skillet, warm extra virgin olive oil over medium heat. Sauté the remaining Aleppo pepper and garlic until fragrant and golden. Pour over the soup.
6. Serve: Garnish with lemon wedges and enjoy.
