## Ingredients

- 1 kilogram prawns
- 100 millilitres extra virgin olive oil
- 4 cloves garlic, finely pasted
- 1 tablespoon chopped parsley
- 2 teaspoons chili powder
- 2 teaspoons paprika
- 60 millilitres white wine
- Salt and pepper to taste

## Method

1. Start by putting the oil in a bowl, then add the white wine and a pinch of salt and pepper. Emulsify everything with a whisk. Then add the pasted garlic, paprika, chili powder and chopped parsley.
2. Arrange the prawns in a baking dish and sprinkle them with the emulsion you have prepared. Let it stand for about 30-40 minutes, or just until the embers are ready to start grilling.
3. Place the prawns on the hot grill, then after about 3 minutes turn them onto the other side to cook them evenly.
4. Cooking times of grilled prawns can vary depending on the intensity of the heat and the height at which the grill is placed. Cooking is usually very short and never lasts more than 7-8 minutes.
5. As soon as the prawns are evenly browned, remove them from the grill and place them in dishes or on a tray.
