## Ingredients

- 70 grams small red chillies
- 5 garlic cloves, lightly crushed
- 70 millilitres olive oil
- 2 tablespoons red wine vinegar
- 2 1/2 teaspoons tomato puree
- 2 1/2 teaspoons caster sugar
- 1/2 teaspoon dry chilli powder
- 1 heaped tbsp sweet paprika
- 2 teaspoons fine salt
- pinch dry oregano

## Method

1. Preheat oven to 180°C. Split chillies lengthways and place them in a small pan or roasting tray with the garlic and olive oil. Roast for 10 minutes, or cook over low heat for 10 minutes, to not allow the garlic to brown.
2. Leave to cool for a few minutes, then transfer to a food processor. Add the remaining ingredients for the marinade and blend till smooth (if using a mortar and pestle start by crushing the roasted chilli and garlic with the salt, before adding the balance of dry spices, alternating with the wet).

## Notes

Use as a marinade for prawns.
