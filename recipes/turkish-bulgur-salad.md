## Ingredients

- 2 cups bulgur wheat, dry (I used Turkish bulgur)
- 3 cups boiling water
- 1/4 cup tomato paste
- 2 tablespoons roasted red pepper paste
- 1 teaspoon salt, or more to taste
- 1/4 cup olive oil (I used Turkish olive oil)
- 1 cup cucumber, diced
- 1 red bell pepper, diced
- 3/4 cup green onion, chopped
- 1/3 cup fresh parsley, chopped
- 3/4 teaspoon cumin powder or 7 spice
- 1 lemon, juiced
- 2 tablespoons pomegranate molasses
- 1/3 cup purple onion (optional)
- 2 tablespoons jalapeño pepper (optional, if you like spicy)

## Method

1. In a bowl, soak the bulgur with the boiling water for 20-30 minutes, mixing occasionally until soft and tender. Bulgur should not be chewy.
2. Meanwhile, dice and chop all your ingredients.
3. In a frying pan bring the olive oil, salt, tomato and pepper paste to a simmer and keep cooking on low for about 5-7 minutes, stirring occasionally to form a creamy tomato paste base. Turn off the heat.
4. Add the bulgur and mix to coat well with the tomato paste. Allow the bulgur to cool down for about 10 minutes.
5. In a bowl, add all the salad ingredients with the bulgur, pomegranate molasses, lemon juice and cumin. Taste and add more salt if needed.
6. Serve right away or cool in the fridge to serve cold.
