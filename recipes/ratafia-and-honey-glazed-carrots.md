## Ingredients

- 120 grams butter, divided
- 6 carrots, peeled and cut into thick circles
- 1/4-1/3 cup ratafia
- 1/3 cup honey
- 1/2 to 1 teaspoon salt
- Freshly ground pepper, to taste
- Chives

## Method

1. Melt 1 tablespoon butter in a large skillet over high heat. Add the carrots in two batches, cooking for 60-90 seconds each batch. Remove from the skillet.
2. Pour in the ratafia and allow to evaporate 30 seconds. Reduce the heat to medium, and add the remaining butter. When the butter melts, drizzle over the honey. Stir together, then add the carrots to the skillet. Cover, and continue cooking for 5 minutes.
3. Remove the lid and add salt and pepper. Continue cooking until the carrots are done and the glaze is thick, about 5 more minutes.
4. Pour onto a platter and serve immediately. Sprinkle with chopped chives if desired.
