## Ingredients

- 500 grams green zucchini
- 500 grams yellow zucchini
- 5 tablespoons (100 millilitres) extra virgin olive oil
- juice of 1 small lemon
- 2 teaspoons grated lemon rind
- 225 grams baby rocket
- 100 grams shaved parmesan

## Method

1. Trim the ends of the zucchini and slice diagonally into wafer-thin rounds. Place in a bowl.
2. Combine the oil, lemon juice and rind, and season with salt and pepper. Pour the mixture over the zucchini and leave for 5 minutes. Pile the rocket onto serving plates, then top with the zucchini and finish with the parmesan.
3. Serve as a first course or as an accompaniment to simply grilled meat and fish.
