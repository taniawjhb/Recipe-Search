## Ingredients

- 4 small pineapples, peeled, cut into rings and cored
- 3 tablespoons brown sugar
- 60gms butter
- 2 tablespoons vanilla rum

## Method

1. To start, peel the pineapples, then carefully remove any hard core. Now sprinkle both sides of the sliced pineapples with the brown sugar.
2. Then in a non stick frying pan, heat the butter until foaming, add the pineapple slices and cook for two minutes a side or until golden brown, flip and repeat. Remove and set aside in a serving platter.
3. Repeat until all the pineapple is cooked (if you require extra butter you can add). Once all the pineapple is cooked add the rum and a little extra butter to the pan, bring to the boil and then pour over pineapple slices.
4. Serve warm with some yoghurt.
