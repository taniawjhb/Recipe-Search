## Ingredients

- 3 avocados
- juice of 1 lemon
- salt, to taste
- cayenne pepper, to taste
- mixed lettuce, to serve
- 400 grams shrimps, boiled
- 50 millilitres fresh flat-leaf parsley, finely chopped
- 100 grams good quality mayonnaise
- 50 millilitres tomato sauce
- 50 millilitres crème fraiche
- 20 millilitres brandy
- juice of 1/2 lemon
- finely ground black pepper, to taste

## Method

1. To start roughly chop your iceberg lettuce and arrange on a serving plate.
2. Cut your avocado in half, deseed it and then peel it. Slice the avocado into slices, drizzle with lemon juice to prevent it from darkening, season with a pinch of cayenne, salt and pepper - arrange on the serving platter over the lettuce.
3. Arrange the blanched shrimp over the avocado, then season with a pinch of cayenne (paprika), salt and pepper.
4. To make your seafood sauce add all ingredients to a bowl and whisk well to combine.
5. Drizzle the sauce sparingly over the prawns, garnish with curly parsley and serve immediately.
