## Ingredients

- 1.3 kilograms fresh tomatoes (mix of heirloom, cherry, vine and plum tomatoes)
- 6 cloves garlic, peeled
- 2 small yellow onions, sliced
- Vine cherry tomatoes for garnish, optional
- 1/2 cup extra-virgin olive oil
- Salt and freshly ground black pepper
- 1 quart chicken stock
- 2 bay leaves
- 4 tablespoons butter
- 1/2 cup chopped fresh basil leaves, optional
- 3/4 cup heavy cream, optional

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 220°C. Wash, core and cut the tomatoes into halves. Spread the tomatoes, garlic cloves and onions onto a baking tray. If using vine cherry tomatoes for garnish, add them as well, leaving them whole and on the vine.
2. Drizzle with 1/2 cup of olive oil and season with salt and pepper. Roast for 20 to 30 minutes, or until caramelised.
3. Remove the roasted tomatoes, garlic and onion from the oven and transfer to a large stock pot (set aside the roasted vine tomatoes for later). Add 3/4 of the chicken stock, bay leaves, and butter. Bring to a boil, reduce heat and simmer for 15 to 20 minutes or until the liquid has reduced by a third.
4. Wash and dry the basil leaves, if using, and add to the pot. Use an immersion blender to purée the soup until smooth.
5. Return the soup to low heat, add the cream and adjust consistency with the remaining chicken stock, if necessary. Season to taste with salt and freshly ground black pepper.
6. Garnish each bowl with 3 or 4 roasted vine cherry tomatoes and a splash of heavy cream.
