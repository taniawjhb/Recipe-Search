## Ingredients

- 400g fresh strawberries
- 150g granulated sugar
- 250ml whole milk
- 250ml heavy cream

## Method

1. To make strawberry gelato at home, start by washing and drying the strawberries thoroughly. Remove the stalk and, if present, any damaged or unripe parts.
2. Cut the strawberries into pieces and put them in a blender or in the container of an immersion blender and blend well until you have a smooth puree. Check that no large chunks remain.
3. Pour the strawberry puree into a bowl and add the sugar.
4. Add the heavy cream and finally the whole milk. Mix thoroughly with a spoon until the sugar is completely dissolved.
5. Pour the mixture into the basket of the ice cream maker, close and start it. After about 40 minutes (the time may vary depending on the model of the machine) your strawberry gelato will be ready. Sweet, fresh and creamy — you just have to taste it!
