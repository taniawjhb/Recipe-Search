## Ingredients

- 300 grams "00" flour
- 3 large eggs (free range)
- 30 millilitres olive oil
- Pinch of salt

## Method

1. Put the flour on a work surface and make a well in the center. Break the eggs into the well, adding your pinch of salt and the olive oil. Using a fork, start whisking the eggs and oil together, incorporating a little flour as you do. Slowly blend the flour and eggs, working from the center outwards.
2. Knead the dough on a lightly floured surface for 6 minutes, or until the dough is smooth, soft, elastic but dry to the touch. If sticky, add a little more flour. Once the correct texture is reached, cover in plastic wrap and allow to rest for 30 minutes before rolling out.
