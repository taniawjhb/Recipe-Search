## Ingredients

- 240 grams dried rice vermicelli noodles
- 2 deboned chicken fillets, sliced finely across the grain
- 1 large yellow onion, quartered
- 2 pieces (5 centimetres each) fresh ginger, unpeeled and halved lengthwise
- 6 cups chicken stock
- 2 cups water
- 1/2 teaspoon ground coriander
- 2 whole cloves (optional)
- 3 tablespoons fish sauce (or less, to taste)
- 1/2 teaspoon hoisin sauce
- 1/2 teaspoon soy sauce
- 1 teaspoon red chilli paste (sambal oelek)
- 1 cinnamon stick
- Salt and freshly ground black pepper

## Method

1. Place a large dry pot over medium heat and add the onion halves and ginger pieces. Cook for 4 minutes, stirring occasionally.
2. Add the stock, water, coriander, cloves, fish sauce, hoisin sauce, soy sauce, chilli paste, cinnamon stick and a pinch of fresh cracked salt and pepper. Bring to a slow boil, then reduce the heat and simmer for 30 minutes.
3. Meanwhile, prepare the noodles according to the package instructions. Once soft, drain, rinse and set aside.
4. Ready the garnishes in individual small bowls for serving: cilantro, basil, green onions, bean sprouts, lime wedges and sliced chillies.
5. One to five minutes before the broth is done cooking, add the finely sliced chicken to the broth to cook, just until done.
6. Discard the ginger, cloves, cinnamon stick and onion pieces from the pot.
7. Divide the noodles among bowls, ladle the broth on top and add the flaked chicken. Add the desired toppings.
