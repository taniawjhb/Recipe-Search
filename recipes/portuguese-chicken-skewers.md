## Ingredients

- 600 grams deboned chicken thighs, cut into 2 centimetres pieces
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 2 garlic cloves, crushed
- 2 teaspoons ground oregano
- 2 teaspoons sweet paprika
- 2 tablespoons lemon juice
- 2 teaspoons brown sugar
- 2 tablespoons white wine
- 2 chorizo
- 1 red onion

## Method

1. Combine chicken, oil, garlic, oregano, paprika, lemon juice, wine and sugar in a bowl. Set aside and allow to marinade; you can leave it in the fridge for up to 2 hours to marinate.
2. Cut the red onion into 2 centimetres size chunks and set aside. Evenly thread the chicken, alternating with onion and sliced chorizo, onto the skewers.
3. Heat a char-grill pan or barbecue on high. Add the skewers and cook, turning, for 6-8 minutes or until chicken is cooked through.

## Notes

Add some heat by basting the chicken skewers with some of your homemade peri peri sauce whilst cooking.
