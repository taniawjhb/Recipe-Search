## Ingredients

### Syrup

- 3/4 cup water
- 1 cup sugar
- 2 teaspoons lemon juice
- 2 teaspoons rose water

### Baklava

- 1/2 cup pistachios
- 1 cup walnuts
- 1 cup almonds
- 1/4 cup granulated sugar
- 2 teaspoons ground cardamom powder
- 1 teaspoon cinnamon powder
- 450 grams phyllo dough
- 3/4 cup butter (may need 1 cup)

## Method

1. Clarify the butter: Melt the butter in the microwave for 30-45 seconds, then let it separate.
2. Prepare the syrup: Boil the water and sugar for 5 minutes, then stir in the lemon juice and rose water. Let it cool completely.
3. Make the nut filling: Grind the pistachios, walnuts and almonds finely. Mix half the pistachios with the granulated sugar, cardamom and cinnamon.
4. Assembly: Preheat the oven to 165°C. Brush a sheet of phyllo dough with butter, fold in half and brush again.
5. Roll and shape: Place a dowel near the bottom edge, fold the phyllo over it, and sprinkle 2 tablespoons of nut mixture, leaving space at the edges. Roll tightly around the dowel, pressing inward to shape.
6. Bake: Slide off the dowel, place the rolls in a buttered 23 x 33 centimetre baking tray, and slice into 3-4 pieces. Brush with butter and bake 35-45 minutes, until golden brown.
7. Finish: Pour the cooled syrup over the hot rolls and sprinkle with the remaining pistachios. Cool completely before serving.
