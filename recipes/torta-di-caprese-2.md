## Ingredients

- 200g unsalted butter
- 210g blanched whole almonds
- 180g fine-quality bittersweet chocolate (not unsweetened)
- 4 large eggs
- 1 cup white sugar

## Method

1. Preheat oven to 180°C and butter a 22cm cake pan. Line bottom of pan with a round of wax paper and butter paper.
2. Melt butter and cool. In a food processor finely grind together almonds and chocolate.
3. Separate eggs.
4. In a bowl with an electric mixer beat yolks with granulated sugar until very thick and pale.
5. Beat in almond mixture and butter.
6. In another bowl with cleaned beaters beat whites with a pinch salt until they just hold stiff peaks.
7. Whisk a 1/4 into almond mixture. Fold in remaining whites gently but thoroughly and spread batter evenly in pan.
8. Bake torte in middle of oven 50 minutes, or until it begins to pull away from side of pan and a tester comes out with moist crumbs adhering.
9. Cool torte on a rack 5 minutes and invert onto another rack.
10. Discard wax paper and cool torte completely. Invert torte onto a plate. Torte may be made 1 day ahead and kept, wrapped well in plastic wrap, at room temperature.
11. Dust torte with confectioners' sugar and serve with whipped cream.

## Notes

A dense rich chocolate almond cake from the island of Capri across the Bay of Naples.
