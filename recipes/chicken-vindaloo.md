## Ingredients

- 1½ tablespoons coriander seeds
- 1 teaspoon cumin
- 10 peppercorns
- 1 3cm piece cinnamon
- 4 cloves
- 2 green cardamoms (optional)
- ½ teaspoon mustard seeds
- 20 dried red chilies (less spicy kind) or 1 tablespoon chilli powder
- 3 tablespoons vinegar (malt/Goan/red wine vinegar)
- 10 garlic cloves (25 grams)
- 1½ inch ginger (25 grams)
- 900g bone-in chicken
- ½ teaspoon turmeric
- ¾ to 1 teaspoon salt
- 3 tablespoons oil
- 2 onions (finely chopped or processed)
- 2 tomatoes (pureed)
- 1 tablespoon vinegar (malt/Goan/red wine vinegar)
- 1 teaspoon sugar
- ¼ to ½ cup hot water

## Method

1. Deseed the red chilies and soak them in 3 tbsp vinegar and 3 tbsp hot water. Set aside.
2. Add coriander seeds, cumin, peppercorn, mustard, cinnamon, cloves and cardamoms to a blender jar and grind to a fine powder. Then add ginger, garlic and the soaked red chilies.
3. Add the vinegar-water mixture and make a smooth paste. Next, add this to the cleaned chicken along with turmeric and salt.
4. Marinate and rest aside for at least an hour, or overnight in the refrigerator, to absorb the flavors.
5. Heat oil in a deep pot and sauté the onions until golden to light brown. Add 1 tbsp vinegar and 1 tsp sugar to the onions.
6. Then add the marinated chicken and sauté for 3 to 5 minutes on a medium heat.
7. Pour in the tomato puree (optional) and continue to sauté for another 3 to 4 minutes.
8. If your chicken lets out lots of moisture, do not add water. The chicken will cook in its own moisture; if it should start to dry out, add some water.
9. Cover and simmer until the chicken is cooked through and fork tender. To check if the meat is done, prick it with a fork. The meat should fall off the bone easily.
10. Simmer until the gravy reaches a thick and semi-dry stage. Taste and add more salt if needed.
11. Serve chicken vindaloo with plain rice or naan.
