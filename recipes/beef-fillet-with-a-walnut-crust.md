## Ingredients

- 750 grams beef fillet
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1/2 cup coarsely chopped fresh flat leaf parsley
- 1/4 cup coarsely chopped fresh dill
- 1 clove garlic, crushed
- 2 teaspoons finely grated lemon zest
- 2 teaspoons lemon juice
- 1/4 cup coarsely chopped roasted walnuts
- 1 tablespoon olive oil

## Method

1. Preheat oven to 220°C.
2. Rub beef with oil. Now heat a frying pan until hot and then add the beef fillet, sear on all sides until browned all over.
3. Combine the herbs, lemon zest, lemon juice, olive oil and nuts in a bowl, stir to combine.
4. Transfer beef to oven, roast beef uncovered for 15 minutes. Remove and sprinkle with 3/4 of the herb and nuts mixture.
5. Cover with foil and roast a further 15 minutes or until cooked as desired.
6. Remove the beef from the oven, and stand for 10 minutes, now slice the beef finely and serve sprinkled with the remaining herb and nut mixture.
