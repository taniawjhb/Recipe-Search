## Ingredients

- 1 head of lettuce, washed and leaves cut or torn into large pieces
- 2 tomatoes, sliced or quartered
- 1/2 red onion, thinly sliced
- 1 avocado, sliced or diced
- 1 tbs finely chopped cilantro
- Juice of 2 limes
- 2 tbs olive oil
- Salt and pepper to taste

## Method

1. Prepare the dressing by mixing the chopped cilantro, lime juice, oil, salt and pepper. Set aside.
2. Put the lettuce, tomatoes, onion and avocado in a salad bowl.
3. Add the dressing to the salad and toss well.
