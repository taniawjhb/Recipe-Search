## Ingredients

- 400 g strained yogurt
- 2 medium raw or pre-cooked beets
- 2-4 sheets minced garlic (depending on how strong you want it)
- Salt to taste
- 1/2 cup finely chopped walnuts
- 1 tbsp chopped dill
- 2-3 tbsp vinegar
- 5-6 tbsp olive oil

## Method

1. Using gloves, grate the beet on the fine side of a grater.
2. In a bowl add your yoghurt, finely minced or grated garlic, salt, vinegar, finely chopped dill and olive oil. Whisk to combine.
3. Add the finely grated beet to the dressing and stir well to combine. Taste and adjust seasoning.
4. Top with the roasted walnut pieces and some extra dill. Serve as a dip or as a salad beside roasted meats.
