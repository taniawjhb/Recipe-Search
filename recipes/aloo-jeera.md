## Ingredients

- 6 medium potatoes (Yukon Gold)
- 2 tsp turmeric powder, divided
- 1.5 tsp red chilli powder (Kashmiri; adjust to taste)
- 4 tbsp chickpea flour
- 2 tsp salt
- 4 tsp water (to form a slurry with the chickpea flour)
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil (plus extra if needed)
- 1 tsp mustard seeds (skip if unavailable)
- 1.5 tsp cumin seeds (or substitute cumin powder)
- Curry leaves or cilantro, to garnish

## Method

1. Bring a pot of water to the boil with 1 tsp of the turmeric. Quarter the potatoes (no need to peel) and add once the water is bubbling. Boil 10–15 minutes.
2. At the 10 minute mark, test with a fork; if it goes through cleanly they are done. Do not over-boil.
3. In a bowl large enough to hold all the potatoes, whisk together the chickpea flour, remaining turmeric, salt and red chilli powder.
4. Drain the boiled potatoes in a colander, rinse under cold water to stop the cooking, then peel.
5. Toss the peeled potatoes in the spice mix to coat evenly, then add about 4 tsp water so the spices stick. Do not add too much water.
6. Heat the vegetable oil in a skillet over medium heat. Once glistening, add the mustard seeds; when they splutter add the cumin seeds and toast about 20 seconds, taking care not to burn them.
7. Add the potatoes without overcrowding. Stir and cook 3–4 minutes, stir again and cook a further 3–4 minutes until golden brown.
8. Turn off the heat, garnish with curry leaves or cilantro and serve hot with naan, rice or any main dish.

## Notes

You can skip the mustard seeds and add the cumin seeds straight to the oil. If using cumin powder, add it after the potatoes instead of toasting seeds.
