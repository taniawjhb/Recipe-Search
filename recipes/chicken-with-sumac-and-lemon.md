## Ingredients

- 1/8 cup lemon juice, plus lemon sliced thin
- 1/8 cup extra virgin olive oil
- 1 1/2 tablespoons sumac
- 1/2 teaspoon allspice
- 1 teaspoon dried thyme
- 1 teaspoon cumin
- 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
- 5 cloves garlic, grated, pressed or finely minced
- 1 1/2 teaspoons kosher salt
- 1.5 kilograms chicken thighs, bone-in (about 6 thighs) or use legs
- 450 grams potatoes, thin-skinned, sliced into 3 millimetre rounds (optional)
- 1 red onion, cut into thin slices
- 6-8 sprigs of fresh thyme
- Aleppo pepper, for garnish (or chilli flakes)
- Fresh parsley leaves, coarsely chopped, for serving

## Method

1. Make the marinade: In a small bowl mix the olive oil, lemon juice, sumac, allspice, dried thyme, cumin, pepper, garlic and salt.
2. Marinate the chicken: Pat the chicken dry. Cut 2 small slits in the skin of each thigh to allow the marinade to penetrate. Rub each piece of chicken, coating all sides well with the sumac marinade. Cover and marinate in the fridge 1-3 hours. Let the chicken rest at room temperature for 1/2 hour before baking.
3. Heat the oven to 200°C.
4. Assemble: Spread the lemon slices on the bottom of a 9 x 13 baking pan. Add a few sprigs of thyme over the lemon slices and place the chicken pieces, skin side up, over the sliced lemons and thyme.
5. Optional potatoes: In a medium bowl, toss the potatoes and onion slices with salt and a drizzle of olive oil. Tuck the potato slices and onion in between the chicken.
6. Bake the chicken: Place in the middle of the hot oven and roast for 25 minutes, rotate the pan, and bake 20 minutes more, until the chicken is cooked through and the skin is brown. To increase crispiness, broil for a few minutes under careful watch so as not to burn.
7. To serve: Spoon a little of the sauce from the pan over the chicken, garnish with fresh parsley, thyme sprigs and sprinkle with Aleppo pepper.
