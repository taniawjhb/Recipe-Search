## Ingredients

- 2 cup cauliflower florets
- 2 cups potatoes, peeled and chopped
- 1 or 1.5 cups peas, fresh or frozen
- 2 small tej patta or 1 large tej patta (Indian bay leaf)
- 1 or 2 green chilies, slit
- 1/2 teaspoon cumin seeds
- 1/4 cup chopped onion
- 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder
- 1/2 teaspoon red chili powder
- 1/2 teaspoon garam masala powder
- 1 teaspoon coriander powder (ground coriander)
- 1 teaspoon dry fenugreek leaves, crushed (kasuri methi)
- 2 tablespoon oil
- few coriander leaves for garnishing (cilantro leaves)
- salt as required

### For the Masala Paste

- 1/2 cup chopped onion
- 3 medium size tomatoes, chopped
- 1/2 inch ginger
- 3 to 4 garlic

## Method

1. Rinse and chop all the veggies. Keep the cauliflower florets in salted water for 15-20 minutes, then drain and keep aside.
2. Blend the onion, tomatoes, ginger and garlic till smooth in a blender with no water or very little water.
3. Heat oil in a pan. Fry the cumin first with the bay leaves.
4. Add the chopped onions and fry till light brown. Add the ground onion-tomato paste.
5. Stir and then add the following spice powders, one by one - 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder, 1/2 teaspoon red chili powder, 1 teaspoon coriander powder and 1/2 teaspoon garam masala powder.
6. Fry the masala till the oil starts to leave the sides, stirring so it does not stick to the pan. This might take around 7-9 minutes.
7. Now add the cauliflower florets, potatoes and peas. Add 3 cups of water and stir the curry.
8. Finally add the slit green chilies and salt. Let the whole curry come to a boil.
9. Cover with a lid and let curry simmer till the veggies are cooked. Finally, add crushed kasuri methi leaves.
10. Garnish with coriander leaves and serve aloo gobi matar hot with some chapatis or steamed rice.
