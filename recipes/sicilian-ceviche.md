## Ingredients

- 450 grams kingklip fillet
- 2 large juicy lemons
- 1/2 large tomato
- 1 shallot
- 1 large bunch of fresh mint
- 1 handful of basil leaves
- 1 pinch of cinnamon powder
- 1 teaspoon of extra virgin olive oil
- salt

## Method

1. Squeeze the lemons and reserve the juice in a bowl.
2. Cut the kingklip fillet into small cubes, place it in a bowl and cover it with lemon juice. Keep it aside and leave it to marinate, at room temperature, for at least a couple of hours.
3. Meanwhile finely chop the shallot, tomato and fresh herbs. Put everything in a bowl, season with olive oil, a pinch of salt and cinnamon. Keep it aside.
4. When the fish is ready (the meat is opaque), drain and gently combine the contents of the two bowls.
5. Serve with crusty bread.
