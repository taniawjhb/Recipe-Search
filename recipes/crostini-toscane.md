## Ingredients

- Ciabatta, sliced
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 400 g chicken livers
- 1 clove of garlic
- 1 small red onion
- a few sage leaves
- 100 ml (½ cup) of Vin Santo or white wine
- 1 tsp of anchovy paste, or two anchovies
- 60 g capers
- juice of half a small lemon
- chicken stock
- 2 tbsp balsamic vinegar
- extra virgin olive oil
- salt
- pepper

## Method

1. Put oil, garlic and the anchovy fillet in a pan and over a medium low heat fry until the garlic just begins to go golden. Now add the onions and finely chopped capers and sage, salt and pepper and cook slowly over a low heat for 5 minutes until the onions begin to soften. Now add the livers and cook for 15-20 minutes. Stir occasionally.
2. When done, remove from the pan and finely chop the mixture with a knife. Return to the pan over a low heat until it begins to stick slightly, then add the Vin Santo or white wine, and cook for 5 more minutes.
3. Squeeze in the lemon and add the balsamic vinegar (if using) and cook for a further 2 minutes.
4. If the mixture is too thick, add a little stock.
5. Slice the bread, brush with olive oil and grill until lightly crunchy and then top with a spoon of the liver mixture. Top with finely chopped parsley and a splash of olive oil.
