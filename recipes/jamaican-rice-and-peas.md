## Ingredients

- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 large onion, diced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 teaspoon fresh ginger, grated
- 1 teaspoon fresh thyme, chopped
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
- 1/2 teaspoon ground allspice
- 1 Scotch bonnet chile (or Habanero), diced
- 1.75 cups water (or low-sodium chicken stock)
- 1 (13.5 ounce) can full-fat coconut milk
- 2 cups long-grain white rice
- 2 (15 ounce) cans red kidney beans, drained and rinsed
- Fresh lime juice for serving (optional)

## Method

1. Saute the aromatics. Heat the olive oil in a large sauté pot over medium-high heat. Add the onions and cook for 5-6 minutes, or until soft and translucent, stirring often to prevent burning.
2. Reduce heat to medium-low and add the garlic, ginger, fresh thyme, salt, pepper, ground allspice, and Scotch bonnet chile (or habanero). Mix well to combine. Stir continuously for 1-2 minutes.
3. Add the water and coconut milk (or chicken broth) to the pot. Mix to combine and bring just to a boil.
4. Stir in the rice, reduce heat to low, and cover. Cook the rice until liquid is absorbed and rice is tender, checking every 5 minutes and gently fluffing with a fork as it cooks.
5. Remove pot from heat and gently stir in the drained and rinsed kidney beans. Cover and allow the rice to rest for an additional 5 minutes before serving.
