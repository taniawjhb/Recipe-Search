## Ingredients

- 1 tin cannellini beans, drained
- 2 tomatoes, diced
- 1/2 red onion, thinly sliced
- 1 cup fresh parsley, chopped
- 1 teaspoon sumac
- 1/2 teaspoon Aleppo pepper
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1 lemon, juice of
- 2 tablespoons olive oil

## Method

1. Place the drained cannellini beans in a large bowl and add the chopped tomatoes, sliced red onions and chopped parsley. Stir well and set aside.
2. In a small bowl, mix the sumac, Aleppo pepper, salt, lemon juice and olive oil and pour it over the salad.
3. Toss the salad with the dressing and refrigerate for 30 minutes. Serve cold as a side dish.
