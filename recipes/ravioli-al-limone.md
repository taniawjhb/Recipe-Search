## Ingredients

For the filling

- 1.5 lemons, finely zested
- 500 g ricotta
- 60 g grated parmesan cheese
- fine salt, to taste
- pepper, to taste
- 1 sprig thyme

For the sauce

- 60gms butter
- 1 clove garlic, finely chopped
- 1 tsp finely chopped thyme
- 200 ml fresh cream
- Zest of 1/2 lemon
- 2 tbsp lemon juice

## Method

1. Place the ricotta in a bowl and season it with the grated zest of 1.5 lemons. Add the grated cheese, season with salt and pepper, add a little thyme, and mix well.
2. Take your pasta dough and run it through your pasta roller a few times, turning and folding each time to laminate the dough. Now run it through the machine to a 1-2mm thick level, about a Level 5.
3. Take a sheet of pasta and gently fold in half length ways to mark the centre point, then unfold again. Now place a generous spoonful of filling in the center of the bottom half and space before repeating, continue until all the ravioli are formed.
4. Wet the pasta around the filling, and now gently fold over the top half, sealing from the inside out, press the pasta closed. Cut out the ravioli with a crimped cutter. Using a fork, seal the edges of each ravioli well and arrange them on a floured tray.
5. Salt a large pot of water and bring to a rolling boil, now add the ravioli in batches cooking for about 3 minutes per batch. Drain them directly into the pan and mix.
6. To prepare the sauce start by browning your butter, once the butter is browned add the thyme and garlic and cook for 30 seconds, now add the cream and reduce slightly before adding the lemon zest and the cooked pasta. Once the pasta has been added, take 2 tbsp lemon juice and dilute in 50ml pasta water and stir through the cream sauce, top with freshly chopped parsley and serve immediately.
