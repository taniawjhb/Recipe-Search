## Ingredients

- 200g medium to dark chocolate
- 6 eggs, separated
- 2 tbsp real vanilla extract
- 60g butter
- 90ml milk

## Method

1. In a double boiler, break the chocolate into pieces in a bowl, add the milk and then slowly heat while stirring occasionally, until the chocolate is melted.
2. Remove the bowl from the hot water and let the chocolate cool a little, then add the vanilla extract and the butter and stir them into the chocolate. Stir until the butter is melted and the chocolate looks nice and glossy.
3. Whisk your egg yolks then add to the chocolate, stirring until well combined.
4. Beat the egg whites until stiff, then gently fold them into the chocolate mixture until well mixed in.
5. Divide the mixture into pudding bowls, cover and put into the refrigerator overnight.
