## Ingredients

- 2 teaspoons turmeric powder, divided
- 1.5 teaspoons red chilli powder (Kashmiri chilli powder is potent; adjust to taste)
- 4 tablespoons chickpea flour
- 2 teaspoons salt
- 4 teaspoons water, to form a slurry with the chickpea flour
- 2 tablespoons vegetable oil (you can add extra if you like)
- 1 teaspoon mustard seeds (skip if unavailable)
- 1.5 teaspoons cumin seeds (can substitute with cumin powder)
- 6 medium Yukon Gold potatoes

## Method

1. Bring a pot of water to boil along with 1 teaspoon of turmeric powder. Quarter the potatoes (no need to peel) and drop them into the water once you start to see bubbles appear. Boil for about 10-15 minutes.
2. Note: At the 10 minute mark, take a fork and poke your potatoes. If the fork goes through cleanly, you are good to go. If they do not, let them cook a bit more, checking every minute. You do not want to over boil them.
3. In a medium sized bowl (that can hold all the potatoes) add the chickpea flour, remaining turmeric, salt, and red chilli powder and give it a good whisk.
4. Once the potatoes are done boiling, remove from heat using a colander or slotted spoon. Rinse the potatoes under cold water to stop them from further cooking and peel the potatoes (the peels should come right off).
5. Drop the peeled potatoes into the bowl with the spice mix and give it a good toss to coat them evenly. Now, add about 4 teaspoons of water to make sure the spices stick to the potatoes. Do not add too much water.
6. In a skillet, heat the vegetable oil on medium heat. Once the oil starts to glisten, add the mustard seeds. Then, once the mustard seeds start sputtering, add the cumin seeds. Toast for about 20 seconds taking care not to burn the seeds.
7. Toss the potatoes into the skillet, taking care not to overcrowd them. Give them a good stir and let them cook for about 3 to 4 minutes. Then give them one more good stir and let them cook another 3 to 4 minutes. Your potatoes should be golden brown by this time. If not, give it a few more minutes until they are.
8. Once they are golden brown, turn the heat off, garnish with some curry leaves or cilantro if you wish, and serve hot with naan, rice or any other main dish of choice.
