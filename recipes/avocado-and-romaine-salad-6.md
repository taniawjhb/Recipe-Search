## Ingredients

- 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1 tablespoon fresh chives, chopped
- 1 1/3 cups avocado, chopped
- 3 cups romaine hearts, chopped
- 2 teaspoons parsley, chopped
- 1 teaspoon dill, chopped
- 2 tablespoons red wine vinegar
- 6 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
- Salt and pepper, to taste
- Balsamic vinegar, to taste (optional)

## Method

1. Start by halving the cherry tomatoes. Roughly chop the chives, parsley and dill and add everything to a bowl.
2. Roughly chop the romaine lettuce and add to the bowl. Toss gently and season with a little salt and pepper, then arrange on a serving platter.
3. Gently peel the avocados, cut into rough chunks and add these to the tomato and lettuce salad.
4. Whisk together the olive oil and red wine vinegar, season with a little salt and pepper, and drizzle over the prepared salad.
