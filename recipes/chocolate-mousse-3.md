## Ingredients

- 200 grams medium to dark chocolate
- 6 eggs, separated
- 2 tablespoons real vanilla extract
- 1/4 cup sugar
- 60 grams butter
- 45 millilitres milk
- 45 millilitres strong coffee

## Method

1. In a double boiler, in a bowl, break the chocolate into pieces, add the milk and coffee and then slowly heat it while stirring occasionally, until the chocolate is melted.
2. Remove the bowl from the hot water, let the chocolate cool a little bit, then add the vanilla extract and the butter and stir them into the chocolate, stir until the butter is melted and the chocolate looks nice and glossy.
3. Whisk your egg yolks and sugar until the ribbon stage, then add the mixture to the chocolate, stir until well combined.
4. Next, beat the egg whites till stiff then gently fold them into the chocolate mixture until they are mixed well in.
5. Divide the mixture into pudding bowls, cover and put into the refrigerator overnight.
