## Ingredients

- 1 cup passion fruit puree
- 1 tin (400 millilitres) coconut milk
- 1 tin (400 grams) condensed milk
- 1 cup heavy cream
- 1/4 cup milk powder

## Method

1. Blend all the ingredients together in a blender until well combined.
2. Transfer to an ice cream maker and freeze according to the manufacturer's instructions.
3. To make this ice cream without an ice cream maker, transfer the blended mixture into the container you plan to freeze it in (or leave it in the blender if you have enough freezer space). After 45 minutes to 1 hour, check on it; if you see the mixture freezing at the edges, remove from the freezer and give it a whizz. Repeat the process 3-4 times, then transfer to the container and let freeze completely.
