## Ingredients

- 2 cups heavy whipping cream
- 1 cup milk
- 150 grams white sugar
- 5 egg yolks
- 1-2 teaspoons ground cardamom powder
- 1 teaspoon rose essence
- 1/4 cup flaked almonds, toasted lightly
- large pinch of saffron (optional)

## Method

1. Combine heavy cream, milk, sugar and cardamom powder in a saucepan; bring to a simmer over low heat. Cook and stir until sugar is dissolved, about 5 minutes. Remove from heat, and if adding saffron add now. Cover saucepan and allow the mixture to steep for 10 minutes.
2. Uncover pan and bring back to a simmer over low heat. Beat the egg yolks in a bowl. Gradually stir one cup of the hot cream mixture into the eggs, several tablespoons at a time. This will help bring the eggs up to temperature without scrambling them.
3. Stir the egg mixture back into the cream mixture in the saucepan. Cook and stir over low heat until the mixture just coats the back of a spoon, 5 to 10 minutes. Transfer to a large bowl; cover. Refrigerate overnight.
4. Stir rose essence into the cold ice cream mixture, taste and adjust the rose based on your preferences. Freeze in an ice cream maker according to manufacturer's directions.
5. Transfer ice cream to a lidded freezer container, sprinkle with the lightly toasted almonds and freeze for 4 hours before serving.
