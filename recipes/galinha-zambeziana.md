## Ingredients

- 1 chicken, cut into pieces
- 3/4 cup peri peri sauce
- 3/4 cup coconut milk
- 1/4 cup lemon juice
- 1 clove garlic
- Salt to taste

## Method

1. In a large bowl, combine all the ingredients for the marinade.
2. Add the chicken, cover, and allow to stand at room temperature for up to 2 hours.
3. Heat your grill until hot, then grill the chicken, basting often with the remaining marinade, until the chicken is cooked through and charred on the edges.
