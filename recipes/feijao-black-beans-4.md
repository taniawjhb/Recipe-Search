## Ingredients

- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 large onion, finely chopped
- 1 garlic clove, finely chopped
- 1 small carrot, finely diced
- 1 celery stick, finely diced
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1 teaspoon paprika
- 1 1/2 tablespoons tomato paste
- 800 grams canned black beans
- 1 vegetable stock cube
- few sprigs of thyme

## Method

1. Heat the oil in a large saucepan, add the chopped vegetables and cook over a medium-low heat until softened, about 8-10 minutes.
2. Stir in the spices and thyme, and cook for a further 2-3 minutes.
3. Add the tomato puree and the drained black beans, the stock cube and 150 millilitres water.
4. Bring to a simmer for about 10-12 minutes until slightly thickened.
