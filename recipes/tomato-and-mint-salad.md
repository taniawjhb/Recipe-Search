## Ingredients

- 400 grams cherry tomato
- 1 small red onion
- handful of mint leaves
- 1/2 teaspoon finely chopped thyme leaves
- extra-virgin olive oil, for drizzling
- a generous pinch of lemon zest

## Method

1. In a bowl add the chopped tomatoes, torn mint leaves and thyme leaves, the finely chopped onion and a generous pinch of lemon zest. Add the olive oil and then season with salt and pepper, before tossing to coat, set aside.
2. When ready serve on a platter with all the juices to mop up with crusty bread.
