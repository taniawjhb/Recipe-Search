## Ingredients

- 1 cup grated Parmesan cheese
- 300gms ricotta, crumbled
- 1 cup full fat milk
- 1/4 cup vegetable oil, plus 1 Tbsp for oiling your hands
- 2 eggs
- 500gms tapioca flour (also known as tapioca starch)
- 2 tsp kosher salt

## Method

1. Preheat oven to 175C.
2. Add the tapioca flour and the salt to a large bowl, whisk to combine and set aside.
3. In a medium bowl, combine the cheeses and the eggs and set aside.
4. Add the milk and the oil to a small saucepan and bring to a simmer on the stove top over medium heat.
5. When the liquid simmers, remove the pan from heat and immediately pour the hot liquid into the tapioca mixture. Using a wooden spoon, stir to combine – the mixture will not be homogeneous, it will be gummy and clumpy.
6. Wait 5-10 minutes until the tapioca mixture is cool to the touch, and only then add the cheese and egg mixture to the tapioca mixture. Mix with a wooden spoon, or your hands, to combine. You should now have a homogeneous mixture that comes together as a ball. You will still see some white tapioca specks in the dough – that is totally normal.
7. Oil your hands, then scoop Tbsp to 2 Tbsp size portions, roll them into balls, and arrange them on a baking sheet approximately 2" apart from one another.
8. Bake until golden brown, approximately 30-35 minutes.
