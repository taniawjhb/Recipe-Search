## Ingredients

- 3 cups cake flour
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 6 eggs, separated
- 2 cups white sugar, divided
- 1 cup butter, softened
- 2 teaspoons grated lemon zest
- 2 tablespoons lemon juice
- 1 cup plain whole-milk yogurt

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 175°C. Grease one 10-inch tube pan.
2. Sift the flour, baking soda, and salt together. Set the mixture aside.
3. In a large bowl, beat the egg whites until soft peaks form. Gradually add 1/2 cup of the sugar, beating until stiff glossy peaks form. Set aside.
4. Beat the butter and remaining 1 1/2 cups sugar in a large bowl with an electric mixer until fluffy, 3 to 5 minutes. The mixture should be noticeably lighter in colour.
5. Blend in the egg yolks, lemon zest, and lemon juice. Add the flour mixture alternately with the yogurt, mixing until combined. Gently fold in the egg whites and pour the batter into the prepared pan.
6. Bake in the preheated oven until a tester inserted in the center comes out clean, 50 to 60 minutes. Let the cake cool in the pan for 10 minutes, then turn out onto a rack to finish cooling. Serves 12.
