## Ingredients

- 200 grams dried rice noodles, wide
- 2 tablespoons oil (peanut, vegetable or canola)
- 3 large cloves of garlic, minced
- 2 birds eye chillies or Thai chillies, deseeded, very finely chopped
- 1/2 onion, sliced
- 200 grams chicken, cut into bite-size pieces
- 2 teaspoons fish sauce
- 2 teaspoons soy sauce
- 2 green onions, cut into 3 centimetres
- 1 cup Thai or Thai holy basil leaves (sub regular basil)
- 3 tablespoons oyster sauce
- 1 1/2 tablespoons light soy sauce
- 1 1/2 tablespoons dark soy sauce
- 2 teaspoons sugar
- 1 tablespoon water

## Method

1. Prepare the noodles per packet directions.
2. Mix the sauce in a small bowl.
3. Heat the oil in a wok or large heavy based skillet over high heat.
4. Add the garlic and chilli and cook for 10 seconds. Don't inhale — the chilli will make you cough!
5. Add the onion, cook for 1 minute.
6. Add the chicken and fish sauce, and fry until cooked, around 2 minutes.
7. Add the green onion, noodles and sauce and cook for 1 minute until the sauce reduces and coats the noodles.
8. Remove from heat and immediately add the basil, toss until just wilted, then serve immediately.
