## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup pistachio kernels
- 1/2 (1.75 kilogram) small seedless watermelon, peeled, quartered
- 1/4 cup olive oil
- 2 teaspoons finely grated lemon rind
- 125 grams feta, lightly crumbled

## Method

1. Heat a small frying pan over medium heat. Add pistachios. Cook, stirring occasionally, for 5 minutes or until light brown. Transfer to a bowl. Cool for 5 minutes. Roughly chop.
2. Slice watermelon quarters into 1 centimetre-thick wedges. Arrange on plates.
3. Place oil and lemon rind in a screw-top jar. Season with salt and pepper. Secure lid. Shake to combine.
4. Crumble feta over watermelon. Sprinkle with pistachios. Serve drizzled with oil mixture.
