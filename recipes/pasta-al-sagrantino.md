## Ingredients

- 150 grams smoked bacon, cut into cubes
- 150-200 millilitres cream
- 1/2 onion, sliced
- 1 glass of red wine (preferably Sagrantino DOP Umbria)
- 3 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
- Salt to taste
- 50 grams Parmigiano Reggiano DOP, grated

## Method

1. Put the oil in a pan, add the half onion cleaned and cut into thin slices and let it fry over low heat.
2. Add the pancetta and fry gently until it has begun to render and turned a light brown - always over low heat.
3. Now deglaze with the red wine and let it evaporate.
4. Allow to cool and then place the sauce in a food processor-mixer, pulse until the sauce is uniformly chopped and mixed but not a paste.
5. Return to the pan, add the cream, cook for a minute or two and mix so that the sauce is uniform.
6. Now add your cooked gnocchi and a little splash of the pasta water, stir till coated and serve topped with more grated parmesan.
