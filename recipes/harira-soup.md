## Ingredients

- 1-2 tablespoons oil
- 2 large onions
- 4 garlic cloves
- 2 teaspoons turmeric
- 2 teaspoons cumin
- 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon
- 2 red chillies
- 500 grams carton passata
- 1.7 litres vegetable stock
- 1 can green lentils
- 2 carrots
- 1 sweet potato
- 5 celery sticks
- 2/3 small pack coriander
- 1 lemon, to serve

## Method

1. Heat the oil in a large non-stick saute pan over a medium heat and fry the onions and garlic until starting to soften.
2. Tip in the spices and chilli, stir briefly, then pour in the passata and stock. Add the lentils, carrots, sweet potato and celery, and bring to the boil.
3. Cover the pan and leave to simmer for 30 minutes, then cook uncovered for a further 5-10 minutes until the vegetables and lentils are tender.
4. Stir in the chopped coriander and serve in bowls with lemon wedges for squeezing over, and the reserved coriander sprinkled over.
