## Ingredients

- 5 eggs (3 yolks, 2 whole)
- 12 tablespoons sugar (split half and half)
- 1 teaspoon corn starch/flour
- 500 millilitres milk
- Lemon rind
- Cinnamon quill
- 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract

## Method

1. In a non-stick frying pan add half the sugar. Over a medium high heat allow the sugar to start to melt. Don't stir until the sugar is almost completely melted. Then you can mix to keep the lighter and darker sugars. Once the caramel is golden (remember it will still darken slightly in the pan so allow for it), pour the melted sugar into the bottom of your mould, try to get it to go up the sides a little.
2. Beat the eggs, sugar and corn starch till mixture lightens.
3. In a saucepan, heat the milk, lemon rind, cinnamon quill and vanilla on a stove on medium heat until milk is just hot.
4. Slowly add the warm milk mixture to the egg mixture, bit by bit, making sure the mixture is well incorporated.
5. Pour into crème caramel mould, place in a bain marie for 20 minutes.
6. Remove from heat, and refrigerate till cold.
7. To serve, dip the base of the mould into hot water for a few seconds, place a plate over the open mould, then flip the mould over into an appropriate dish.
