## Ingredients

### Salad

- 1 tin precooked chickpeas / garbanzo beans
- 1 teaspoon ground sumac
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1/2 medium red onion, halved and thinly sliced
- 10 cherry tomatoes, quartered
- 3 spring onions, finely chopped
- 1 red bell pepper, deseeded and finely chopped
- 40 grams spinach leaves, washed and roughly chopped

### Dressing

- 2 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
- 1/2 juice of lemon
- Salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste
- Sprinkle of extra sumac to serve
- Flatbread or pita bread to serve

## Method

1. Prepare the onions: Massage the salt and ground sumac into the sliced onions until softened and infused with tangy flavour.
2. Combine the base: Rinse and drain the chickpeas, then mix with the onions. Stir in the ground cumin and salt to taste.
3. Add freshness: Fold in the chopped tomatoes, peppers, spring onion and spinach, ensuring even distribution.
4. Make the dressing: Whisk together the extra virgin olive oil and lemon juice, seasoning with salt and black pepper to taste.
5. Finish and serve: Pour the dressing over the salad, toss well and transfer to a serving plate. Sprinkle with extra sumac, if desired, and enjoy with flatbreads or pita.
