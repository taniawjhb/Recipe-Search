## Ingredients

- 4 sandwich bread rolls (Portuguese papo seco)
- 600 grams pork cutlets, sliced very thin
- 5 cloves garlic, chopped
- 70 grams lard
- 150 millilitres white wine
- 3 bay leaves
- Juice of a lemon
- 1 teaspoon paprika
- Salt
- Pepper, freshly ground

## Method

1. In a container, place a layer of cutlets and season with salt, pepper, bay leaf, paprika, lemon juice and garlic. If necessary, make several layers with all the cutlets, seasoning them the same way at each layer.
2. Finally, pour the white wine over the cutlets and marinate for 3 hours in the refrigerator.
3. In a large frying pan, heat the lard over medium heat.
4. Drain the cutlets and reserve the marinade. Fry them in lard over high heat, turning them constantly.
5. Once the cutlets are fried, add the reserved marinade and cook over medium heat until the liquid has evaporated by half.
6. Toast the bread loaves.
7. Fill each bread with cutlets and drizzle with the remaining sauce.
