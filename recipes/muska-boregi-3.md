## Ingredients

- 1 kilogram fresh spinach, washed, dried, trimmed and coarsely chopped
- 3 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
- 1 bunch scallions (about 10 small), white and light-green parts only, trimmed and finely chopped
- 2 cups crumbled feta cheese (300 grams)
- 1/2 cup finely grated parmesan
- 1 cup grated mozzarella
- 2 large eggs, lightly beaten
- 1 teaspoon paprika
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1/3 cup finely chopped fresh flat-leaf parsley
- 1/4 teaspoon freshly grated nutmeg
- Kosher or fine sea salt
- 1/3 cup extra-virgin olive oil for brushing, more as needed
- 500 grams frozen phyllo dough, thawed and at room temperature
- 2 teaspoons milk

## Method

1. Wilt the spinach: Heat a large sauté pan over medium-high. Add a few handfuls of spinach, tossing with tongs. As it wilts, add more gradually, cooking until bright green (about 4 minutes). Transfer to a colander, let cool slightly and squeeze out the excess liquid.
2. Prepare the filling: Wipe the pan clean, then heat oil over medium. Sauté the scallions until soft (about 4 minutes), then stir in the paprika and cumin. Add the spinach, remove from the heat and let cool 5 minutes. Mix in the cheeses, eggs, parsley, nutmeg and salt until well combined.
3. Layer the phyllo: Lightly oil a 23 x 33 centimetre baking pan. Brush the phyllo sheets with oil and layer them, ensuring the overlapping edges cover the pan.
4. Assemble and seal: Add 3 centred sheets, then spread the filling evenly. Layer the remaining sheets using the same pattern, then press the edges down to seal.
5. Score and bake: Using a sharp knife, score the top into 24 rectangles, brushing milk along the cuts to prevent flaking. Bake at 175°C for 35-45 minutes, until golden brown.
6. Serve: Let cool slightly before cutting along the score marks. Enjoy warm.
