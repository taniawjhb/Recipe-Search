## Ingredients

- 2 cups fresh diced tomatoes (or canned when out of season)
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 1 teaspoon chopped garlic
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1/2 teaspoon cumin, chili powder, curcuma, paprika (each)
- 1 tablespoon orange juice (or lime)
- Dash of honey
- 1/2 cup diced mango (or pineapple)
- Salt to taste

## Method

1. In a small pan warm the oil with the spices and garlic over medium heat.
2. Once fragrant, add the onions and cook until just clear.
3. Now add all the other ingredients and let simmer until the salsa is thick enough, taste and adjust with a little salt.
