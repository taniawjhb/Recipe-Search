## Ingredients

- 2 cans chickpeas
- 3 tablespoons cooking oil
- 2 bay leaves
- 2 cloves
- 1 teaspoon whole cumin
- 1 green chilli, finely chopped
- 6–7 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 teaspoons grated ginger
- 1 large red onion, finely chopped (about 1 cup)
- 1 teaspoon red chilli powder
- 1 teaspoon turmeric
- 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 teaspoon coriander powder
- 1 pinch asafoetida powder
- 2 brown cardamom, crushed seeds
- 420 grams canned diced tomatoes
- 2 tablespoons tomato paste
- 1/2 teaspoon brown sugar
- 2 teaspoons chana masala / garam masala / curry powder
- 1/2 cup chopped cilantro, to garnish

## Method

1. Take about half a cup of chickpeas from a can and crush coarsely with a masher; set aside.
2. Heat the oil in a heavy-bottomed pan over medium heat. When hot, add the cumin seeds, cloves and bay leaves.
3. Add the garlic, ginger and chilli. Sauté a minute, then add the chopped onions.
4. Sauté, stirring frequently, until the onions start to turn golden brown, then reduce the heat to low.
5. Add the turmeric, cardamom seeds, black pepper, salt, asafoetida, chilli powder and coriander powder. Stir quickly and add 2 tablespoons water.
6. Let the spices cook for half a minute, then add the diced tomatoes and tomato paste. Cook until the oil separates.
7. Add the whole and crushed chickpeas, brown sugar and chana masala with enough water to reach the desired consistency. Simmer 15–20 minutes.
8. Garnish with chopped cilantro and serve.
