## Ingredients

- 400 millilitres passion fruit pulp (100% pulp)
- 1 small can cream
- 1 can condensed milk
- 450 millilitres heavy whipping cream

## Method

1. Remove the fruit required to make up 400 millilitres pulp and add it to the jug of the blender.
2. Whip the heavy cream. Use an electric hand held mixer. Whip until peaks of the cream stay firm and don't lose their shape. Set it aside. Do not overwhip the cream.
3. Blend the passion fruit pulp, cream and condensed milk.
4. Pour the blended mix into the bowl where you whipped the cream. Make sure you have a big enough mixing bowl.
5. Fold the blended mix into the whipped cream. Pour into serving cups. You can add crumbled cookies in the bottom if desired. Refrigerate for at least 2 hours.
6. Serve with fresh passion fruit, raspberries, whipped cream and optional other toppings according to taste.
