## Ingredients

- 6 dried red chilli, soaked and finely chopped
- 1 slice galangal/ginger (or both)
- ⅔ of a stalk of lemongrass
- 2 shallots
- Zest of 2 limes
- 2 Makrut lime leaves
- 2 cloves garlic
- ½ tsp dry turmeric
- ½ tsp roasted coriander seed
- 1 tsp crushed, roasted peanuts
- 1 tsp peanut butter
- 2 tsp salt
- ½ tsp shrimp paste

## Method

1. Start by finely chopping all the ingredients, as much as possible. Then add them to a heavy stone mortar and grind until a smooth paste is formed and all the aromas have been released.

## Notes

Curry pastes can be stored in the fridge in a clean container for 7-10 days, but they are best fresh.
