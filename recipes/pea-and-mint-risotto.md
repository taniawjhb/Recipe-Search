## Ingredients

- 1 litre vegetable or chicken stock, warmed
- 3 tablespoons olive oil, divided
- 2 shallots (or substitute 1 leek or 1 white onion), diced
- 2 garlic cloves, roughly chopped
- 1 cup Arborio rice
- 1/2 cup dry white wine
- 2 cups peas, fresh (blanched) or frozen
- 1/2 cup grated parmesan cheese
- Zest of 1 lemon
- Salt and pepper, to taste
- 1/4 cup fresh mint, packed, sliced

## Method

1. Heat the stock in a medium pot.
2. In a heavy-bottomed pot or Dutch oven, heat 2 tablespoons olive oil over medium-low heat and sauté the shallots and garlic until fragrant and golden, about 5 minutes, stirring.
3. Add the Arborio rice and stir to coat for two to three minutes. Add the white wine to deglaze the pan and simmer until the wine has completely been absorbed. Add one cup of hot broth and stir every 45-60 seconds, letting the rice absorb it slowly over medium-low heat, about 5 minutes. Continue adding one cup at a time, each time allowing the rice to slowly absorb the broth until the rice is tender yet still holding its shape. This takes about 20 minutes.
4. At the same time, blanch the fresh peas. Bring a small pot of salted water to the boil. Add the peas and simmer for 2-3 minutes until tender; drain and set aside.
5. Add 1/2 cup blanched peas to a blender with 1/2 cup stock and 1 tablespoon olive oil. Blend until silky smooth. Add a handful of spinach to boost the colour if you like, and blend until smooth again. Set this aside.
6. When the risotto is tender and you are close to serving, fold in the cheese, lemon zest, the blanched peas and the pea puree. Add salt and pepper to taste. Stir in 2/3 of the mint, saving the rest for the garnish.
7. Taste, adjust the salt, pepper and lemon. Add chilli flakes if you like.
8. Divide the pea risotto among bowls and serve.
