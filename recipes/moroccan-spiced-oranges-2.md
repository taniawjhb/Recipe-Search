## Ingredients

- 2 1/2 cups orange sections, cut into 1/2-inch pieces (about 6)
- 1/4 cup slivered almonds
- 2 1/2 tablespoons chopped pitted dates (about 4)
- 1 tablespoon powdered sugar
- 1 tablespoon fresh lemon juice
- 1/4 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- ground cinnamon (optional)
- grated orange rind (optional)

## Method

1. Peel oranges carefully removing all white membrane, then carefully cut wedges of orange, removing the segments from the central pith. Set aside in a bowl.
2. Now add the remaining ingredients, and gently stir to combine.
3. Set aside in the fridge to chill and then serve plain.
