## Ingredients

For the cake base:

- 300 grams semolina, coarse
- 180 grams granulated sugar
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 250 grams whole milk
- 1/2 teaspoon mastic, powder
- 1 level teaspoon mahlab, powder

For the syrup:

- 350 grams water
- 350 grams granulated sugar
- 1 stick cinnamon

For the cream:

- 500 grams whole milk
- 130 grams granulated sugar
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 3 egg yolks, from medium eggs
- 50 grams corn starch
- 50 grams butter
- 200 grams whipped cream

To serve:

- cinnamon, powder

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 180°C set to fan.
2. In a bowl add the semolina, sugar, baking powder, baking soda, milk, mastic, mahlab, and whisk well.
3. Grease a 32x25 centimetres baking pan and spread the mixture evenly. Bake for 20 minutes. Remove and set aside to completely cool.
4. For the syrup: In a pot add the water, sugar, cinnamon, and transfer it over medium-high heat until it comes to a boil and the sugar dissolves. Using a ladle, pour the hot syrup over the cold base.
5. For the cream: In a pot add 450 grams of the milk, half of the sugar, the vanilla extract, and transfer it over medium heat. Allow to heat without stirring.
6. In a bowl, add the yolks, the rest of the sugar and whisk. Add the rest of the milk, the corn starch and whisk.
7. When the milk comes to a boil, slowly transfer it to the yolk mixture whisking continuously. Transfer the whole mixture back to the pot and place it over medium heat. Whisk continuously until the cream thickens.
8. Remove from the heat, add the butter, and whisk until it melts. Transfer to a bowl and cover with plastic wrap, making sure that the wrap directly touches the surface of the cream. Refrigerate for 30-60 minutes until completely chilled.
9. To assemble: Add the pastry cream into a bowl and whisk it well until fluffy. Add the whipped cream and fold gently with a silicone spatula until homogenized and you have a fluffy cream.
10. Spread the cream over the base using a spatula. Refrigerate for 1 hour until completely chilled.
11. Sprinkle with cinnamon and serve.
