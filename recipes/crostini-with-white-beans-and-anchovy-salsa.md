## Ingredients

- 1 baguette
- Olive oil for drizzling
- For the cannellini bean purée:
- 400 gram tin cannellini beans, rinsed and drained
- 1 small clove garlic
- Grated zest and juice of 1 lemon
- 50 millilitres olive oil
- For the anchovy salsa:
- Handful of fresh flat-leaf parsley
- 50 grams good quality anchovy fillets in olive oil, drained
- 2 tablespoons capers, drained
- 1/2 garlic clove, finely grated
- Grated zest and juice of 1 lemon
- 2-3 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 160°C. Cut the baguette diagonally into thin slices and arrange on a baking sheet. Drizzle with olive oil and bake for 10-12 minutes until crisp. Set aside to cool.
2. Put the beans, garlic, lemon zest and half the juice in a food processor. Pulse briefly while drizzling in the 50 millilitres olive oil to give a chunky purée. Add salt and pepper, along with more lemon juice if needed.
3. For the salsa: chop the parsley, anchovies and capers, transfer to a bowl and add the garlic, lemon zest and juice. Add the olive oil to cover. Stir well and season with salt, pepper and more lemon juice if needed.
4. Dollop the bean purée onto the crostini and drizzle generously with anchovy salsa to serve.
