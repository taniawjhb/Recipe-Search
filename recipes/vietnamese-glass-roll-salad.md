## Ingredients

- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1 1/2 tablespoons soy sauce
- 1 tablespoon rice wine vinegar
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tablespoon fresh grated ginger, minced
- 1/3 cup peanut butter, smooth
- 1 teaspoon sriracha, or hot sauce of choice
- 1 tablespoon honey
- 1/2 lime, juiced
- 1 or 2 tablespoons water to thin, or extra lime
- 450 grams chicken breast
- 1 tablespoon olive oil, to fry the chicken
- Salt and pepper
- 100 grams vermicelli noodles
- 1 1/2 cups purple cabbage, shredded
- 2 mini cucumbers, chopped
- 2 carrots, shredded
- 1 jalapeño, chopped
- 1 avocado, chopped
- 1/4 cup green onions, chopped
- 1/4 cup fresh cilantro, chopped
- 1/4 cup fresh mint, chopped
- 1/4 cup peanuts, roughly chopped for topping

## Method

1. Season the chicken with salt and pepper, then in a medium size frying pan, add the olive oil and pan fry the chicken on medium heat for 4-5 minutes per side, until cooked through. Set aside to cool while you prepare the salad and dressing.
2. Prepare the vermicelli noodles according to package instructions. Set aside until ready to use.
3. Prepare the peanut dressing by adding everything (olive oil, soy sauce, rice wine vinegar, garlic, ginger, peanut butter, sriracha, honey, lime juice and water) to a small bowl and whisk well, adding extra lime juice or water to thin out slightly, to achieve the perfect consistency.
4. Assemble the salad ingredients in a large serving bowl, starting with the vermicelli noodles followed by all other ingredients.
5. Now shred the chicken with two forks and then top the salad before drizzling with the creamy peanut dressing.
6. Toss together nicely to ensure it is well mixed and then enjoy right away. Top with the chopped peanuts.
