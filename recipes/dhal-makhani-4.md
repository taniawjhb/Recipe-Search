## Ingredients

- 1 cup lentils
- 1/4 cup dry kidney beans (optional)
- water to cover
- 5 cups water
- 2 tablespoons salt
- 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
- 1 tablespoon cumin seeds
- 4 cardamom pods
- 1 cinnamon stick, broken
- 4 bay leaves
- 6 whole cloves
- 1 1/2 tablespoons ginger paste
- 1 1/2 tablespoons garlic paste
- 1/2 teaspoon ground turmeric
- 1 pinch cayenne pepper, or more to taste
- 1 cup canned tomato puree, or more to taste
- 1 tablespoon chili powder
- 2 tablespoons ground coriander
- 1/4 cup butter
- 2 tablespoons dried fenugreek leaves (optional)
- 1/2 cup cream (optional)

## Method

1. Place the lentils and kidney beans in a large bowl; cover with plenty of water. Soak for at least 2 hours or overnight. Drain.
2. Cook the lentils, kidney beans, 5 cups water, and salt in a pot over medium heat until tender, stirring occasionally, about 1 hour. Remove from heat and set aside. Keep the lentils, kidney beans, and any excess cooking water in the pot.
3. Heat the vegetable oil in a saucepan over medium-high heat. Cook the cumin seeds in the hot oil until they begin to pop, 1 to 2 minutes. Add the cardamom pods, cinnamon stick, bay leaves, and cloves; cook until the bay leaves turn brown, about 1 minute.
4. Reduce heat to medium-low; add the ginger paste, garlic paste, turmeric, and cayenne pepper. Stir to coat.
5. Stir the tomato puree into the spice mixture; cook over medium heat until slightly reduced, about 5 minutes. Add the chili powder, coriander, and butter; cook and stir until the butter is melted.
6. Stir the lentils, kidney beans and any leftover cooking water into the tomato mixture; bring to a boil, reduce heat to low. Stir the fenugreek into the lentil mixture.
7. Cover the saucepan and simmer until heated through, stirring occasionally, about 45 minutes. Add the cream and cook until heated through, 2 to 4 minutes.
