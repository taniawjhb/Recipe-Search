## Ingredients

For the piri-piri marinade:

- 1/3 cup white vinegar
- 1/3 cup freshly squeezed lemon juice
- 1/3 cup olive oil
- 2 tbsp paprika
- 2 tbsp salt
- 2 tbsp chopped garlic
- 5-10 bird's eye chillies, minced

For the chicken:

- 1 spatchcock chicken
- 6 potatoes, sliced 1cm thick
- 1 lemon
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1 cup piri-piri marinade

## Method

1. Blend all marinade ingredients together until smooth and emulsified.
2. Wash and peel your potatoes, slicing them and lining the bottom of a roasting tray with them. Drizzle with olive oil, pepper and your garlic cloves halved.
3. Wash, trim and pat dry your bird, then spatchcock it (this is the removal of the backbone in order for the bird to lay flat on a roasting tray or grill).
4. Place your chicken on top of the potatoes, then use the piri-piri marinade to cover the chicken (be wary of the amount of chilli). Rub the piri-piri all over the bird, making sure to get under the skin. Add 4 thin lemon slices under the skin in various areas.
5. Drizzle with olive oil, and some extra salt then pop into the oven at 190C for 60 minutes, turning the bird at the halfway mark and bake till skin is crispy.
