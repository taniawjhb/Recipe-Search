## Ingredients

### For the Crying Tiger Sauce

- 2 tablespoons tamarind sauce
- 2 tablespoons fish sauce
- 1/2 tablespoon palm sugar
- 1 tablespoon chili flakes
- 1 tablespoon ground roasted rice
- 1/2 tablespoon chopped shallot
- 1/2 tablespoon chopped spring onion
- 1/2 tablespoon chopped coriander

### For the Sirloin Steak

- 300 grams sirloin steak
- 1/2 tablespoon oyster sauce
- 1 tablespoon seasoning sauce / Maggi sauce
- 1 tablespoon cooking oil

## Method

1. For the Crying Tiger Sauce: Combine tamarind sauce, fish sauce and palm sugar and mix well until the sugar dissolves. Add chili flakes, ground roasted rice and all the herbs. Taste and adjust if required.
2. For the steak: Use a fork to pierce the beef all over, then add the seasoning sauce and oyster sauce. Give it a good rub for around 5 minutes before adding the oil and rubbing well again.
3. Leave the beef to marinate for 15-20 minutes. Then cook by your preferred method, such as grilling on charcoal, BBQ or pan grill.
4. Rest the steak before slicing and serve with the dipping sauce.
