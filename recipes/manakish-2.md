## Ingredients

- 2 teaspoons active dry yeast
- 300 millilitres water (warm)
- 500 grams flour
- 2 teaspoons salt
- 2 teaspoons sugar
- 12 tablespoons olive oil
- 6 tablespoons za'atar
- 300 grams halloumi
- lemon (for serving)

## Method

1. Dissolve yeast in lukewarm water. Add flour, salt and sugar to a bowl. Make a well and add yeast mixture, followed by one third of the olive oil. Work into a dough using your hands and knead for 5 minutes until elastic. The dough should spring back when poked with your finger. Form dough into a ball, place in a large bowl, oil the top, and cover with plastic wrap. Set aside somewhere warm for 1 hour.
2. Once the dough has risen, roll out into a log and divide into 4 equal pieces. Place on a parchment-lined baking sheet and press out slightly into flatbreads. Cover with a clean kitchen towel and let rise again while you prepare the topping. Mix the za'atar with remaining olive oil. Grate halloumi on the largest side of a box grater, then add to the za'atar mixture and mix to combine.
3. Preheat oven to 200°C/400°F. Stretch out the flatbreads and dimple the surfaces with your fingertips. Top each generously with the za'atar-halloumi mixture and drizzle more olive oil, if desired.
4. Transfer to oven and bake for approximately 8 minutes. Serve immediately with lemon wedges.
