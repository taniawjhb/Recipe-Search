## Ingredients

- For the fish: 1.5kg firm white fish – cleaned, scaled and cut in steaks
- 1/2 teaspoon grated ginger
- 1/2 teaspoon white pepper
- 1 teaspoon minced garlic
- 1/2 teaspoon thyme
- 1/2 teaspoon minced garlic
- 1/2 teaspoon white pepper
- 1/2 teaspoon green seasoning (optional)
- 1/2 teaspoon chicken bouillon powder (optional)
- 1 lemon
- Salt to taste
- For the stew: 1/2 cup vegetable oil or more as needed
- 1 medium onion, thinly sliced
- 1 teaspoon minced garlic (about 2 garlic cloves)
- 1/2 teaspoon ginger
- 1 teaspoon thyme
- 1 bay leaf
- 1 Scotch bonnet pepper, pierced or replace with 1/2 teaspoon cayenne pepper
- 3-4 medium tomatoes, diced
- 1 bell pepper, sliced
- 2 green onions (scallion), chopped
- Bouillon seasoning (optional)
- White pepper or black as needed to taste

## Method

1. Rinse the fish; carefully drain, and pat dry with paper towels or a clean towel and rub with lemon or lime.
2. Place the fish in a large bowl or saucepan then season with salt, garlic, ginger, thyme, white pepper and green seasoning.
3. Mix the fish with a spoon or with hands until they are well coated, set aside in the fridge and marinate for 30 minutes or overnight.
4. When ready to cook remove the fish from the fridge, lightly shake off any marinade sticking out.
5. In a large skillet heat the oil over medium heat until hot, add the fish, cook each side for about 2-3 minutes on both sides. Remove the fish and set aside.
6. Drain the oil and leave about 3 or more tablespoons of oil, if using the same pan, or heat oil in a medium saucepan.
7. Then add the onion, scotch bonnet pepper, garlic, green seasoning, thyme, bay leaf, sauté until onions are tender, about 2 minutes or more. Stir frequently to avoid any burns.
8. Add the tomatoes, continue stirring and let it simmer for about 5 minutes or more with about 1/2 cup stock or water or more as needed.
9. Finally add the bell pepper, green onions followed by the fish one at a time and more stock, about 1/2 cup stock or water. Simmer for about 5 minutes or more. Adjust for seasonings and thickness of the stew with stock or oil.
10. Remove and serve with rice.
