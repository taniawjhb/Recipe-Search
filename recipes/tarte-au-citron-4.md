## Ingredients

- 220 grams plain/all-purpose flour
- 50 grams soft icing sugar, sifted
- 15 grams almond meal/ground almonds
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 100 grams (7 tablespoons) unsalted butter, softened, cut into 1 centimetre cubes
- 1 large egg, at room temperature
- 1 tablespoon lemon zest (1 lemon's worth)
- 125 millilitres lemon juice (from 1-2 lemons)
- 150 grams white sugar
- 170 grams unsalted butter, cut into 1 centimetre cubes
- 3 whole large eggs
- 3 egg yolks (from large eggs)

## Method

1. For the pâte sucrée, place all the dry ingredients in the bowl of a food processor. Add the cold butter and pulse until the butter is incorporated. Now add the whole egg and run the machine until the dough starts to come together.
2. Gather the dough and wrap in cling wrap, shape into a 2 centimetre thick disc, and place in the freezer for 10 minutes.
3. Remove the pastry from the freezer and, on a lightly floured surface, roll out until 3 millimetres thick. Transfer the pastry to your baking tin and gently ease it into the sides; do not stretch the pastry as this will cause shrinking. Dock the pastry thoroughly to avoid it rising. Place the pastry shell in the freezer for 10 minutes.
4. Remove from the freezer, add a sheet of baking paper and fill with baking beans. Place in the oven preheated to 200°C (390°F, 180°C fan). Bake for 15 minutes then remove the beans and baking paper and bake for a further 10 minutes until the base is lightly golden. Remove from the oven and allow to cool completely before filling.
5. For the filling, preheat the oven to 180°C. Put the lemon zest, lemon juice, white sugar, butter, whole eggs and egg yolks in a medium saucepan and whisk to combine.
6. Place the saucepan on the stove over low to medium-low heat. Whisk constantly, especially as the butter is melting, to ensure it doesn't split. Keep stirring until the mixture thickens enough to visibly mound on the surface when dolloped, about 5 minutes.
7. Strain into a bowl using a fine mesh strainer. Pour into the tart shell and smooth the filling surface using an offset spatula.
8. Bake for 5 minutes. It will still be a soft custard when you touch it but not liquid; it will set more when cooled so it's sliceable. Allow to cool completely before cutting.
