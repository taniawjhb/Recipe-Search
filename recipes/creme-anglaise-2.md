## Ingredients

- 1160 millilitres full cream milk
- 4 teaspoons pure vanilla extract with seeds
- 8 large free-range egg yolks
- 6 tablespoons caster sugar

## Method

1. Put the milk in a medium saucepan with the vanilla pod and seeds and slowly bring just up to the boil. Set aside to infuse for 10 minutes. Remove the pod.
2. In a mixing bowl, beat the egg yolks with the sugar using a wooden spoon until well combined. Pour in the infused milk, stirring constantly until well combined.
3. Return the custard to the cleaned pan and heat gently, stirring, for about 5 minutes until it thickens and lightly coats the back of a spoon. Don't let it boil or the yolks will scramble.
4. When the custard has reached the desired thickness, remove it from the heat and strain through a sieve into a medium bowl. Put a piece of cling film on the surface to prevent a skin forming and set aside or chill until ready to use.
