## Ingredients

- 1 cup boiling water
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1 cup cake flour
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- Oil for deep frying
- Cinnamon sugar for dusting

## Method

1. Place the flour, salt and baking powder in a bowl, stir well to combine.
2. Add the boiling water and olive oil, and stir quickly to combine; do not over mix, it should form a heavy batter.
3. Transfer to a piping bag and set aside to cool while you heat the oil.
4. Heat the oil to 160°C, then add 10 centimetre long strips of the batter using a pair of scissors to cut them. Cook until golden brown and cooked through, about 4 minutes. Drain on paper towel and, once cooled, dust with cinnamon sugar.
