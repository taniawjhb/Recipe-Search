## Ingredients

- 5 eggs, separated
- 1 cup brown sugar, or 3/4 cup white sugar
- Pinch sea salt
- 1/2 cup oil
- 1/3 cup coconut milk
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 2 teaspoons baking powder
- 1 cup white flour
- 3 teaspoons green tea powder, such as Japanese matcha
- 1 teaspoon or more icing sugar

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 175°C. Prepare an angel food cake pan (or a 23 centimetre cake pan), either by greasing it well or lining it with parchment paper.
2. Crack the eggs into 2 separate mixing bowls, with the egg whites in one bowl and the yolks in another. Beat the egg whites until stiff and set aside.
3. Add the salt and sugar to the yolks and stir well until smooth. Add the coconut milk, oil and vanilla to the yolks and beat until mixed in.
4. In a third bowl, stir together the flour, baking powder and green tea powder. Add (or sift) this into the yolk mixture. Stir well or beat to incorporate. The batter will turn green from the green tea, which is the effect you want.
5. Fold in the egg whites, stirring and mixing by hand just until the egg whites have dissolved into the batter (do not overmix).
6. Pour the batter into the prepared pan and bake for 25 to 35 minutes. Check the cake after 25 minutes by inserting a fork into the centre; if it comes out clean, it is done. (The centre should form a peak; if it is concave, the cake is not finished.)
7. Decorate the cake with a sifting of icing sugar. Add toasted coconut or some blueberries and mint for extra flair. Serve warm or at room temperature.
