## Ingredients

- 1 lime, cut into tiny pieces
- 1 piece ginger, cut into tiny cubes (rinse off in cold water once chopped)
- 1 red onion, cut into tiny cubes
- 1/2 cup roasted peanuts, chopped
- 1/2 cup coconut flakes
- 5 green chillies, chopped into tiny discs
- 1 lemongrass stalk, cut into small chunks
- 20 bok choy leaves (or tender spinach leaves)
- 6 tablespoons sweet plum sauce

## Method

1. To assemble the miang kham, prepare all the ingredients and place into individual bowls, arranged around the pile of bok choy leaves.
2. To eat, place a small amount of the different fillings into a bok choy leaf, drizzle with a little plum sauce, wrap up and enjoy.
