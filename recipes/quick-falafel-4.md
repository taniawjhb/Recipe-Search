## Ingredients

- 1 tin chickpeas (rinsed, drained and patted dry)
- 1/3 cup chopped fresh parsley
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 medium shallots, minced
- 2 tablespoons raw sesame seeds
- 1 1/2 teaspoons cumin (plus more to taste)
- 1/4 teaspoon each sea salt and black pepper (plus more to taste)
- 1 healthy pinch coriander (optional)
- 3-4 tablespoons all-purpose flour
- 3-4 tablespoons oil for cooking
- Breadcrumbs for coating

## Method

1. Add the chickpeas, parsley, shallot, garlic, sesame seeds, cumin, salt, pepper (and coriander if using) to a food processor or blender and pulse to combine, scraping down the sides as needed until thoroughly combined. You are looking for a crumbly dough, not a paste.
2. Add the flour 1 tablespoon at a time to combine until no longer wet and you can mould the dough into a ball without it sticking to your hands, about 4 tablespoons. Taste and adjust the seasonings as needed; you want the flavour to be bold.
3. Transfer to a mixing bowl, cover and refrigerate for 1-2 hours to firm up. If you are in a hurry you can chill in the freezer for 45 minutes or skip this step, but they will be more fragile when cooking.
4. Once chilled, scoop out rounded tablespoon amounts (about 30 grams) and gently form into 11-12 small discs.
5. Optional: Sprinkle on panko breadcrumbs and gently press to adhere, then flip and repeat. This produces a crispier falafel.
6. Heat a large skillet over medium heat and add enough oil to generously coat the pan, about 2 tablespoons. Swirl to coat. Once the oil is hot, add only as many falafel as will fit comfortably, about 5-7.
7. Cook for a total of 4-5 minutes, flipping when the underside is deep golden brown. Repeat until all the falafel are browned; the deeper the golden brown, the crispier they will be. They will firm up more once slightly cooled.
