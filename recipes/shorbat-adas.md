## Ingredients

- 1.5 cups red lentils
- 6 cups chicken/vegetable stock
- 1 cup carrots, chopped
- 1 onion, chopped
- 2 garlic cloves, chopped
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- Salt & pepper to taste
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1/2 tsp turmeric

## Method

1. In a pot, add the olive oil. To that, add the onions, garlic and carrots. Add salt and pepper and sauté for 5 minutes until fragrant.
2. Add the lentils and sauté for a few minutes. Season with the cumin and turmeric. Mix together.
3. Add the cooking liquid and bring the mixture to a boil. Once it reaches a boil, bring down to a simmer. Let this cook for about 25 minutes until the carrots are tender.
4. Once tender, use an immersion blender to puree the soup until creamy. If you do not have an immersion blender, pour the soup into a regular blender for a minute until smooth.
5. Enjoy with crispy pita chips and fresh lemon juice!
