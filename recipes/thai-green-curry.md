## Ingredients

- 1 cup sliced chicken breast
- 2 tablespoons green curry paste
- 4 lime leaves, thinly sliced
- 1 red chilli
- 3 baby marrow, sliced
- 10 green beans, cut into thirds
- 3 patty pan, quartered
- 1 red onion, quartered
- 1/2 cup basil
- 2 cans coconut milk
- 1 tablespoon fish sauce
- 1 teaspoon sugar
- Coconut/sunflower oil for frying

## Method

1. In a medium saucepan, on medium-high heat, start by adding your oil and half the curry paste till the aromas are released.
2. Brown your chicken in the curry paste, adding salt to taste.
3. Remove the chicken once browned and set aside.
4. In the same saucepan, add the coconut milk and bring to a simmer, making sure to stir and deglaze the bottom.
5. Once simmering, add the rest of your curry paste, vegetables, fish sauce, and sugar (keep your basil, chilli and lime leaves till the end).
6. When the vegetables are past halfway cooking (around 6 minutes), add your chicken back into the curry and simmer for another 6 minutes or until the chicken is cooked through.
7. Stir through sliced basil, whole chilli and finely shredded lime leaves and serve with rice.
