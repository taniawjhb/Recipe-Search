## Ingredients

- 5 eggs, separated
- 1 cup brown sugar, or 3/4 cup white sugar
- Pinch sea salt
- 1/2 cup oil
- 1/3 cup coconut milk
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 2 teaspoons baking powder
- 1 cup white flour
- 3 teaspoons green tea powder, such as Japanese "matcha"
- 1 teaspoon or more icing sugar

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 175°C. Prepare an angel food cake pan (or a 9-inch cake pan), either by greasing it well or lining it with parchment paper.
2. Crack the eggs into 2 separate mixing bowls, with the egg whites in one bowl and the yolks in another. Beat the egg whites until stiff, and set aside.
3. Add the salt and sugar to the yolks and stir well until smooth. Add the coconut milk, oil, and vanilla to the yolks, and beat until mixed in.
4. In a 3rd bowl, stir together the flour, baking powder, and green tea powder. Now add (or sift) this into the yolk mixture. Stir well or beat to incorporate. Note: the batter will turn green from the green tea — this is the effect you want.
5. Now fold in the egg whites, stirring and mixing by hand and just until the egg whites have dissolved into the batter (do not overmix).
6. Pour the batter into the prepared pan and place in the oven for 25 to 35 minutes (depending on your oven). Check the cake after 25 minutes by inserting a fork into the centre of the cake. If it comes out clean, it's done (the centre should form a peak — if it is concave, your cake isn't nearly finished).
7. Decorate the cake with a sifting of icing sugar. Add toasted coconut or some blueberries and mint for extra health benefits. Serve warm or at room temperature and enjoy.
