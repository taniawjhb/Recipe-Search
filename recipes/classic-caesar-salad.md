## Ingredients

### For The Croutons

- 1/2 French baguette, cut in half and thinly sliced, 0.5 centimetres thick
- 3 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
- 1 teaspoon minced garlic, 2 small cloves
- 2 tablespoons grated parmesan cheese

### Caesar Salad Dressing

- 6-8 anchovies
- 2 garlic cloves
- 2 pasteurized eggs
- juice of 1 large lemon
- 1 cup olive oil
- 1/2 cup grated parmesan cheese
- sea salt

### For the Caesar Salad

- 1 large romaine lettuce (or 2 small heads romaine)
- 1/3 cup parmesan cheese, shredded or shaved

## Method

### Croutons

1. Preheat oven to 190˚C. Cut the baguette in half lengthwise through the top of the baguette then slice diagonally into 0.5 centimetres thick pieces. Place the breads onto a baking sheet.
2. In a small bowl, combine 3 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil and 1 teaspoon of finely minced garlic. Drizzle the garlic oil over the croutons and sprinkle the top with 2 tablespoons grated parmesan cheese.
3. Toss until evenly coated. Spread in a single layer over the baking sheet and bake at 190˚C until light golden and crisp (10-12 minutes), or to desired crispness.

### Caesar Salad Dressing

1. In the bowl of a small food processor add the anchovies and garlic and pulse 20-30 times until the ingredients are finely chopped.
2. Next, add in the eggs and 1/2 of the lemon juice and process on high for 20 seconds until completely mixed together and smooth.
3. While processing at high speed, slowly drizzle in the oil until it becomes thick and creamy.
4. Finish by adding in the cheese, remaining 1/2 lemon juice, and salt, and process on low speed until combined. Refrigerate until ready to use.

### Assemble

1. Rinse, dry and chop or tear the romaine into bite-sized pieces. Place in a large serving bowl and sprinkle generously with shredded parmesan cheese and cooled croutons. Drizzle with caesar dressing and toss gently until lettuce is evenly coated.
