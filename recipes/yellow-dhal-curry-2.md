## Ingredients

- 4 tablespoons butter
- 1 teaspoon black mustard seed
- 4 teaspoons cumin seed
- 4 dried red chillies
- 10-15 curry leaves
- 2 bay leaves
- 2 cinnamon sticks
- 2 teaspoons crushed garlic
- 2 medium onions, chopped
- 2 medium tomatoes, chopped
- 1 teaspoon turmeric
- 2 teaspoons curry powder
- 2 tablespoons chopped coriander
- 800 grams cooked dhal

## Method

1. Heat the butter in the pan, now add the chopped onion and cook until softened.
2. Now add the mustard seed, cumin seeds, chilli, garlic, curry leaf, cinnamon and bay leaf and cook until fragrant.
3. Now add the turmeric powder and masala - cook for 1 minute.
4. Now add the finely chopped tomato and a splash of boiling water - cook for 2 minutes.
5. Stir in the dhal and add salt to taste.
6. Bring to boil and simmer for 5 minutes on a low heat.
7. Once ready add chopped coriander to garnish.
