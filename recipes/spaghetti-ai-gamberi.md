## Ingredients

- 800 grams fresh prawns
- 320 grams spaghetti
- 300 grams cherry tomatoes
- 50 grams extra virgin olive oil
- 50 grams white wine
- 50 grams water
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1 shallot
- Fine salt to taste
- Parsley to taste

## Method

1. To prepare spaghetti with prawns, start by cleaning the prawns, taking care to keep the heads aside and eliminate the internal black vein.
2. Wash, clean and cut the cherry tomatoes in half. Peel the garlic clove, clean the shallot and slice it quite finely.
3. Pour a tablespoon or two of olive oil into a large pan, add the prawn heads, one of the two cloves of garlic, the sliced shallot, a couple of cherry tomatoes and cook for a few minutes, until the prawns and garlic are fragrant and starting to colour.
4. Add the wine and water and continue cooking for another fifteen minutes, taking care to crush the prawn heads with a wooden spoon to flavour the stock.
5. In the meantime, take a non-stick pan, pour in the remaining oil, add the garlic clove, the leftover cherry tomatoes and sauté everything over a high heat for a few minutes.
6. While this is happening cook the spaghetti in plenty of lightly salted water, remembering to drain it when it is still al dente.
7. Add the cleaned prawns to the pan with the tomatoes, continuing cooking for another couple of minutes.
8. Take the pan with the prawn heads again, remove them, process the rest of the ingredients with a food mill and then pass the liquid obtained through a narrow mesh strainer.
9. Add the liquid just obtained into the pan with the cherry tomatoes and prawns, stir to combine and also add the spaghetti.
10. Stir to combine the spaghetti with the sauce and finish cooking the pasta. Plate, sprinkle with fresh chopped parsley and serve.
