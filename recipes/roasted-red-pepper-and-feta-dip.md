## Ingredients

- 4 red bell peppers
- 1/4 cup extra-virgin olive oil
- 2 tablespoons red wine vinegar
- 2 garlic cloves, peeled
- 2 cups crumbled feta cheese

## Method

1. Char the peppers directly over a gas flame or in the broiler until blackened on all sides. Enclose in a paper bag for 10 minutes. Peel, seed, and quarter the peppers.
2. Purée the peppers, oil, garlic, red wine vinegar and cheese in a processor. Season with salt and pepper.
3. Chill at least 3 hours or overnight before serving.
