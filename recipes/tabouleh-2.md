## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 cups couscous
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 15 cherry tomatoes
- 30 grams fresh parsley
- 30 grams coriander
- 20 grams mint
- 1 small cucumber
- 1/2 red pepper
- 1/2 red onion
- 2 tablespoons chives
- 1/2 cup flaked almonds

### For the dressing

- juice of 1 lemon
- 4 tablespoons olive oil
- 2 tablespoons red wine vinegar
- 1 clove garlic

## Method

1. Place the couscous in a bowl, add salt and stir. Boil water, and add just enough to cover the couscous. Wrap with cling wrap and set aside.
2. Prepare all the individual ingredients, cutting everything into a very small dice.
3. To assemble the salad, start by loosening the couscous with a fork. The couscous should be loose and fluffy.
4. Now add all the prepared ingredients to the couscous, adding everything before stirring to mix to avoid making the couscous mushy.
5. Prepare your dressing by combining the juice of 1 lemon, 4 tablespoons olive oil, 2 tablespoons wine vinegar, salt, pepper and the crushed garlic. Set aside until needed.
6. Add the dressing and gently stir to combine all the ingredients.
