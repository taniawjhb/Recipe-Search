## Ingredients

- 270 grams granulated sugar
- 4 eggs
- 2 egg yolks
- 400 grams milk
- 400 grams heavy cream
- 1 pinch salt
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- 450 grams phyllo dough sheet
- 150 grams butter
- 1 tablespoon dark brown soft sugar
- 2 tablespoons icing sugar
- 1 tablespoon cinnamon

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 200°C (390°F) set to fan.
2. Melt the butter in a small saucepan over medium heat, and using a pastry brush, brush a 30x35 centimetre baking pan generously with butter.
3. Brush a sheet of phyllo dough with butter and place it in the pan. Brush another sheet with butter and place it on top.
4. Do not brush the rest of the phyllo sheets with butter; instead drizzle some butter on to them, one at a time. Crumple each sheet, folding it like an accordion and transfer to the pan.
5. Be careful not to press down on them so they do not flatten.
6. Continue in the same way, placing each folded sheet of phyllo gently next to the other, until all of the sheets of phyllo dough are in the pan.
7. Drizzle the leftover butter over the top of the folded phyllo in the pan.
8. Bake for 20 minutes or until the phyllo turns golden brown and crunchy. To make sure it does become as crunchy as possible, place the baking pan on the highest rack in the oven so the air is able to reach both the top and bottom of the pan.
9. While the phyllo is baking, prepare the custard. Start by beating the 4 eggs, 2 egg yolks, and sugar in a bowl, using a hand whisk. Beat until the sugar has completely dissolved.
10. Add the vanilla, baking powder, milk, heavy cream and salt, similar to a crème anglaise.
11. When the sheets of phyllo dough are ready, remove from the oven. Turn the heat down to 180°C.
12. Pour the custard mixture over the phyllo. Allow the pastry to absorb the custard for a few minutes.
13. Sprinkle the soft brown sugar over the top. This will give the surface a sweet crunch.
14. Return to the oven and bake for 20-25 minutes more.
15. When ready, remove from the oven and set aside to cool for a bit. Sprinkle with icing sugar and cinnamon. Cut into pieces and serve.
