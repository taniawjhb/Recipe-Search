## Ingredients

- 500 grams lamb shoulder meat, cut into small cubes
- 2 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
- 2 shallots, quartered
- Sea salt and freshly ground black pepper
- 1 sprig rosemary
- 2 sprigs thyme
- 1 bay leaf
- 1 small white onion, chopped
- 1 cup dry red wine
- 2 cups tomato sauce
- 1 sprig fresh basil, chopped
- 500 grams pasta
- 1/2 cup grated aged Pecorino Sardo cheese (or other pecorino cheese)

## Method

1. Chop the meat into small cubes and set aside.
2. In a medium saucepan, heat the olive oil, shallots, thyme, and rosemary over medium heat.
3. Add the chopped meat to the saucepan and simmer for 2 minutes. Season with salt and pepper to taste. Add the bay leaf and onion. Saute for 5 minutes, until meat has browned.
4. Add wine, stirring frequently, and deglaze, scraping browned bits from bottom of the pan. Add tomato sauce and basil. Let simmer on low heat for 15 minutes. (Add lamb or vegetable stock if sauce becomes dry.)
5. Cook for another 15 minutes until the lamb cubes are tender.
6. Add to the pasta at the same time stirring in the grated cheese.
