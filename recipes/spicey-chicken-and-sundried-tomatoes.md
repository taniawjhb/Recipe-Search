## Ingredients

- 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil
- 5 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tablespoon fresh thyme leaves
- 1 teaspoon crushed red pepper flakes
- 1 teaspoon Sicilian oregano
- 3/4 cup chicken broth
- 1/2 cup heavy cream
- 1/2 cup chopped sun-dried tomatoes
- Kosher salt and black pepper
- Fresh basil, for serving
- Seasoned flour to dust chicken

## Method

1. Prepare seasoned flour. In a large skillet over medium-high heat, heat oil. Dust chicken lightly in flour. Sear until golden and skin is crispy, 3 minutes per side. Transfer chicken to a plate and pour off half the fat.
2. To skillet, add garlic, thyme, oregano and crushed red pepper flakes and let cook until fragrant, 1 minute. Add chicken broth, heavy cream, sun-dried tomatoes, and Parmesan and season with salt. Bring to a simmer, then return chicken to skillet and simmer covered, gently for 15 minutes, until chicken is fully cooked through.
3. Garnish with basil and serve.
