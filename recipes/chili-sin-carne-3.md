## Ingredients

- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1/2 dried chili pepper, minced
- 1 green pepper, diced
- 2 tablespoons canola oil
- 1 2/3 cups canned tomatoes (diced)
- 2 tablespoons tomato paste
- Ground pepper to taste (optional)
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 2 tablespoons brown sugar
- 1/2 cup vegetable broth, low-sodium
- 2 tablespoons water, if necessary
- 3 cups beans/lentils/legumes (canned)
- 1 pinch salt (optional)
- 1 tablespoon fresh cilantro, chopped (optional)
- 1 lime (optional)

## Method

1. Prepare the vegetables. Finely chop the onion, mince the garlic and chili pepper, and finely dice the bell pepper.
2. Heat the oil in a skillet or pan over medium heat. Cook the onion and garlic about 3 minutes until the onion is soft and translucent, with occasional stirring, paying attention not to let them burn.
3. Add the diced tomatoes, tomato paste, diced bell pepper, minced chili pepper, cumin, and brown sugar. Pour in the warm broth. Cover and cook 15 minutes, checking occasionally that the mixture stays moist. Add water if necessary.
4. Drain the legumes, rinse them and drain again. Add them to the pan, mix well, and cook an additional 20 minutes. Add salt and pepper to taste.
5. Sprinkle with freshly chopped cilantro leaves, garnish with lime wedges, then serve.
