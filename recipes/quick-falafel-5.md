## Ingredients

- 1 tin chickpeas (rinsed, drained and patted dry)
- 1/3 cup chopped fresh parsley
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 medium shallot, minced
- 2 tablespoons raw sesame seeds
- 1 1/2 teaspoons cumin (plus more to taste)
- 1/4 teaspoon each sea salt and black pepper (plus more to taste)
- 1 healthy pinch coriander (optional)
- 3-4 tablespoons all-purpose flour
- 3-4 tablespoons oil for cooking
- Breadcrumbs for coating

## Method

1. Prepare the mixture: In a food processor, pulse the chickpeas, parsley, shallot, garlic, sesame seeds, cumin, salt, pepper (plus coriander, if using) until crumbly, not pasty. Scrape down the sides as needed.
2. Bind the dough: Add the flour, 1 tablespoon at a time, until the mixture holds together without sticking, about 4 tablespoons total. Adjust the seasoning to taste.
3. Chill: Cover and refrigerate for 1-2 hours (or freeze for 45 minutes for a quicker option).
4. Shape: Once firm, scoop 30 gram portions and gently form into 11-12 small discs.
5. Optional: Press panko breadcrumbs onto each falafel for extra crispiness.
6. Cook: Heat 2 tablespoons olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add 5-7 falafel at a time and cook 4-5 minutes, flipping once deep golden brown.
7. Serve: Allow to firm up slightly before serving. The deeper the golden brown, the crispier they will be.
