## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup pitted black olives, such as Niçoise or oil-cured olives
- 1 tablespoon drained capers
- 2 drained oil-packed anchovy fillets
- 3 medium cloves garlic
- 5 basil leaves (optional)
- 1 tablespoon loosely packed fresh oregano, marjoram, or thyme leaves
- 1 teaspoon Dijon mustard
- 1 teaspoon fresh juice from 1 lemon
- Extra-virgin olive oil, as needed

## Method

1. Combine the olives, capers, anchovies, garlic, basil (if using), other herbs, and mustard in the work bowl of a food processor and process, scraping down the sides, until a finely chopped paste forms.
2. Process in the lemon juice. With the processor running, drizzle in just enough olive oil to loosen to a spreadable paste, about 2 tablespoons. Season with salt and pepper only if needed.
3. Alternatively, roughly chop the olives, capers, anchovies, and garlic, then add to a mortar with basil (if using) and other herbs. Tap, crush, and smash with a pestle until the ingredients have been reduced to a thick paste (a little chunkiness is okay).
4. Using the pestle, work in the mustard and lemon juice, then drizzle in just enough olive oil to form a spreadable paste, about 2 tablespoons. Season with salt and pepper only if needed.

## Notes

Intense and loaded with flavour!
