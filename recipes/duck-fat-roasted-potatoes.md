## Ingredients

- 1 kilogram potatoes, peeled and cut into pieces
- 170 grams duck fat
- 1/2 tablespoon salt
- 2 tablespoons polenta

## Method

1. Preheat your oven to 240°C.
2. Place the duck fat in a roasting tray and place in the oven to start heating up. The secret to these potatoes is to get the duck fat ferociously hot.
3. In a large pot, cover the potatoes with cold water, season well and bring to the boil. Parboil the potatoes for no more than 10 minutes. Drain the potatoes and return them to the pot.
4. Place the polenta/semolina in the pot, cover tightly and give the potatoes a ferocious shake to coat them evenly and smash up their edges a bit (these are the parts that really crisp up nicely).
5. Carefully remove the tray from the oven and using a pair of tongs, place the potatoes gently into the sizzling duck fat.
6. Return the tray to the oven and roast the potatoes for 1 hour, turning them throughout the cooking time so that they brown on all sides.
7. Remove from the trays, drain on some kitchen towel and serve immediately. Golden, crusty, crunchy perfection.
