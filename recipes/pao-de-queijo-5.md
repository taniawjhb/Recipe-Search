## Ingredients

- 1 cup grated Parmesan cheese
- 1 (10 ounce) round of Queso Fresco, crumbled
- 1 cup full-fat milk
- 1/4 cup vegetable oil, plus 1 tablespoon for oiling your hands
- 2 eggs
- 1 pound tapioca flour (also known as tapioca starch)
- 2 teaspoons kosher salt

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 175°C. Add the tapioca flour and the salt to a large bowl, whisk to combine and set aside.
2. In a medium bowl, combine the cheeses and the eggs and set aside.
3. Add the milk and the oil to a small saucepan and bring to a simmer on the stovetop over medium heat. When the liquid simmers, remove the pan from the heat, and immediately pour the hot liquid into the tapioca mixture. Using a wooden spoon, stir to combine; the mixture will not be homogeneous, it'll be gummy and clumpy.
4. Wait 5-10 minutes until the tapioca mixture is cool to the touch, and only then add the cheese and egg mixture to the tapioca mixture. Mix with a wooden spoon or your hands to combine. Now you should have a homogeneous mixture and it should come together as a ball. You'll still see some white tapioca specks in the dough; that's normal.
5. Oil your hands, then scoop 1 to 2 tablespoon size portions, roll them into balls, and arrange them on a baking sheet approximately 2 inches apart from one another.
6. Bake until golden brown, approximately 30-35 minutes.
