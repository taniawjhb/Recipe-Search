## Ingredients

- 450g jumbo prawns, peeled and deveined
- 2 tbsp tequila
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1/2 tsp brown sugar
- 1/4 tsp chili powder
- 2 large garlic cloves, minced
- 1 lime, juice and zest
- 1 jalapeño, seeds removed and diced
- 1/2 cup fresh cilantro, roughly chopped

## Method

1. Season prawns with salt, pepper, paprika, cumin, chili powder, and brown sugar.
2. Heat olive oil in a large heavy bottomed pan over medium-high heat. Sauté garlic for 30 seconds, until fragrant. Arrange the shrimp in a single layer, cook for 3-4 minutes, and flip.
3. Immediately after flipping the shrimp, add the lime juice, tequila, and jalapeño to the skillet. Cook for another 2 minutes or until shrimp are opaque and cooked through.
4. Remove from heat and top with lime zest and cilantro.
