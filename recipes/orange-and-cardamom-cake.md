## Ingredients

- 1 cup extra-virgin olive oil, fruity or more neutral-tasting, plus extra for the pan
- 240 grams all-purpose flour, plus more for the pan
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- 1/4 teaspoon baking soda
- 3/4 to 1 teaspoon ground cardamom
- 250 grams sugar, plus 2 tablespoons granulated sugar
- 3 large eggs
- Grated zest of 2 oranges, plus 2 tablespoons fresh orange juice
- 1 1/4 cups whole milk
- 2 tablespoons sifted confectioners' sugar, for garnish

## Method

1. Position a rack in the centre of the oven and preheat the oven to 175°C.
2. Brush the bottom and sides of a 21 centimetre round cake pan with a little olive oil. Line the bottom with a round of parchment paper and dust with a bit of the flour, shaking out the excess.
3. In a medium bowl, whisk together the flour, salt, baking powder and baking soda.
4. In a large bowl, combine the sugar, cardamom and eggs. Using an electric hand mixer set on high, beat until thick and fluffy, about 5 minutes. While the mixer is running, slowly drizzle in the olive oil and beat until incorporated. Reduce the speed to low and add half the orange zest, the orange juice and milk. Beat until smooth.
5. Add the flour mixture to the wet ingredients and mix on low just until you have a uniform batter.
6. Pour the batter into the pan and sprinkle the top with the remaining 2 tablespoons granulated sugar. Bake for 45 to 50 minutes, or until the centre is set and a skewer inserted into the middle comes out clean.
7. Allow the cake to cool for 30 minutes in the pan, then run a small knife around the edge, invert the cake onto a large plate, then invert again onto a rack to cool completely.
8. Before serving, dust the cake with powdered sugar and sprinkle with the remaining orange zest.
