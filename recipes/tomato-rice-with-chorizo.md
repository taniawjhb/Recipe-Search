## Ingredients

- 2 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
- 1 tbsp butter
- 1 chorizo, split in 2, and diced
- 1 cup diced onion
- 2 teaspoons minced garlic
- 2 cups fresh tomatoes, cored, deseeded and cut into large chunks (save any juice from cutting)
- 1.5 cups stock
- 1 cup long-grain white rice
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
- 2 level tsp paprika
- 1 bay leaf

## Method

1. In a medium pot, melt butter in the oil over medium high heat, add half the chorizo diced, onions, bay leaf and garlic and cook for 5 minutes.
2. Add tomatoes with their juice, cover and simmer for 10-15 minutes.
3. Add stock, bring to a boil and add rice, salt and pepper.
4. Once boiling, lower to a gentle simmer and cook 20 minutes covered.
5. Pan fry remaining chorizo with half the paprika till just crispy.
6. Remove rice from heat and fluff with fork, adding crispy chorizo, fresh chopped parsley and a sprinkling of paprika.

## Notes

Traditionally served as a side dish without the chorizo.
