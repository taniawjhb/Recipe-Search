## Ingredients

- 1/4 cup each pine nuts, almonds, and walnuts (total 3/4 cup)
- 1/2 cup breadcrumbs
- 1 small yellow onion, roughly chopped
- 3 cloves garlic, roughly chopped
- 1 small red bell pepper, roughly chopped
- 1 small jalapeño pepper, seeds and ribs removed, roughly chopped
- 1/2 cup cilantro leaves, gently packed (okay to substitute parsley)
- 900 gms ground lamb
- 3/4 teaspoon ground cumin
- Heaping 1/4 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- Heaping 1/4 teaspoon ground cardamom
- Heaping 1/4 teaspoon ground cloves
- 1 1/2 teaspoons salt
- 1/4 teaspoon white pepper
- Tzatziki and hummus, for serving

## Method

1. Toast the nuts: Pulse the nuts in a food processor until finely chopped, then transfer to a dry skillet. Toast over medium heat, stirring, until fragrant (5-6 minutes). Cool in a mixing bowl.
2. Prepare the vegetables: Pulse the onion, garlic, bell pepper, jalapeño and cilantro until finely minced. Wring out excess liquid using a clean dishcloth, then add to the bowl with the nuts.
3. Mix the kofta: Combine the lamb, cumin, cinnamon, cardamom, cloves, salt and white pepper with the nut mixture. Mash with your hands until evenly blended.
4. Shape & chill: Form into patties, place on a baking sheet, cover and refrigerate until ready to cook.
5. Grill: Preheat to medium-high, oil the grates, and cook 4 minutes per side, covered, until browned and cooked through.
6. Serve: Enjoy with tzatziki and hummus.
