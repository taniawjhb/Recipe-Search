## Ingredients

- 500 grams firm white fish fillet, no skin, cut into 2.5 centimetre cubes
- 1 tablespoon lime juice
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- Black pepper
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1 1/2 tablespoons olive oil or coconut oil
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 small onion, finely diced
- 1 large red bell pepper, halved and sliced
- 1 1/2 teaspoons sugar
- 1 tablespoon cumin powder
- 1 tablespoon paprika
- 1 teaspoon cayenne pepper (optional)
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 400 millilitres coconut milk (full fat is best)
- 400 millilitres canned crushed tomatoes
- 1 cup fish stock (or chicken or vegetable)
- 1-2 tablespoons lime juice, plus more for serving
- 3 tablespoons roughly chopped fresh coriander, plus more for serving

## Method

1. Combine the fish, lime juice, 1 tablespoon oil, 1/4 teaspoon salt and black pepper in a bowl. Cover with cling wrap and refrigerate for 20 minutes.
2. Heat the 1 tablespoon olive oil in a large skillet over high heat. Add the fish and cook until just cooked through and light golden brown. Remove from the skillet and set aside.
3. Reduce the stove to medium-high and heat 1 1/2 tablespoons olive oil in the same skillet. Add the garlic and onion and cook for 1 1/2 minutes, or until the onion is starting to become translucent.
4. Add the bell peppers and cook for 2 minutes. Add the sugar, cumin, paprika, cayenne, 1/2 teaspoon salt, coconut milk, crushed tomatoes and fish stock. Bring to a simmer, then turn down to medium. Cook for 15 to 20 minutes or until it thickens. Adjust the salt and pepper to taste.
5. Return the fish to the broth to reheat, about 2 minutes. Stir through the lime juice.
6. Garnish with coriander and serve with rice or lime rice.
