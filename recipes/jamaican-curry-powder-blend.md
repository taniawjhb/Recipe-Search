## Ingredients

- 2 teaspoons coriander
- 2 teaspoons fenugreek seeds
- 2 teaspoons turmeric
- 2 teaspoons cumin
- 2 teaspoons black pepper
- 1 teaspoon cinnamon
- 1 teaspoon bay leaf, dried
- 1 teaspoon fennel seed
- 1 teaspoon nutmeg
- 1/2 teaspoon clove
- 1/2 teaspoon ginger
- 1/2 teaspoon cayenne
- 1/2 teaspoon allspice
- 1/2 teaspoon cardamom

## Method

1. Grind all whole spices and seeds and bay leaves to a powder.
2. Combine all the ingredients and store in an airtight jar.

## Notes

While you can use pre-ground spices, the flavor is really better if you grind whole cloves, nutmeg, etc.
