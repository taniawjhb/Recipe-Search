## Ingredients

- 1 cup ricotta
- 2 tablespoons grated parmesan
- 2 teaspoons fresh chopped chives
- 1 tablespoon fresh chopped flat-leaf parsley (Italian parsley)
- 2 teaspoons fresh chopped basil
- 1 teaspoon fresh chopped thyme
- 200 grams butter
- 12 fresh sage leaves

## Method

1. Mix the ingredients for your filling, and season with salt and pepper. Taste to check saltiness.
2. Take a pasta sheet, and gently fold along its midline to make a crease, then re-open it.
3. Keeping your spacing at about 3 centimetres, place 1 tablespoon of your filling at a time along the bottom half of your sheet.
4. Lightly moisten the outside edges of your sheet using a little water on a pastry brush.
5. Fold it over along the earlier crease. Press from the folded point outwards to remove any excess air.
6. Gently pat the dough down around each filling to create a seal.
7. Finally use a pasta cutter to cut your ravioli into squares.
8. Panzarotti: Take your pasta sheet, and using the floured rim of a water glass, make discs along your sheet. Remove the excess pasta. Place a ball of filling in the centre of the disc.
9. Brush the outside edges with very little water and then gently fold in half, trying to remove as much air as possible when doing so.
10. Use a fork to crimp the edges and form a seal. Plunge into rapidly boiling, salted water for 3-4 minutes.

## Notes

Serve with browned butter and the fresh sage leaves.
