## Ingredients

- 1.8kg eggplants – about 5, sliced lengthways 8mm thick, skin on
- 1/4 cup olive oil (for brushing)
- 1 2/3 cups parmesan, finely grated (for layers)
- 2 cups basil leaves (loosely packed), stalks reserved (for layers)
- 1 1/2 cups mozzarella, shredded (for topping)
- 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil (sugo)
- 1 onion, finely diced (sugo)
- 5 garlic cloves, finely minced (sugo)
- 600ml tomato passata (sugo)
- 400g canned tomatoes, crushed or finely chopped (sugo)
- 1 1/2 tsp white sugar (sugo)
- 1 1/2 tbsp fresh oregano, finely chopped (or 1/2 tsp dried oregano, or 1 sprig fresh basil) (sugo)
- 1 cup water (sugo)
- 3/4 tsp salt (sugo)
- 1/4 tsp black pepper (sugo)

## Method

1. Preheat oven to 220°C / 430°F (200°C fan). Line three large trays with baking / parchment paper. Brush eggplant slices on each side with oil.
2. Arrange eggplant in a single layer on the trays and bake for about 35–45 mins or until browning and tender, turning the eggplant halfway. Remove from oven, leave on trays to cool.
3. Tomato sauce (sugo): Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Sauté onions and garlic for 3 minutes until softened and just about to colour on the edges.
4. Pour in the passata, canned tomatoes, water, oregano and sugar. Stir and bring to a simmer.
5. Simmer uncovered for about 30–40 minutes, stirring occasionally, until thickened, adding the basil stalks halfway through cooking (so they don't lose their flavour).
6. The final consistency should be slightly thicker than a pasta sauce so it's spreadable and not leaching liquid, but not too pasty either. Add extra water a little at a time if required during cooking if it gets too thick. Remove the basil stalks and discard.
7. Stir in salt and pepper. (Remember, we get salt from the parmesan too, so no need to go overboard here.)
8. Assemble: Reduce oven to 180°C / 350°F (160°C fan). Smear a little tomato sauce in the bottom of a baking dish, 23 x 33cm / 9 x 13" (or thereabouts).
9. Layer 1: Lay one third of the eggplant so it covers the base (some overlap is OK). Spread 1/3 of the remaining sugo over eggplant. Drizzle with a little olive oil. Sprinkle with 1/3 of the parmesan, and 1/3 of basil leaves.
10. Layer 2: Repeat another 1/3 each of eggplant, tomato sauce, olive oil, basil and parmesan.
11. Layer 3 (top layer): Repeat again with remaining eggplant, tomato sauce and some olive oil. Do not put basil on top layer (garnish after baking). Scatter with remaining parmesan, then all the mozzarella.
12. Bake for 25 mins until bubbling and golden.
13. Let it rest for 5 to 10 minutes (easier to slice neatly). Scatter over remaining fresh basil leaves. Cut like lasagna and serve. (Optional: sprinkle with extra parmesan.)
