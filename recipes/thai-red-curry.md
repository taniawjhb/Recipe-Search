## Ingredients

- 4 chicken breasts, sliced
- 2 tablespoons fresh Red Curry Paste
- 1 red chilli
- 3 baby marrow, sliced
- 3 patty pan, quartered
- 3 baby squash, halved
- 1 red onion, quartered
- 1/2 cup basil
- 400 millilitres coconut milk
- 200 millilitres coconut cream
- 1 tablespoon fish sauce
- 1 teaspoon sugar
- Coconut/sunflower oil for frying

## Method

1. In a medium saucepan, on medium-high heat, start by adding your oil and half the curry paste, cook until the aromas begin to release.
2. Brown your chicken in the curry paste, adding salt to taste.
3. Remove the chicken once browned and set aside.
4. In the same saucepan, add coconut milk and coconut cream and bring to a simmer, making sure to stir and deglaze the bottom.
5. Once simmering, add the rest of your curry paste, vegetables, fish sauce, and sugar (keep your basil and chilli till the end).
6. When the vegetables are past halfway cooked (around 6 minutes), add your chicken back into the curry and simmer for another 6 minutes or until the chicken is cooked through.
7. Stir through a chiffonade of basil and garnish with the red chilli and serve with rice.
