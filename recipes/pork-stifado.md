## Ingredients

- 1.2kg pork shoulder, boneless, cut into bite size chunks
- Salt
- Pepper
- 4 tablespoons olive oil
- 2 tablespoons vinegar, white wine
- 2 tablespoons all-purpose flour
- 3 carrots
- 1 leek
- 2 cloves of garlic
- 700g shallots, finely chopped
- 100 g white wine
- 1 tablespoon tomato paste
- 400 g canned tomatoes
- 2 bay leaves
- 1 stick cinnamon
- 2 sprigs oregano
- 2 sprigs thyme
- 200 g water (optional)

## Method

1. Put the pork pieces in a bowl and add the salt, pepper, 2 tablespoons of olive oil, vinegar, and flour, and mix everything together with your hands. Place a pressure cooker over high heat. Add the meat - in 2 batches, if needed - and sauté it for 2-3 minutes, until nicely browned.
2. Remove the meat from the pressure cooker and transfer it to a bowl. Cut the carrots and the leek into thick rounds, and add them to the pressure cooker.
3. Coarsely chop the garlic and add it to the pressure cooker. Add 2 tablespoons of olive oil, the shallots, salt, and pepper, and mix well with a serving spoon. Sauté the vegetables for 4-5 minutes until they are tender and nicely golden.
4. Transfer the pork to the pressure cooker and deglaze it with the wine. Add the tomato paste, canned tomatoes, bay leaves, cinnamon, oregano, thyme, and water, and stir well with a wooden spoon.
5. Seal the pressure cooker with the lid, turn the safety valve to the proper pressure indicator, lower the heat, and simmer the stew for 35-40 minutes.
6. Remove the pressure cooker from the heat, depressurize it, open the lid, and serve.
