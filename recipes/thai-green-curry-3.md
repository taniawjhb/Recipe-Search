## Ingredients

- 1 quantity homemade green curry paste
- 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
- 1 cup (250 millilitres) chicken stock
- 400 grams coconut milk, full fat
- 3 teaspoons fish sauce
- 3 teaspoons white sugar
- 1/8 teaspoon salt
- 6 makrut lime leaves, torn in half
- 350 grams chicken thigh, skinless and boneless, sliced
- 1 cup zucchini discs
- 1 1/2 cups snow peas, small, trimmed
- 16 Thai basil leaves
- Juice of 1/2 lime, to taste

### Garnishes

- Thai basil or coriander
- Green or red chilli slices, optional
- Steamed jasmine rice

## Method

1. Heat the oil in a heavy-based skillet or pot over medium-high heat.
2. Add the curry paste and cook for 2 to 3 minutes until it mostly dries out.
3. Add the chicken stock and coconut milk and mix to dissolve the paste.
4. Add the fish sauce, sugar and salt.
5. Add the makrut lime leaves. Mix, then bring to a simmer.
6. Add the chicken, stir, then lower the heat to medium so it is bubbling gently. Cook 7 minutes.
7. Add the zucchini and cook 5 minutes until soft.
8. Taste the sauce. Add fish sauce or salt for more saltiness, sugar for sweetness.
9. Add the snow peas and cook 2 minutes until a bit softened, then stir through the basil and lime juice. The sauce should have reduced but will still be on the thin side, which is how it should be. Do not keep simmering or the sauce will darken.
10. Serve the curry over jasmine rice with garnishes of choice.
