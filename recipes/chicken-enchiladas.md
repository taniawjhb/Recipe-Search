## Ingredients

Enchilada Seasoning:

- 1 teaspoon EACH onion powder, garlic powder, salt
- 1 tablespoon EACH dried cumin powder, paprika, dried oregano
- 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
- 3/4 teaspoon cayenne pepper

Enchilada Sauce:

- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 3 tablespoons flour, plain/all purpose
- 2 cups chicken stock
- 1.5 cups tomato passata

Chicken Filling:

- 600 grams chicken breast, sliced in half horizontally (or boneless thigh)
- 2 tablespoons olive oil, separated
- 1/2 onion, chopped
- 2 garlic cloves, finely minced
- 1 red capsicum/bell pepper, diced
- 400 grams refried beans
- 1 3/4 cup frozen corn
- 1/4 cup (65 millilitres) water
- 1 cup (100 grams) grated cheese, cheddar

Topping:

- 1.5 cups (150 grams) cheese, shredded
- 2 tablespoons coriander/cilantro, roughly chopped

## Method

1. Mix Enchilada Seasoning spices. To be used for Filling and Sauce.
2. Enchilada Sauce: Heat oil in saucepan over medium heat. Add flour and stir 20 seconds. Add 2 tablespoons Enchilada Seasoning, stock and tomato. Stir to combine. Increase heat slightly to medium high. Cook for 4 minutes, whisking regularly, until the sauce thickens to the consistency of thick syrup. Remove from stove.
3. Chicken Filling: Drizzle 1 tablespoon oil over chicken, mix to coat. Sprinkle with Seasoning, toss to coat.
4. Heat remaining 1 tablespoon oil in a large skillet over high heat. Add chicken, cook 2 minutes. Turn and cook 1.5 minutes. Remove, rest 2 minutes then chop.
5. In the same skillet, add onion and garlic, cook 1 minute. Add capsicum, cook 2 minutes until onion is translucent.
6. Add refried beans, diced chicken, corn and water. Stir and cook for 2 minutes until it reduces slightly. Should be thick and juicy, not watery.
7. Assemble: Preheat oven to 180°C. Lay tortilla on work surface. Spread 2/3 cup Filling on lower third, sprinkle with cheese then roll up, finishing with the seam side down (to hold it closed). Repeat to make 8.
8. Smear a scoop of Enchilada Sauce across base of a 22 x 33 centimetres / 9 x 13" pan. Place Enchiladas in, pour over remaining Sauce, sprinkle with cheese.
9. Bake 20-25 minutes or until cheese is melted and golden. Serve immediately, sprinkled with coriander.
