## Ingredients

- 1 medium head of cauliflower, cut into florets (about 6 to 7 cups)
- 5 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil, divided
- 1/2 teaspoon salt, divided
- 4 tablespoons harissa paste
- 1 tin chickpeas, rinsed and drained
- 1 medium shallot, sliced into thick rounds
- 1/4 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
- 1/3 cup fresh torn herbs (a mix of mint and dill)

### Lemon-Tahini Dressing

- 2 tablespoons tahini
- 2 tablespoons fresh lemon juice
- 1 garlic clove, minced
- 1/4 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 220°C. In a large bowl add the harissa and 2-3 tablespoons olive oil and whisk to loosen the harissa paste. If needed, add a little more olive oil. Add the cauliflower and toss to coat, add some salt. Place on a large lined baking sheet and bake for 20 minutes.
2. Remove the cauliflower from the oven, toss, and brush with the remaining 2 tablespoons harissa paste. Push the cauliflower to one half of the baking sheet, and add the chickpeas and shallots to the remaining half.
3. Toss the chickpeas and shallots with the remaining 1 tablespoon olive oil, 1/4 teaspoon salt and black pepper. Place the baking sheet back in the oven for another 15 to 20 minutes, until crisp.
4. Meanwhile, prepare the lemon-tahini dressing by combining all the ingredients in a small bowl and stirring to combine. Gradually stream in 1 to 2 tablespoons of warm water, whisking constantly, until the dressing is smooth and creamy.
5. Assemble the salad by transferring the roasted cauliflower, chickpeas and shallots to a large bowl or serving platter. Toss with the fresh herbs and drizzle with the lemon-tahini dressing.
