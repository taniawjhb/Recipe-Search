## Ingredients

- 2 cucumbers
- 100 millilitres vinegar
- 1 red chilli
- 2 shallots
- 1 tablespoon sugar

## Method

1. Mix the vinegar with a tablespoon of sugar in a medium bowl, stirring until the sugar has mostly dissolved.
2. Wash the cucumbers and cut them into thin slices. You can do this with a mandoline or with a chef's knife.
3. Peel the shallots and cut them into thin rings. Remove the seeds from the red chilli, if desired, and cut into pieces.
4. Mix the cucumber, red chilli and shallot well with the vinegar, and season with salt.
5. This cucumber salad is best when the flavour has time to develop. Delicious as a side dish or as part of an Indonesian meal.
