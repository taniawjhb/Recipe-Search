## Ingredients

- 2 large eggplant, thinly sliced
- olive oil cooking spray
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1 medium brown onion, finely chopped
- 2 garlic cloves, crushed
- 800 grams lamb mince
- 420 gram can crushed tomatoes
- 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1/2 teaspoon ground allspice
- 1/3 cup grated pizza cheese
- lemon wedges, to serve

For the white sauce:

- 75 grams butter
- 1/3 cup plain flour
- 2 cups milk

## Method

1. Spray or brush the eggplant with oil. Heat a large frying pan over medium-high heat. Cook the eggplant, in batches, for 2 to 3 minutes each side or until browned. Transfer to a large plate.
2. Heat the oil in a large saucepan over medium-high heat. Add the onion and garlic. Cook, stirring, for 5 minutes or until the onion has softened. Add the mince. Cook, stirring with a wooden spoon to break up the mince, for 6 to 8 minutes or until browned. Add the tomatoes, cinnamon and allspice. Bring to the boil. Reduce heat to medium-low. Simmer for 30 minutes or until the sauce is thick and the liquid has evaporated.
3. Meanwhile, make the sauce. Melt the butter in a saucepan over medium-high heat. Add the flour. Cook, stirring, for 1 minute or until bubbling. Gradually stir in the milk. Bring to the boil. Reduce heat to medium. Cook, stirring, for 5 minutes or until the mixture has thickened. Remove from heat.
4. Preheat the oven to 180°C/160°C fan-forced. Grease an 8 cup-capacity ovenproof dish. Place one-third of the eggplant, slightly overlapping, over the base of the prepared dish. Spread half the meat sauce over the eggplant. Repeat the layers, ending with eggplant. Spread the white sauce over the eggplant. Sprinkle with cheese. Bake for 45 minutes or until golden. Stand 15 minutes. Serve with lemon wedges.
